# MON PAYS NATAL — S1 — IBIBAZO 50 (INCAMAKE Y'UBUSHAKASHATSI)

Iyi ni inyandiko y'ubushakashatsi (production reference), atari narration. Igizwe n'ibibazo 50 BBG yashizeho nk'umwitwarariko wa documentaire ya Ntare Rushatsi, buri kimwe gihuzwa n'aho gisubijwe (canke gitarasubizwa) muri EP01–EP10.

Ikimenyetso: ✅ Cyishuwe | ⚠️ Cyishuwe igice | ❌ Kirekire, kigikeneye ubushakashatsi

---

## A. Kumenya Ntare Rushatsi (1–7)

1. Ntare Rushatsi yari nde? — ✅ EP09 ("Ntare batatu": w'imigenzo, w'amateka, w'imigani)
2. Kubera iki afise ikibanza gihambaye mu mateka y'Uburundi? — ⚠️ Bivugwa mu buryo bwaraye mu ma episode yose, ariko nta episode yihariye ku giciro c'iki kibazo
3. Ni kuki yitwa "Ntare Rushatsi"? — ✅ INSIGURO Y'AMAZINA
4. Izina "Rushatsi" risobanura iki? — ✅ INSIGURO Y'AMAZINA ("intare y'ubwoya" / *hairy lion*)
5. Ni ryari yabayeho? — ✅ EP09 (imvyerekano zine zitandukanye)
6. Ibimenyetso vyerekana igihe yavukiye? — ⚠️ EP09 ivuga ko atazwi, ariko nta gace kihariye kabivuzeko
7. Ibimenyetso vyerekana igihe yapfiriye? — ✅ EP09 ("Aho yapfiriye: ntihamenyekana neza")

## D. Kuba umwami (25–31)

25. Ni gute Ntare Rushatsi yabaye umwami? — ⚠️ EP08/EP09 (Karyenda, Umuganuro), ariko uburyo nyakuri bwo kwimikwa ntiburavugwa
26. Ni nde yamushize ku ngoma? — ❌ Ntiraravugwa
27. Ni kuki abantu b'i Nkoma bamwemeye nk'umwami? — ❌ Ntiraravugwa
28. Ubwami bw'Uburundi bwari bumeze gute imbere ya Ntare? — ✅ EP01 (roitelets)
29. Ntare Rushatsi ni we koko yatanguje ubwami? — ✅ EP09 (🟢 mu migenzo)
30. Ibihe bice vy'Uburundi vyari bimaze gushika munsi y'ububasha bwiwe? — ✅ EP09
31. Ni gute yagiye ahuza abatware n'uturere dutandukanye? — ⚠️ EP09 muri rusangi, birimbitse EP11

## E. Intambara n'abamurwanya (32–37)

32. Ntare Rushatsi yarwanye na bande? — ✅ EP11 (Fumbije, Ruhinda)
