# MON PAYS NATAL — S1 — EP10 — ISOZERO RYA NTARE RUSHATSI

Twaramaze kwiyumvamwo inkuru imwe.

Inkuru ya Ntwero, Jabwe na Nsoro, Inamabuye, Buha n'umwungere Cambarantama — inkuru yitwa **umuzunguruko wa Nkoma**, iyiganje cane mu migenzo yakusanyijwe.

Ariko si yo yonyene.

Kandi iyi episode ni yo iheza inkuru ya Ntare Rushatsi — atari ngo turangize ivyo tuzi vyose, ahubwo ngo turangize urugendo twagenze kugira twumve neza aho tugeze.

---

## Indi nkomoko: umugenzo wa Kanyaru

Hari indi nkuru, itandukanye rwose, ivuga aho Ntare Rushatsi yaturutse.

Iyi nkuru yitwa **umugenzo wa Kanyaru**.

Ntibavuga Buha. Ntibavuga Inamabuye canke umwami w'i Buha.

Bavuga ko Ntare yaturutse mu **Rwanda**, akaca ku ruzi rwitwa **Kanyaru**, hanyuma akinjira mu Burundi biciye mu buraruko. Izina ryayo ryakomotse kuri urwo ruzi ubwarwo.

Ariko dutegerezwa kuvuga ukuri: iyi migenzo, mu masoko dufise ubu, ntiyaraboneka mu buryo burambuye nk'uko umuzunguruko wa Nkoma waboneka. Nta nkuru irambuye tudufise ivuga ico Ntare yakoze akiri mu Rwanda, ni bande yahuye na bo, canke ni iki cabaye igihe yashika mu Burundi biciye muri iyo nzira.

Aya makuru make afise agaciro gakomeye nyene: avuga ko **nta mvugo imwe gusa yigeze ivuga ku nkomoko ya Ntare Rushatsi**. Iyo tudufise ibisigo bibiri — kimwe kivuye mu buseruko, i Buha, kandi kikaba ariko cane cane cabitswe; ikindi kivuye mu buraruko, mu Rwanda, kikaba gato mu migenzo isigaye. Ntitwovuga ngo kimwe ni co "cy'ukuri" kandi ikindi ari "ikinyoma" — ni ukwibutsa gusa ko amateka y'inkomoko ya Ntare, uko yabitswe mu migenzo mvugo, atari umurongo umwe utagira ivunjwe.

---

## Intambara: Fumbije na Ruhinda

Ntare amaze gushika i Nkoma, ubwami ntibwatanguye ku bwiza. Bwatanguye ku ntambara.

Imigenzo ivuga ko Ntare, ashaka guhuza ibice bitandukanye vy'Uburundi bwa kera, yahuye n'abatware b'utwo turere duto. Aca abatsinda.

Hari **Jabwe**, twaramaze kwiga inkuru yiwe — atari intambara y'akarere, ahubwo ikibazo cagutse kubera kumugumana Ntare mu rugo rwiwe.

Hari n'andi mazina abiri: **Fumbije**, uwo imigenzo imwe ivuga ko Ntare yatsinze — nta karere kavugwa neza, nta gihe, nta nkuru y'intambara ubwayo, ijambo rimwe gusa. Kandi hari **Ruhinda**, intambara itamenyekana neza — kandi tudashobora kwemeza ko ari uwundi muntu, atari uwo mu Rugo rwo i Buha twize kera, uwitwa na we, mu bindi bisigo, Ruhaga.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Chrétien ("Du hirsute au hamite," 1981) yagize ati: « Jabwe et Fumbije voisinent, alors qu'un siècle et demi les séparent au moins » — bivuga ko Fumbije ari izina nyakuri riboneka mu migenzo y'aba "roitelets," ariko Chrétien ubwiwe avuga ko iyo migenzo ihuza abantu batandukanijwe n'ibihe birebire, ku buryo tutashobora kwizera intambara zabo nk'inkuru imwe ifise igihe kimwe.

Aha tugomba kwibuka: kutamenya ni na ryo igice c'amateka. Twashoboye kwerekana ukuntu Kiranga yapfuye kubera ko imigenzo yabitse inkuru irambuye. Ntitushoboye kwerekana ukuntu Ntare yatsinze Fumbije, kubera ko imigenzo ntiyabitse uwo murongo w'inkuru — izina gusa.

## Ikibazo gikomeye: ni gute yabaye umwami?

Hari ikibazo tutigeze dushobora gusubiza, naho twagerageje kenshi:

**Ni nde yamwemeye? Ni nde yamushize ku ngoma?**

Amasoko dufise ubu ntafise inyishu. Amasoko yose ari muri iyi repo ku vyerekeye "ukwimikwa" ni ay'abami bo mu myaka ya vuba — Mwambutsa, Ntare V — atari ay'uwo twita umwami wa mbere. Ni ikibazo dusigaranye, ata guhimba.

---

## Ivyo yasize

Twarabibonye: **Karyenda**, ingoma yavuye mu nkuru y'inzoka i Nkoma. **Umuganuro**, umunsi mukuru w'isarura yafatanywa. **Abaganwa**, umuryango w'ubutegetsi. **Bashingantahe**, urwego rw'ubutungane.

Ariko na ho, hari ikibazo kitarasubizwa: ivyo vyose, ni Ntare yabishinze bushasha, canke yasanze bimwe muri vyo bimaze kuba, akabikomeza gusa? Imigenzo ivuga ko ari we, ariko ntiyerekana neza umurongo uri hagati y'ukurema n'ukugumya.

---

## Urupfu rwiwe

Kandi hari ikibazo giheruka, gikomeye kurusha ibindi vyose: **Ntare yapfuye gute?**

Kuri Kiranga, twarabishoboye kuvuga — yishwe n'impongo, inkuru irambuye. Kuri Ryangombe, twarabishoboye kuvuga. Ariko kuri Ntare Rushatsi ubwiwe — umwami wa mbere, uwo urwo rugendo rwose ruzengurutseko — **nta nkuru na hamwe y'urupfu rwiwe idufitiye.**

Aho yapfiriye, ntibizwi. Uko yapfuye, ntibizwi. Ahantu bafata nk'aho yashinguwe ni **Budandari**, mu karere ka Kabarore, hamwe na Mwezi, Mutaga na Mwambutsa — ariko na ho tuvuga "bifatwa nka", atari "byemejwe."

Umwami wa mbere w'Uburundi, uko imigenzo imwibuka, apfa nta nkuru. Bishoboka ko ari ikimenyetso c'uko iyo nkuru, mu migenzo, iharirwa abandi — ababanjirije (Kiranga, Ryangombe) hamwe n'abamukurikira — mu gihe we ubwiwe, akaba ari **umuzi**, atagira inkuru y'iherezo yihariye. Ariko ivyo ni iciyumviro, atari icemezo.

---

## Ntare batatu

Ku isozero ry'iyi nkuru, dusubira ku gitekerezo twabonye kera muri EP09:

Ntare Rushatsi ni **batatu**.

**W'imigenzo** — umwami wa mbere, uwashinze Karyenda n'Umuganuro, uwahuje ibice vy'Uburundi bwa kera.

**W'amateka** — umuntu birashoboka cane yabayeho, ariko dukirekura vyinshi: igihe yabayemwo, uko yabaye umwami, uko yapfuye.

**W'imigani** — umwana ava i Buha, Cambarantama, umwungere, Kiranga, inzoka Inkoma, Karyenda — inkuru dukwiye kubika nk'umugenzo, atari nk'ivyabaye.
