# MON PAYS NATAL — EP11 — ABAMI BATANDATU B'IMPINDUKA

Ntare Rushatsi yarapfuye. Ingoma ntiyahagarara.

Izina ryagiye risubiramwo: Mwezi, Mutaga, Mwambutsa — hanyuma bagasubira kuri Ntare, uko ibisekuru bigenda.

Muri iki gice, ntituzokurikirana umwami umwe umwe mu buryo burambuye, nk'uko twagenje kuri Ntare Rushatsi. Kuko aba bami batandatu — bose bo mu rutonde rwa kera, hagati ya 1550 na 1720 — bahuriye ku kintu kimwe: **amakuru make cane.**

---

## Mwezi I Baridamunka

Uwakurikiye Ntare Rushatsi ku ngoma. Ni ryo zina gusa dufise ryizewe. Ntitwovuga na ngo yari umuhungu wa Ntare — kuba yaramukurikiye ku ngoma ntibisobanura ubuvukane. Se, nyina, aho yavukiye, uko yategetse, uko yapfuye — ntibizwi.

## Mutaga I Mutabazi

Uwakurikiye Mwezi I. "Mutaga" ni izina ry'ingoma, "Mutabazi" akaba ari ryo rimutandukanya n'abandi Mutaga. Ariko nta soko rikomeye ridufasha kumenya ikindi na kimwe ku buzima bwiwe.

## Mwambutsa I Nkomati

Uwakurikiye Mutaga I. Nta biography na hato. Ico dufise ni umwanya wiwe mu rutonde n'igihe kigereranijwe, hagati ya 1600 na 1620.

---

Aha hari ikintu gikomeye dukwiye kuvuga: amazina "Mwezi," "Mutaga," na "Mwambutsa" yagiye asubiramwo incuro nyinshi mu rutonde rw'abami — Mwezi I, Mwezi II, Mwezi III; Mutaga I, Mutaga II, Mutaga III. Iyo dutavye maso, dushobora kuvanga inkuru y'umwami umwe n'uwundi bafise izina rimwe. Ni co gituma tugenda buhoro, dutandukanya buri wese ku giti ciwe.

---

## Mutaga II Senyamwiza Mutamo

Aha hari ikintu kigomba kutwitondera cane.

Mu rutonde rwa kera, Mutaga II ashirwa hagati ya 1680 na 1700. Ariko hari **indi nkuru ikomeye cane**, y'intambara n'umwami w'u Rwanda Cyirima Rujugira, y'urukundo na Inabizoza (umukobwa wa Rujugira), n'urupfu ku rugamba — inkuru izwi cane.

Iyo nkuru ni iya **Mutaga Senyamwiza wo mu yindi chronology**, ashirwa hagati ya 1739 na 1767, uwo abashakashatsi bamwe bita Mutaga III. **Si Mutaga II wo mu rutonde rwa kera.**

Ntitwoshira inkuru ya Mutaga III kuri Mutaga II, naho basangiye izina. Kuri Mutaga II ubwiwe, uko ari mu rutonde rwa kera, ico dufise ni umwanya wiwe n'igihe kigereranijwe gusa.

## Mwambutsa II Nyarushamba

Uwakurikiye Mutaga II. Nako, ntutuvange na Mwambutsa III (uzwi nka Mbariza, ~1767–1796) canke Mwambutsa IV Bangiricenge (umwami wa nyuma w'igihe c'ubwami, 1915–1966). Izina "Nyarushamba" ni ryo rimutandukanya, ariko nta soko riduhagije risobanura aho iryo zina rikomoka. Ubuzima bwiwe: nta co dufise.

## Mwezi III Kavuyimbo, canke Ndagushimiye

Uyu na we, mu bushakashatsi bumwe, yitwa "Mwezi I Ndagushimiye" mu rundi rutonde ("modern chronology"), naho ari Mwezi III mu rutonde rwa kera. Ni co gituma tuzokoresha amazina yombi hamwe, kugira ntihagire uwibaza ko ari ikosa.

Kuri we, hari amakuru make yiyongera ku bandi: imigenzo imuhuza n'abami b'u Rwanda Yuhi III Mazimpaka na Karemera Rwaka, akaba se, muri iyo genealogy, wa Mutaga Senyamwiza (uwo tuvuze hejuru). Kandi hari inkuru y'ubuhamya bw'ahantu: **Budandari**, muri Kabarore, ivugwa nk'aho hashinguwe abami bane — Ntare Rushatsi, Mwezi Ndagushimiye, Mutaga Senyamwiza, na Mwambutsa Mbariza.

Ariko na ho, tuvuga 🟡 — ni ubuhamya bw'ahantu, atari inyandiko y'ico gihe.

---

Aba bami batandatu bahuriye ku kintu kimwe: bafise **umwanya**, ata **nkuru**.

Ntitwobasiga mu nkuru — bagize uruhara mu ngoma y'Uburundi, kandi izina ryabo ryabitswe mu rutonde. Ariko ntitwobahimbira ubuzima kugira gusa iki gice kibe kirekire.

Ico dufise ni indango: ingoma yakomeje, izina rikagenda risubiramwo, kandi imigenzo mvugo, uko ibihe biraheruka, iharira inkuru abami bamwe, ikayibura abandi.

---

Ariko hari umwami umwe azoca akurikira, uwo tuzoshobora kuvuga byinshi kumurusha — kuko afitaniye isano n'intambara zizwi, n'umwami w'u Rwanda, n'inkuru y'inka.

**MON PAYS NATAL — EP12 — NTARE II KIBOGORA**, ni ho tuzomubona.
