# MON PAYS NATAL — EP16 — MWAMBUTSA III MBARIZA (MBONYUBURUNDI)

Mutaga III Sebitungwa amaze kugwa i Nkanda, yasize abahungu batatu.

Bose barashaka kuba umwami.

Ariko ubutegetsi ntibwahawe uwo bahuriyeho canke uwo bemeranyijweko.

Bwahawe uwo umugenzo wemeje.

---

## Ikigeragezo ca Karyenda

Abo bahungu batatu — **Mbonyingingo**, **Sebitungwa**, na **Ntisemberwa** — bose bariko barahiganwa. Kugira ngo bamenye ni nde akwiye kuba umwami, haciye hashirwaho ikigeragezo: uwushobora **guterura Karyenda**, ingoma y'ubwami, ni we yari kuba umwami.

Ntisemberwa, ari we mutoyi muri bo, ni we ashobora kuyiterura.

Aca ahabwa izina: **Mwambutsa Mbariza**.

Karyenda, twaramaze kubimenya kuva kuri Ntare Rushatsi, ntiyari ingoma yo gukubita umuziki gusa. Yari ikimenyetso c'ubwami n'ububasha. Ubushobozi bwo kuyiterura bwafashwe nk'ikimenyetso c'uko umugenzo ubwawo wari watoye uwo mwana.

---

## Umwami akiri muto

Imigenzo ivuga ko Mwambutsa Mbariza yari akiri muto cane igihe yaja ku ngoma. Kubera iyo mpamvu, abamurezi baramufashije mu ntango y'ingoma yiwe — hakabaho ubutegetsi bw'abantu bamufasha gutwara igihugu.

---

## Imigambi yo kumwica

Ubuto bwiwe ntibwabujije abandi kwifuza ubutegetsi.

Imigenzo ivuga ko bamwe mu bamwegereye bagerageje kumwica. Hari inkuru izwi cane: bamutumira koga mu ruzi **Kogabami**, ariko bakaba barashize amacumu akarishe mu mazi imbere.

Mwambutsa ngo yarakekeranije uwo mugambi, arungika umukozi imbere yiwe. Uwo mukozi ni we winjira mu mazi, agapfa mu mwanya wiwe.

Ni umugani. Ntituwushire nk'ikintu cemejwe. Ariko urerekana amakimbirane y'ubutegetsi yari inyuma y'ugutoranya umwami.

---

## Umusi mukuru w'urupfu

Imigenzo ivuga ko Mwambutsa, amaze kubona ko hari abamurwanya, yafashe ingingo yo kubakuraho.

Yateguye umusi mukuru ku kirimba, atumira abo yakeka ko bari mu mugambi wo kumwica. Bamaze kunywa urwarwa, hateguwa umuriro — benshi baca barapfa.

Iyi na yo ni inkuru y'imigenzo, ntitwoyikoresha nk'ukuri kw'amateka atarongera kwemezwa n'ibindi bimenyetso.

---

## Umuriro wa Kinywamagana

Hanyuma haza inkuru y'iherezo, ikomeye kuruta izindi zose.

Imigenzo ivuga ko umuriro waciye ukwira igihugu cose, ugatera amakuba n'amapfa. Abapfumu bo mu migenzo ngo babwiye umwami ko ari we nyanduruko w'ayo makuba, kandi ko ategerezwa kwitanga kugira igihugu gisubire mu mahoro.

Imigenzo ivuga ko Mwambutsa yagiye mu muriro i **Kinywamagana**, arapfa, hanyuma amapfa aracika.

Ntitwovuga "Mwambutsa Mbariza yiyahuye." Tuvuga: **mu migenzo yanditswe, havugwa ko Mwambutsa yitangiye mu muriro i Kinywamagana kugira ngo igihugu gikire amakuba.** Ni ivyatandukanye rwose.

---

## Umurage wiwe

Inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe, ingoma yagiye kuri **Ngambiri**, uwafashe izina rya **Ntare Rugamba** — uwo, mu nkuru y'umuryango imwe, avugwa nk'umuhungu wiwe. Ariko inkuru z'imiryango ziratandukanye, ni co gituma tudashaka kwinjiza urwo rutonde nk'ukuri kwemejwe.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Nduwamahoro (thesis) yagize ati: "akiri umuyabaga yitwa Ngambiri. Ntare Rugamba yabaye umwami akomeye cane" — bikemeza ko "Ngambiri" ari ryo zina Ntare Rugamba yari afise akiri muto, imbere yo kwitwa "Rugamba" kubera ubutwari bwiwe ku rugamba.

Uwo Ntare Rugamba ni we azoba umwami w'ingenzi cane muri urwo rutonde — uwo tuzomuha season yiwe bwite.

---

## Ico dutazi

Aho Mwambutsa III yahambwe ntibisobanutse. Kandi hari amazina menshi amuranga — Mbonyuburundi, Mbariza, Serushambo Butama — bivuye mu masoko atandukanye, ata gihamya c'uko ari izina rimwe ryemejwe.
