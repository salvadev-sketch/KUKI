# MON PAYS NATAL — S1 — EP06 — RYANGOMBE

Kiranga yapfuye, ariko ntiyazimiye.

Izina ryiwe ryabandanije mu mihango ya Kubandwa, mu bantu bagaragaza impwemu yiwe, mu migenzo yagumye iva ku gisekuru kija ku kindi.

Ariko uko dukurikirana iyo migenzo, iva mu Burundi, ikaja mu Rwanda, hari izina rimwe ryinjira mu nkuru — izina risa n'iry'uwo tumaze kumenya, ariko rikaba ari irindi:

**Ryangombe.**

---

Mu Rwanda, Ryangombe avugwa nk'**umwami w'Imandwa** — umukuru w'imyuka yubahirizwa muri Kubandwa, idini rishingiye ku gusabana n'iyo myuka biciye mu mihango yo kwinjizwa.

Ariko ntitwovuga ko yari umwami w'igihugu, nk'uko dufata Ntare Rushatsi.

Yari umwami mu **muryango w'imyuka n'imigenzo y'idini** — atari mu bwami bwa politike.

Abashakashatsi bamwita "héros mythique" — intwari y'imigenzo. Hari inkuru zimwerekana nk'umuntu yabayeho, ariko izo nkuru zuzuyemwo amayobera n'ivy'idini.

---

Kandi hari ikibazo kimwe kikomeye kuruta ibindi vyose:

**Ryangombe na Kiranga, mbega ni umuntu umwe?**

Umuhinga Luc de Heusch yigeze kubivuga: Kiranga ashobora kuba ari **"doublet"** ya Ryangombe — ni ukuvuga ishusho isa cane, yahindutse uko yinjiye mu migenzo y'u Burundi. 🔴 *(Igitabu ca Luc de Heusch ubwaco ntikiraboneka muri iyi repo — dufise gusa aho Chrétien (1993) awuvugako mu bisobanuro biwe, atari umwandiko nyawo wa de Heusch. Rikeneye kwongera kwemezwa hakoreshejwe igitabu ciwe nyaco.)*

Impamvu yatanze: bombi ni abahigi bakomeye, kandi inkuru z'urupfu rwabo zisa cane — bombi bishwe n'inyamaswa ifise amahembe. Twaraye tubibona kuri Kiranga: yishwe n'impongo. Muri Rwanda, Ryangombe na we avugwa ko yishwe agiye guhiga kwa nyuma, n'inyamaswa ifise amahembe.

Ariko ico ni **iciyumviro c'umuhinga**, si icemezo c'amateka.

---

Hari n'ikindi kimenyetso gishimishije: **Binego.**

Mu Rwanda, Binego avugwa nk'umwana wa Ryangombe, umwe mu bantu b'ingenzi mu mihango ya Kubandwa.

Mu Burundi, Binego avugwa nk'umwana — canke umufasha — wa **Kiranga**.

Ni ukuvuga:

Mu Rwanda: **Ryangombe → Binego.**

Mu Burundi: **Kiranga → Binego.**

Ivyo ntibihagije ngo tuvuge ko Ryangombe na Kiranga ari umuntu umwe. Ariko biratanga ikimenyetso gikomeye c'uko imigenzo y'abo bantu yagiye ihinduka no gusubirwamwo, uko yinjiye mu turere dutandukanye.

---

Kubera iki amazina yahindutse gutya?

Kuko Kubandwa ntiyari idini ry'igihugu kimwe gusa.

Yari umugenzo wakwiragiye mu bice byinshi vy'akarere k'ibiyaga bigari — Rwanda, Uburundi, Buha, na kure kuruta aho.

Mu Rwanda, umukuru w'Imandwa yitwa Ryangombe.

Mu Burundi no mu Buha, akenshi yitwa Kiranga.

Ntibisobanura ko ari umuntu umwe wemejwe. Bisobanura ko imigenzo y'idini rimwe, uko yinjira mu turere dutandukanye, itwara amazina atandukanye — ariko igasigara ifise igishushanyo kimwe: umukuru w'imyuka, umuhuza hagati y'abantu n'Imana, uwazwi mu bihe by'ubuhigi, uwapfuye ashwe n'inyamaswa ifise amahembe.

---

Ariko turatanga ubwitonzi bumwe.

Ntitwovuga: "Ryangombe ni Kiranga, nta tandukaniro na rimwe rihari." Ivyo ni ugusimbuka ikibazo, atari ugukiyisha.

Kandi ntitwovuga ko umuntu yitwa Ryangombe yavuye mu Rwanda akaza mu Burundi ubwiwe, nka nyene uko yagenda ku rugendo. Nta cemezo c'amateka kivuga gutyo.

Ico dufise ni:

Imigenzo ihuza Ryangombe na Kiranga.

Amazina y'abantu b'Imandwa ahinduka bivanye n'akarere.

Binego agasubira mu migenzo yombi.

Imigani igasa ku bintu byinshi.

Ariko nta muryango wizewe uduhuza Ryangombe na Ntare Rushatsi. Ntitubahuza nk'umuryango.

---

Ryangombe rero, dufise ico dushobora kuvuga: yari umukuru w'Imandwa n'intwari ikomeye ya Kubandwa mu migenzo yo mu karere k'ibiyaga bigari. Mu Rwanda izina ryiwe rizwi cane. Mu Burundi no mu Buha, imigenzo imwe ikoresha izina Kiranga ku muntu afise uruhara rusa. Hari abashakashatsi babona Kiranga nk'ishusho ihindutse ya Ryangombe.

Ariko nta cemezo gihagije dufise co kuvuga ko ari umuntu umwe mu buryo bw'amateka.

Turamwugarira aha.

---

Ariko izina rimwe ryamaze kwigaragaza kabiri — rimwe mu nkuru ya Ryangombe, rindi mu nkuru ya Kiranga — riri gusigara.

Umwana, canke umufasha, agaragara mu migenzo yombi, ahantu habiri, mu miryango ibiri itandukanye.

**Binego.**

**MON PAYS NATAL — S1 — EP07 — BINEGO**, ni ho tuzoheza tumenya uwo muntu ari we, kandi ico afasha kumenya ku isano hagati ya Kiranga na Ryangombe.
