# MON PAYS NATAL — S1 — INCAMAKE Y'IGICE CA MBERE

Twaratanguye ku musozi ucecetse.

Twaravuze ngo: mu misozi y'Uburundi hari inkuru.

Kandi twarabajije ikibazo tutarashoboye gusubiza vuba: **Uburundi bwatanguye gute?**

Ubu, tumaze kunyura mu bibanza icumi, reka dusubire inyuma gato — atari ngo turangize inkuru, ahubwo ngo turabe inzira twaciyemwo, imbere yo kubandanya.

---

Twaratanguye kuri **Ntwero.**

Umukuru w'akarere, atari umwami w'igihugu cose.

Twarabonye ko afitaniye isano na Bututsi na Bushi, mu migenzo ibiri itandukanye tutigeze duhitamwo.

Yari **irembo**, atari inkuru yuzuye.

---

Hanyuma haza **Jabwe na Nsoro**, abahungu biwe.

Imvura yaguye ku Jabwe.

Urugendo rwatumye ashika kwa Nsoro, i Gashinyira.

Kandi ico gitondo, mu buryo imigenzo ivuga, hazovamwo umwana azoba **Ntare Rushatsi**.

Intambara ikaza gukurikira, Nsoro akazimira i Gitanga — inkuru izosigara mu mugani: **"kunyika nka Nsoro."**

---

Uwo mwana, Ntare, ntiyakuriye mu ntambara.

Yajanywe i **Buha**, kw'ishami ry'umuryango wiwe — kuko **Inamabuye**, mushiki wa Jabwe na Nsoro, yari yararongowe n'umwami w'i Buha.

Ng'aho, Ntare yabaye **umwungere**.

Yitwa **Cambarantama.**

Impfizi yiwe iratsinda iy'umwami.

Umwami aramutinya.

Ntare **arahunga**, aca ku ruzi rwa Malagarazi, agashika i **Nkoma**.

---

Uwo mwami wo i Buha, twaramwise **Ruhaga**, canke **Ruhinda** — kuko amasoko atandukana. Twarabonye ko atagenzwa uwo yari we cane, kandi ko izina Ruhinda mu Burundi ritagenzwa n'iryo mu bindi bihugu vyo mu karere k'ibiyaga bigari.

---

I Nkoma, Ntare ahura n'ikindi kintu: idini.

**Kiranga**, umukuru wa Kubandwa, umuhuza hagati y'abantu n'Imana.

Twarabonye ukuntu yapfuye — yishwe n'impongo, mu nkuru y'agatima gakomeye — kandi ukuntu yagumye ari umugenzo, atari umuntu gusa.

Twaramubonye ashamikiye na **Ryangombe**, umukuru w'Imandwa mu Rwanda, hamwe n'inkuru y'umuhinga Luc de Heusch avuga ngo ni "doublet" imwe.

Kandi twaramubonye kumwe na **Binego**, umwana-umwambari w'icumu, agaragara mu miryango yombi.

---

Hanyuma twarasubiye kuri Ntare, aho yagera i Nkoma.

Impfizi yiwe irarambitswe ku mugina.

**Inzoka Inkoma** iraza ikubita ku rusato.

Ijwi rirumvikana.

**Karyenda** iravuka.

Ntare atangura **Umuganuro.**

---

Ubwami buratangura.

Ariko twarabonye ko amatariki atazwi neza — hari uwuvuga 1530, uwundi 1680, uwundi kinjana ca 15.

Twarabonye ko Ntare Rushatsi na **Ntare Rugamba** ari abami babiri batandukanye, kandi ko imigenzo yaragiye ivanga ibihe vyabo.

Twarabonye ko ubwami bwa mbere butari bufise imbibe nk'iz'Uburundi bw'ubu.

Kandi twaragabanije Ntare Rushatsi mu **batatu**: uwo mu migenzo, uwo mu mateka, n'uwo mu migani.

---

Amaherezo, twarabonye ko hari **indi nkuru y'inkomoko** — **umugenzo wa Kanyaru** — uvuga ko Ntare yaturutse mu Rwanda, atari i Buha. Twarabonye ko ari make cane, ariko ko ari ikimenyetso gikomeye: nta nkuru imwe gusa ivuga ku ntango y'Uburundi.

---

Muri iryo tororokanya ryose, hari ikintu kimwe twagumye dukurikiza, kuva ku ntango gushika ubu:

**Ntitwahisemwo hagati y'imigenzo.**

Aho amasoko atandukana, twarayerekanye ko atandukana.

Aho ata cemezo gihagije, twaravuze: **ntiturabizi neza.**

Kandi twagumye dukurikiza chronology — kuva ku muzi w'umuryango, gushika ku kimenyetso c'ubwami, tutigeze dusimbuka ibihe.

---

Ariko urugendo ntiruraheza.

Hari intambara za Ntare — kuri **Fumbije** na **Ruhinda** — dukiri kuza kwiga, naho amakuru ari make.

Hari urupfu rwa Ntare ubwiwe, rukiguma ari ikibazo gifunguye.

Kandi inyuma y'ivyo vyose, hari **abandi bantu 100** batwitezeko.

Abami bakurikira.

Abagabo n'abagore babaye inyuma yabo.

Abantu batazwi cane, ariko basize ikimenyetso mu rugendo rw'igihugu.

**MON PAYS NATAL** ntizoba yaratanguye kwerekana ivyo tuzi.

Izoba yaratanguye kwerekana n'ivyo tutazi.

Kandi urugendo, nk'uko twabivuze kuva ku ntango, ntiruzohagarara ku ntango.

**Ruzokomeza.**
