# MON PAYS NATAL — EP12 — NTARE II KIBOGORA

Inyuma y'aba bami batandatu bo mu mwijima, hari umwe muri bo tudashobora kuraba mu buryo bumwe.

Kuko afise inkuru.

Inkuru imwe gusa, ariko ikomeye.

Izina ryiwe: **Ntare II Kibogora.**

---

## Izina rifise ivyifuzo bibiri

Mu nyandiko za Alexis Kagame, umuhinga yize imivugo y'abami b'u Rwanda, uyu mwami avugwa ku mazina abiri: **Kibogora**, canke, mu bindi bisigo, **Bijanyali**. 🔴 *(Igitabu ca Alexis Kagame ubwaco ntikiraboneka muri iyi repo — iri zina "Bijanyali" rikeneye kwongera kwemezwa.)*

Ivyo bituma tubona ikintu gikomeye: n'izina ry'umwami ubwaryo rishobora kugira variants, uko imigenzo yagiye igenda.

Aza mu rutonde rwa kera inyuma ya **Mwambutsa I Nkomati**, imbere ya **Mwezi II Nyaburunga**. Igihe kigereranijwe: hagati ya 1620 na 1650. Ariko, nk'uko twabimenye kenshi, ayo matariki y'abami ba mbere ni ayagereranijwe gusa.

---

## Intambara y'u Bugesera

Hari inkuru imwe ikomeye ivuga kuri Ntare II — inkuru ivuye mu migenzo y'amateka y'u Rwanda.

Rwagitare, umwami w'u Bugesera, avugwa nk'uwahanganye na Ntare Kibogora w'Uburundi. Rwagitare aca ahamagara umwami w'u Rwanda, Ruganzu II Ndoli, ngo amufashe. Ruganzu arungika ingabo ze zitwa **Ibisumizi**. Ingabo za Ntare II zirasubizwa inyuma.

Hari n'iyindi version ivuga ko, inyuma y'iyo ntambara, haje amasezerano y'amahoro n'uguhanahana imico hagati y'ibihugu bibiri. Ntitwovuga rimwe gusa "yaratsinzwe, birarangira" — imigenzo ivuga ko haciye hagakurikira ibindi.

Ariko dutegerezwa kubivuga uko bikwiye: iyi nkuru ntiyavuye ku nyandiko yo mu gihe ca Ntare II ubwiwe. Yavuye mu **migenzo y'amateka y'u Rwanda**, iza kwandikwa n'abashakashatsi nyuma y'imyaka myinshi. Ni co gituma tudashobora kuvuga "mu mwaka kanaka, Ntare II yateye u Bugesera" — ahubwo tuvuga "imigenzo y'amateka y'u Rwanda ivuga ko."

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Nduwamahoro (thesis, asubiramwo Mworoha, 1987:115) avuga ko "amasoko y'i Rwanda... avuga ko mu gice ca mbere c'ikinjana ca 17, ingoma y'Ubugesera yaganzwa na Ntare Kibogora yari abangabanganywe na Ruganzu Ndori, umwami w'Urwanda." Iyi soko iduha n'igihe kigereranijwe: **intango y'ikinjana ca 17**.

---

## Umurongo utukura: amatariki n'amazina

Aha hari ikintu gikomeye kigomba kutwitondera.

Hari **urutonde rwa kera** rushira Ntare II hagati ya 1620 na 1650. Ariko hari n'**urundi rutonde rwa none**, ruvuga ko ingoma y'Uburundi yatanguye ku muntu witwa "Ntare III Rushatsi," hafi ya 1680 — chronology itandukanye rwose.

Kandi hari ikindi kibazo: Ruganzu II Ndoli, mu bushakashatsi bwinshi bw'u Rwanda, ashirwa ku ngoma mu ntango z'ikinjana ca 16 (hafi 1510–1543) — igihe kitahuye n'urutonde rushira Ntare II hagati ya 1620-1650.

Rero: ntitwovuga ngo turazi neza igihe iyi ntambara yabereye. Twemeza gusa ko imigenzo ihuza abo bantu babiri.

Kandi ikindi cane: **ntitwovanga Ntare II Kibogora na Ntare IV Rutaganzwa Rugamba**, uwundi mwami, wo mu kinjana ca 19. Amasoko amwe arashobora kuvanga ayo mazina.

---

## Karyenda na Karinga

Hari akantu gato, ariko gashimishije: mu gihe kimwe Ruganzu II Ndoli avugwa ko yatanguje ingoma y'ubwami y'u Rwanda, **Karinga**, Uburundi bwabo bwari bumaze kugira ingoma yabwo, **Karyenda**, iyo twize kera ihuye n'inkuru ya Ntare Rushatsi.

Ivyo ntibiduha ikintu Ntare II ubwiwe yakoze, ariko biduha **igihe**: yashizwe mu gihe ingoma z'ubwami n'ibimenyetso vyazo vyari bimaze kugira uruhara rukomeye muri politike y'akarere kose.

---

## Ico tutazi

Se wiwe, nyina, aho yavukiye, uwo yubakanye, abana biwe, uko yagiye ku ngoma, izindi ntambara, urupfu rwiwe, n'aho yahambwe — vyose biracari **🔴 ntibizwi**.

Ntare II Kibogora ntaragera ku rwego rwo kuvuga ngo tumuzi neza, nk'uko tuzi Ntare Rushatsi. Ariko afise inkuru imwe ikomeye, kandi iyo nkuru ni yo iyamuha ikibanza kiwe muri iyi documentaire — atari uko amakuru ari menshi, ahubwo kuko iryo somo rimwe rifise uburemere.

---

Uwakurikiye Ntare II ku ngoma, mu rutonde rwa kera, yitwa **Mwezi II Nyaburunga**.
