# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 1 — ISOZERO

Twaratanguye ku musozi ucecetse, tubaza ikibazo kimwe: **Uburundi bwatanguye gute?**

Kuva ng'aho, twanyuze mu bibanza icumi na gatandatu.

---

Twarabonye **Ntwero**, umukuru w'akarere. **Jabwe na Nsoro**, bahanganira ubutegetsi, umwe akazimira mu bishanga. **Ntare Rushatsi**, umwana avutse mu ntambara, umwungere yambaye urusato rw'intama, uwahunze i Buha aza kugera i Nkoma — aho urusato rw'impfizi yiwe rwavuyemwo ijwi ry'ingoma **Karyenda**.

Twarabonye idini ryagendanye n'ubwami: **Kiranga**, **Ryangombe**, **Binego**.

Kandi twaramenye ko Ntare Rushatsi yagumye ari ikibazo kirenze ico ari inyishu — atazwi uko yimikwe, ivyo yahinduye, canke uko yapfuye. Yarasize izina, ingoma ikakomeza.

---

Hanyuma twaciye mu bami cumi na umwe bakurikiye.

Bamwe muri bo bafise izina gusa — **Mwezi I, Mutaga I, Mwambutsa I, Mutaga II, Mwambutsa II, Mwezi III** — abo twahuje muri episode imwe kubera ko imigenzo itabashigikiye n'inkuru zirambuye.

Abandi bafise inkuru zabo bwite: **Ntare II Kibogora**, n'intambara ya Bugesera. **Mwezi II Nyaburunga**, n'inkuru ya Ngaye n'ihinduka ry'imigenzo. **Ntare III Kivimira**, umwami w'intambara, uwafashe u Bugesera, akagwa, mu migenzo, ku nka Gitare. **Mutaga III Sebitungwa**, uwarongoye umukobwa w'umwami w'u Rwanda, akagwa mu ntambara ya Nkanda. **Mwambutsa III Mbariza**, uwaterūye Karyenda akiri muto, akiyitanga mu muriro wa Kinywamagana kugira igihugu gikire amapfa.

---

Muri urwo rugendo rwose, hari itegeko dukurikije, kuva ku ntango:

Aho amasoko abiri atandukana, ntitwahisemwo iryo tubona ryiza kurusha irindi.

Aho ata gihamya gihagije, twaravuze: **ntiturabizi.**

Kandi twagumye dukurikiza igihe, ata n'ahamwe twasimbutse.

---

**SEASON 1 irarangiye aha.**

Abami cumi na barindwi bagize ubwami bwa mbere bw'Uburundi, kuva Ntare Rushatsi, bamaze kuvugwa.

Ariko hari umwe muri bo udasa n'abandi.

Umwe biciyeko urwego — atari ku ngoma yiwe gusa, ahubwo ku bunini n'imiterere y'ubwami bwose.
