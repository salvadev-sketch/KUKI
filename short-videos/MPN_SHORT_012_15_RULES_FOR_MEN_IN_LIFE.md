Today, we're breaking down 15 Rules for Men in Life.

Let's get into it.

---

1. Don't Return to Betrayal

If someone repeatedly breaks your trust, don't ignore the lesson. Respect yourself enough to recognize when a relationship is no longer healthy.

---

2. Don't Accept Disrespect

Set boundaries. You can be calm and respectful while making it clear how you expect to be treated.

---

3. Stand When Appropriate

Simple manners matter. When greeting someone or shaking hands, standing when appropriate can show confidence and respect.

---

4. Don't Go Broke to Impress People

You don't need expensive clothes, cars, or lifestyles to prove your worth. Protect your money and build your future.

---

5. Respect What Belongs to Others

If someone bought something, don't assume you're entitled to the last piece. Small acts of consideration reveal your character.

---

6. Always Aim to Improve

Don't compete with everyone around you. Compete with the person you were yesterday. Keep learning, growing, and becoming better.

---

7. Protect and Respect Others

Look after the people who depend on you, and treat the people beside you with respect. Strength should come with responsibility.

---

8. Pause Before You Answer

You don't have to answer every question immediately. Take a moment to think, then respond clearly and calmly.

---

9. Don't Beg for Love

A healthy relationship should involve mutual interest and effort. Don't destroy your self-respect trying to convince someone to choose you.

---

10. Take Care of Your Body

Exercise regularly, eat reasonably well, rest, and take your physical health seriously. Your body supports everything else you want to accomplish.

---

11. Respect Invitations

If you're not invited somewhere, don't force your way into the situation. Respect other people's space and decisions.

---

12. Be Prepared

Don't depend completely on one payment method or one source of support. Carry what you reasonably need and prepare for unexpected situations.

---

13. Dress With Self-Respect

You don't need expensive clothes to look good. Keep yourself clean, presentable, and dressed appropriately for the situation.

---

14. Learn to Listen

Don't just wait for your turn to speak. Listen carefully, make appropriate eye contact, and show people that you value what they're saying.

---

15. Build More Than One Income Skill

Don't depend on one skill forever. Learn skills that can create additional opportunities and strengthen your financial future.

---
