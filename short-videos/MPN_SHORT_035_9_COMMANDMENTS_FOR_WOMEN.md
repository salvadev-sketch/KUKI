Today, we're talking about 9 COMMANDMENTS FOR WOMEN.

Let's get into it.

---

1. Don't Rush to Leave Home

Independence is valuable, but rushing into it without preparation can create unnecessary pressure. Leave when you're ready to support yourself and manage your responsibilities.

---

2. Don't Wait for a Man to Start Living

Build a life you enjoy on your own. A relationship should add to your life—not become the only reason you feel fulfilled.

---

3. Be Careful With Alcohol

You don't have to drink to fit in. Protect your judgment, your health, and your ability to make decisions you won't regret.

---

4. Be Selective About Who You Give Access To

Not every late-night call deserves your attention. Protect your time, your boundaries, and your safety.

---

5. Take Care of Your Body

Eat well, stay active, and develop healthy habits. Your body is part of the life you're building.

---

6. Present Yourself Well

How you dress can influence first impressions, but appearance isn't your entire identity. Let your character, confidence, and behavior speak too.

---

7. Don't Marry for Money Alone

Financial stability matters, but a healthy marriage needs more than money. Look for respect, trust, compatibility, responsibility, and genuine care.

---

8. Build Your Own Future

Develop your skills. Build your career. Learn how money works. Never make another person your only plan for security.

---

9. Beauty Is Not Your Only Asset

Beauty can change with time. Character, intelligence, discipline, confidence, and valuable skills can continue growing throughout your life.
