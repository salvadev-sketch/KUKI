Today, we're breaking down 12 Life Rules for a Happier Life.

Let's get into it.

---

1. Believe Problems Can Be Solved

Some problems take time.

Some require help.

But don't assume that today's pain is your permanent future.

Look for the next step.

---

2. Change How You See Yourself

The way you think about yourself affects how you live.

Don't constantly remind yourself of your weaknesses.

Recognize your value and work on becoming better.

---

3. Don't Live for Other People's Opinions

People will talk.

Some opinions will help you.

Others won't.

Listen to useful feedback, but don't let every comment control your emotions.

---

4. Choose Your Friends Carefully

Stay close to people who respect you and encourage growth.

You don't need relationships that constantly bring you down.

---

5. Make Time for Things You Enjoy

Work matters, but life needs balance.

Exercise.

Listen to music.

Read.

Play.

Enjoy your hobbies.

---

6. Don't Let Money Define Your Worth

Someone having more money doesn't make them more valuable than you.

Your financial situation can change.

Keep learning, working, and improving.

---

7. Don't Give Up Easily

Difficult seasons happen.

You may fail.

You may have to start again.

Keep moving.

---

8. Make Time for Prayer

If faith is important to you, make prayer part of your life.

Use that time to reflect, seek guidance, and strengthen your hope.

---

9. Be Courageous

If you want something meaningful, you may have to step outside your comfort zone.

Take calculated risks.

Learn from failure.

Keep moving toward your goals.

---

10. Surround Yourself With Supportive People

Your environment matters.

Choose people who encourage you, respect you, and want to see you grow.

---

11. Practice Gratitude

Don't focus only on what you don't have.

Notice what you do have.

Be grateful for the people, opportunities, and progress in your life.

---

12. Keep Working Toward Your Goals

Happiness isn't only about feeling good today.

Having purpose matters too.

Set goals.

Take action.

And keep improving.
