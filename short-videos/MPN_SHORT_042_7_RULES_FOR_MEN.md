Today, we're talking about 7 RULES FOR MEN.

Let's get into it.

---

1. Become Strong, but Stay Disciplined

Strength without self-control can become a weakness. Build your body, mind, and character—but learn to control your power.

---

2. Build Yourself, Not Excuses

If you're struggling, look for what you can improve. Train when you're weak, learn when you're lost, and take responsibility for your next step.

---

3. Earn Your Value Every Day

Potential is only a possibility. Your habits, skills, reliability, and results are what turn potential into something real.

---

4. Master Your Emotions

Anger, jealousy, fear, and frustration are normal emotions. The goal isn't to eliminate them—it's to learn how to respond without letting them control your decisions.

---

5. Stop Chasing and Start Building

Don't make attention, money, or relationships your entire mission. Build a meaningful life, develop your skills, and become someone you respect.

---

6. Accept Discomfort for Growth

Progress often requires doing things that aren't comfortable. Choose the temporary discomfort of discipline over repeatedly living with the consequences of avoiding what matters.

---

7. Live by a Code

Choose principles that guide your actions: honesty, loyalty, responsibility, courage, and respect. When your values are clear, difficult decisions become easier to navigate.
