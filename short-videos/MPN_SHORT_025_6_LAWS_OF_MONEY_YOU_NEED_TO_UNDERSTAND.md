Today, we're breaking down 6 LAWS OF MONEY YOU NEED TO UNDERSTAND.

Let's get into it.

1. MONEY FOLLOWS VALUE.

Simply wanting money doesn't create wealth. Developing useful skills, solving problems, creating value, and managing opportunities can improve your ability to earn.

---

2. EARNING MONEY ISN'T ENOUGH.

Someone can earn a lot and still struggle financially. What matters is how you manage what you earn—especially your spending, saving, and investing decisions.

---

3. MONEY IS A TOOL, NOT A MASTER.

Money can make life easier, but if you allow it to control every decision, you can lose sight of what really matters. Use money to support your values and goals.

---

4. SAVE SOME OF WHAT YOU EARN.

Income can change, but unexpected expenses will happen. Building savings gives you a financial cushion when life doesn't go according to plan.

---

5. DON'T LET MONEY CHANGE WHO YOU ARE.

Money can provide comfort and opportunity, but it shouldn't cost you your integrity, relationships, or humanity. Build wealth without losing yourself.

---

6. MORE MONEY DOESN'T AUTOMATICALLY MEAN ENOUGH.

As income grows, desires can grow too. Financial peace comes from knowing what is enough and learning to control your wants—not simply earning more.
