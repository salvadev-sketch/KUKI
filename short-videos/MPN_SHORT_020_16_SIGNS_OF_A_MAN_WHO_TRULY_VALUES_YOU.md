Today, we're breaking down 16 Signs of a Man Who Truly Values You.

Let's get into it.

---

1. He Doesn't Let Conflict Drag On

He wants problems resolved, not ignored. He values peace and communication.

---

2. He Makes Time for You

Even when life gets busy, he finds ways to make your relationship a priority.

---

3. He Checks In

He genuinely wants to know how you're doing, not just what you can do for him.

---

4. He Makes an Effort to Make You Happy

He pays attention to the little things that matter to you.

---

5. He Knows How to Compromise

He understands that a healthy relationship isn't about winning every argument.

---

6. He's Consistent

He doesn't disappear whenever things become difficult. His effort remains steady.

---

7. He's Thoughtful

Small surprises, kind messages, or thoughtful gestures can show that you're on his mind.

---

8. He Admits When He's Wrong

A mature man can say, "I was wrong." Pride should never be more important than the relationship.

---

9. He Communicates His Feelings

He doesn't make you constantly guess how he feels. He communicates honestly.

---

10. He Takes Care of Himself

He makes an effort to look presentable and take pride in himself.

---

11. He Enjoys Your Company

He enjoys being with you without always expecting something in return.

---

12. He Misses You

Distance doesn't make him forget you. He appreciates your presence and looks forward to seeing you again.

---

13. He Makes Reasonable Sacrifices

Sometimes love means adjusting your plans or giving up a little for someone you genuinely care about.

---

14. He Supports You

When you need support, he doesn't immediately disappear. He stands beside you when it matters.

---

15. He Doesn't Want to Sleep on Unresolved Anger

He understands that disagreements happen, but he tries to resolve them instead of allowing resentment to grow.

---

16. He Remembers What Makes You Happy

He pays attention to what you enjoy and sometimes does those things simply because they matter to you.

---
