Today, we're breaking down 20 UNSPOKEN RULES OF RESPECT AND CHARACTER.

Let's get into it.

1. DRESS WELL.

Dress appropriately for the occasion. Taking care of your appearance shows self-respect.

---

2. ALWAYS BE PREPARED.

Carry what you reasonably need, including a payment method for unexpected situations.

---

3. LISTEN WHEN PEOPLE SPEAK.

Make eye contact, pay attention, and don't constantly think about what you'll say next.

---

4. CONTROL YOUR ANGER.

Being angry is normal. Losing control is a choice. Learn to pause before reacting.

---

5. SHOW RESPECT IN GREETINGS.

Stand when appropriate and greet people with confidence and respect.

---

6. PROTECT AND RESPECT OTHERS.

Look out for the people who depend on you, and treat the people beside you with dignity.

---

7. RESPECT INVITATIONS.

If you're not invited, don't force your way into someone's plans.

---

8. NEGOTIATE WITH PATIENCE.

Don't rush to reveal your position. Understand the situation before making an offer.

---

9. DON'T TAKE CREDIT FOR OTHER PEOPLE'S WORK.

Give credit where it belongs. Your reputation is worth more than temporary praise.

---

10. RESPECT YOUR HOST.

If someone welcomes you into their home, show gratitude. Don't insult their food or hospitality.

---

11. DON'T TAKE WHAT ISN'T YOURS.

Even something small should be respected when it belongs to someone else.

---

12. TAKE RESPONSIBILITY AND GIVE CREDIT.

Own your mistakes, and recognize the people who helped create success.

---

13. AIM FOR EXCELLENCE.

Whatever you choose to do, do it with focus and intention. Don't settle for careless work.

---

14. DON'T BEG FOR LOVE.

Healthy relationships require mutual interest, respect, and effort. Don't chase someone who doesn't want to be there.

---

15. DON'T USE ALCOHOL TO DEFINE YOUR IMAGE.

Your character should speak louder than what you hold in your hand.

---

16. PUT YOUR PHONE AWAY DURING MEALS.

Give the people in front of you your attention. Presence is a form of respect.

---

17. SPEAK WITH CLASS.

Good communication isn't about using complicated words. It's about knowing how to express yourself clearly and respectfully.

---

18. MAKE A CONFIDENT GREETING.

A firm, natural handshake and good eye contact can communicate confidence. But always respect personal and cultural boundaries.

---

19. SAY WHAT YOU MEAN.

Be honest, clear, and consistent with your words. Let people know they can trust what you say.

---

20. ASK MORE THAN YOU ANSWER.

Curiosity makes you a better listener and a better learner. You don't have to prove you know everything.
