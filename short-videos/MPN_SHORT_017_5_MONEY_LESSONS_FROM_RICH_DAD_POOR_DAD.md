Today, we're breaking down 5 Money Lessons from Rich Dad Poor Dad.

Let's get into it.

---

1. Learn the Difference Between Assets and Liabilities

An asset can put money into your pocket or build value. A liability costs you money. Understanding the difference can change how you think about spending.

---

2. Don't Depend Only on Your Paycheck

A job can provide income, but your financial life doesn't have to depend on one source. Learn how to save, invest, and build additional sources of income.

---

3. Surround Yourself With People Who Teach You

Spend time with people who know things you don't. Learn from their experience instead of always trying to prove you're the smartest person in the room.

---

4. It's Not Only About How Much You Earn

Making more money doesn't automatically create wealth. What matters is what you keep, how you manage it, and how you use it toward your goals.

---

5. Work to Learn, Not Only to Earn

Every job can teach you something. Build skills, understand how businesses work, improve your communication, and keep learning throughout your life.
