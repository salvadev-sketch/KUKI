Today, we're breaking down 7 Signs You Are a High-Value Individual.

Let's get into it.

---

1. You Can Disagree Respectfully

You don't need to shout to prove your point. Mature people can disagree without disrespect.

---

2. You Keep Your Commitments

Be on time. Keep your promises. Let your actions make people trust you.

---

3. You Don't Judge Too Quickly

You don't measure people by appearance or gossip. You take time to understand them.

---

4. You Encourage Others

You don't feel threatened by someone else's progress. You can celebrate their effort while continuing to improve yourself.

---

5. You Defend People in Their Absence

If you respect someone, you don't destroy their reputation when they're not around. Speak about people with integrity.

---

6. You Don't React to Everything

You may notice everything, but you don't need to respond to everything. Learn when silence is wiser than reaction.

---

7. You Don't Depend on Approval

Someone doubting you doesn't determine your potential. Listen to useful feedback, but keep working toward your goals.
