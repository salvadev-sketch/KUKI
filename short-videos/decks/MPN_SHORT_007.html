20 Rules for Self-Respect

Let's get into it.

RULE 01

Stop Chasing People

If someone isn't interested in you, stop forcing the connection.

Give your energy to people who value it.

RULE 02

Stop Begging

Ask when necessary, but don't repeatedly beg for attention, love, or approval.

Know your worth.

RULE 03

Say What Matters

You don't need to explain everything.

Speak clearly, then let your actions speak.

RULE 04

Address Disrespect

When someone crosses a boundary, don't ignore it.

Stay calm and make your boundary clear.

RULE 05

Don't Take More Than You Give

Healthy relationships need balance.

Be generous, but don't build relationships around taking.

RULE 06

Notice Reciprocity

If you're always the one visiting, calling, or making an effort, step back sometimes.

See whether the effort comes back.

RULE 07

Invest in Yourself

Improve your skills.

Take care of your body.

Build your confidence.

Learn to enjoy your own company.

RULE 08

Stay Away From Gossip

Don't make other people's private lives your entertainment.

What you say about others also reveals your character.

RULE 09

Think Before You Speak

Your words can build respect or destroy it.

Pause.

Think.

Then speak.

RULE 10

Present Yourself Well

Take care of your appearance.

Dress appropriately.

Look after yourself.

How you present yourself affects how people perceive you.

RULE 11

Stay Busy With Your Goals

Have something you're building.

Work toward your goals instead of constantly watching what everyone else is doing.

RULE 12

Respect Your Time

Your time is limited.

Don't give unlimited access to people who constantly waste it.

RULE 13

Don't Stay Where You're Not Respected

Love alone isn't enough.

A healthy relationship needs respect, value, and mutual effort.

RULE 14

Spend on Yourself Wisely

Taking care of yourself isn't selfish.

Use your money responsibly to improve your life, not just to impress others.

RULE 15

Don't Always Be Available

It's okay to have your own life, priorities, and boundaries.

You don't have to respond to everything immediately.

RULE 16

Give Without Keeping Score

Be a giver, but don't let people take advantage of your kindness.

Give freely and maintain boundaries.

RULE 17

Respect Other People's Space

Don't force your presence where it isn't wanted.

When you're invited somewhere, be respectful of the time and space you're given.

RULE 18

Treat People With Respect

Give people the same basic respect you expect from them.

RULE 19

Don't Chase Someone Who Won't Respond

If you've called twice and there's no response, give them space.

If it's important, they can get back to you.

RULE 20

Become Good at What You Do

Build a reputation through competence.

Keep learning.

Keep practicing.

Become someone people can rely on.

Respect yourself.

Respect your time.

Build your value.

And never forget your standards.
