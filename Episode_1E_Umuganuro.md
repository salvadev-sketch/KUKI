# MON PAYS NATAL — ABANTU 100 BAGIZE AMATEKA Y'UBURUNDI
## EPISODE 1E — UMUGANURO: URUGENDO RUHINDUKA UMUHANGO

**Igitigiri:** Episode 1E (ikurikira Nkoma, imbere y'urupfu rwa Ntare)
**Umutwe nyamukuru:** Urugendo Ntare yagenzemwo avuye i Nkoma agana i Bukeye ruhinduka umuhango w'igihugu wamaze imyaka irenga 200
**Ururimi:** Kirundi
**Ibipimo:** 🟢 Ikimenyamenya gikomeye · 🟡 Imigenzo mvugo/disputed · 🔴 Ntibihamye

---

### IGICE 1 — INTANGAMARARA (Bridge kuva kuri Episode 1D)

**[VISUAL: Inzira ivuye ku musozi wa Nkoma igana kure, ikarata igenda yerekana urugendo]**

**NARATION:**

Muri Episode 1D, twabonye ingoma Karyenda ivuka ku Nkoma. Ariko Ntare Rushatsi ntiyaguma ng'aho. Yaragenze, avuye i Nkoma, aciye i Kirwa, agakomereza i Bukeye. Uru rugendo, nk'uko tuzobibona, ntirwaje kuba urugendo rw'umwami umwe gusa — rwahindutse **umuhango w'igihugu cose**.

---

### IGICE 2 — URUGENDO RUHINDUKA UMUGANURO (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata yerekana inzira Nkoma → Kirwa → Bukeye, hamwe n'itariki 1929]**

**NARATION:**

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 46**, hivugwa hati:

> « Le périple initial de Ntare Rushatsi du Nkoma à Bukeye... a ensuite été commémoré chaque année (jusqu'en 1929) par l'itinéraire des délégations se rendant au muganuro. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Urugendo rwa mbere Ntare Rushatsi yaciye avuye i Nkoma agana i Bukeye... rwaciye rwibukwa buri mwaka (gushika 1929) mu nzira intumwa zaciye zigana ku Muganuro."*

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Umuganuro, umuhango w'igihugu w'igiterwa c'ubwoko, ntiwari umuhango uva ahandi — wari **ibuka ry'urugendo nyakuri rwa Ntare Rushatsi ubwiwe**, kandi ryabaye gutyo gushika mu 1929 (igihe ubukoloni bwabuciye).

---

### IGICE 3 — NTARE, UMWUNGERE N'UMUHINZI (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Impfizi ku ruhande rumwe, ibitoke vy'amasaka ku rundi]**

**NARATION:**

Imigenzo yose ihuriza kuri Ntare nk'umuntu ahuza **ubworozi n'uburimyi**. Bamubona ari kumwe n'impfizi yiwe akunda, ariko na none bamwerekana ari umukunzi w'abarimyi, akiri muto, akwegwa n'ibisoro vy'amasaka.

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 46**, hivugwa hati:

> « On le montre... attiré par les épis de sorgho que lui offrent les émissaires de Nsoro. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Bamwerekana... akwegwa n'ibisoro vy'amasaka intumwa za Nsoro zamuhaye."*

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Iyi mice ebiri (umwungere + umuhinzi) ntabwo ari ibisanzwe — Chrétien avuga ko ari **ikimenyetso c'ubutumwa bw'ubwami**: umwami akaba we uhuza ivyo bintu bibiri bikuru vy'ubuzima bw'Uburundi.

---

### IGICE 4 — MUROGANZARA (🟡, isubiramwo)

**NARATION:**

Nk'uko twabibonye muri Episode 1A, Ntare yagize izina ryitiriwe: **Muroganzara**, "uwucisha inzara." Inkuru yakusanyijwe na Jan Vansina mu 1958 ivuga ko yishe amatungo yiwe ashika i Nkoma, imyanda yayo ikoreshwa nk'ifumbire kugira ngo bimere amasaka.

🟡 Iyi ni imigenzo, ariko irerekana insanamu y'ubwami budasanzwe: ubwami buhuza ibintu bibiri bisa n'ubuhemu (ubuhinzi/uburimyi vs ubworozi).

---

### IGICE 5 — IBIBANZA VY'UMUGANURO (🟢🟡)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata yerekana ibibanza vyinshi: Nkoma, Magamba, Kirwa, Gikonge]**

**NARATION:**

Umuganuro ntiwabera ahantu hamwe gusa. Chrétien avuga ko wari umuhango ukwiragiye ku bibanza vyinshi vy'igihugu — abaganuza (abakuru b'imihango) i Nkoma, Magamba, Kirwa na Gikonge, hamwe n'imiryango yashinzwe gutanga ingoma nshasha zo gukubita mu muhango.

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 24**, hivugwa hati:

> « La célébration du muganuro se traduisait... par une constellation de sites. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Umuhango w'Umuganuro... wabereye ku bibanza vyinshi bihuriranye."*

🟡 Ibi bibanza vyose (Ngomante, Giheko, Kumana de Banege, Kayove, Maganahe — aho Ntare yaciye mu rugendo rwiwe) ni ibimenyetso vy'imigenzo, atari ivyacukuwe mu vy'ubucukuzi.

---

### IGICE 6 — UBUTUMWA BW'AKARERE KOSE (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata y'akarere k'Ibiyaga Bigari yerekana ibihugu bitandukanye, ikinjana ca 17]**

**NARATION:**

Ntare Rushatsi ntiyavukiye mu busa. Chrétien avuga ko ikinjana ca 17 cari igihe c'impinduka nyinshi mu karere kose k'Ibiyaga Bigari: ubwami bwo ku bibaya vyo hasi (Bugesera, Buha) bwaragoyeka, mu gihe ubushobozi bwashinze ku bibaya vyo hejuru (nk'i Burundi) bwarushaho gukomera — kubera impinduka z'ikirere n'uburimyi.

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Ubwami bwa Ntare bwavutse mu gihe kimwe n'ubwami bwa **Ruganzu Ndori** mu Rwanda — bombi bavutse mu kinjana ca 17, bombi bimukira ku bibaya vyo hejuru. Iki ni ikintu Chrétien yishimikije nk'igishushanyo cerekana ikintu kimwe kibaho mu karere kose, atari Uburundi bwonyene.

---

### IGICE 7 — NTARE RUGAMBA: UWUNDI NTARE (🟢, isubiramwo/insobanuro)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata y'Uburundi ikwira, imyaka 1800-1850 igaragara]**

**NARATION:**

Hano tugomba kwitondera ikintu gikomeye: **imyaka ijana inyuma ya Ntare Rushatsi, undi mwami aronka Karyenda, akitwa na we Ntare — Ntare Rugamba**, azwi kandi ku mazina Rutanganwa canke Ruyenzi.

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 47**, hivugwa hati:

> « Un siècle après Ntare Rushatsi, un second mwami... hérite du tambour sous le nom de Ntare. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Imyaka ijana inyuma ya Ntare Rushatsi, undi mwami... aronka ingoma yitwa Ntare."*

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Ntare Rugamba (1800-1850) ni we yagize Uburundi bugaba kabiri ubunini bwabwo, atwara Bweru, Buyogoma, Bugufi, n'Imbo. **Ni umuntu WUNDI, atari Ntare Rushatsi** — ni ikintu dukwiye kwitondera cane kugira ntidusubire kuvanga inkuru zabo (nk'uko twari twabihamagajweko mu ntango y'ubushakashatsi bwacu).

---

### IGICE 8 — IMPERA (Bridge kuri Episode 1F)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata y'imva z'abami, Budandari, hamwe n'amazina y'abami bane]**

**NARATION:**

Umurage wa Ntare Rushatsi ntiwagumye ku Muganuro gusa. Igihe cageze, na we yagombye gupfa — kandi imva ziwe n'iz'abamukurikiye zaje kuba ahantu heranda, kuva Budandari (aho abami bane ba mbere baraye) gushika ku bindi bibanza bikoreshwa n'abami bakurikiranye.

Muri Episode 1F, tuzoraba **urupfu rwa Ntare Rushatsi n'umurage wiwe wa nyuma** — imbere yo kuja ku bandi bami bakurikiranye.
