# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP04 — INTAMBARA NA RWANDA

Mu bami b'Uburundi Ntare Rugamba yahanganye na bo, hari babiri b'ingenzi, kandi bafitaniye isano n'u Rwanda:

**Gahindiro.**

**Rwogera.**

---

## Ukwihora

Imigenzo ivuga ko izo ntambara zari zifise intumbero idasanzwe: si ugukura ubutaka gusa.

Ntare Rugamba yashaka **kwihora** — guhora abamubanjirije ku ntsinzi u Rwanda rwari rwaronse ku Burundi mu bihe vyo hambere.

Iyi ni insobanuro ikomeye: ubwami, ku ntwaro yabwo, ntibuja imbere gusa. Rimwe na rimwe, buja inyuma, burondera guhora ivyaco vya kera.

---

## Yuhi Gahindiro

**Yuhi IV Gahindiro** (yategetse hafi ya 1746–1802) yari umwami w'u Rwanda mu ntango z'ingoma ya Ntare Rugamba ubwayo — Gahindiro yaguye ku ngoma mu 1802, ni ukuvuga ko amakimbirane hagati yiwe na Ntare Rugamba yabaye mu **ntango cane z'ingoma ya Ntare**, hafi hafi y'igihe kimwe n'intambara ya Bugesera (1796–1801) twize muri EP03.

## Rwogera

**Mutara II Rwogera** (yategetse hafi ya 1802–1853), uwundi mwami w'u Rwanda, yakurikiye Gahindiro. Amakimbirane na we yagumye ari ay'igihe kirekire kurusha ayo na Gahindiro — agakwira imyaka myinshi y'ingoma ya Ntare Rugamba, birashoboka ko yakomeje gushika no mu gihe c'ibitero vya Buyogoma na Bugufi twize muri EP03.

Intambara na Gahindiro na Rwogera zombi zifise, mu magambo y'umwe mu bahinga biyandikiye, "intumbero ibiri: iya politike, n'iy'igihugu" — ni ukuvuga zari zifitaniye isano n'ukwihora, ariko na none n'ivyifuzo vy'ukwagura no gukomeza ubwami.

---

## Cohoha na Shinge na Rugero

Hari amasoko avuga ko mu ntango z'ingoma ya Ntare Rugamba, habaye ibitero hagati y'ingabo z'Uburundi n'iz'u Rwanda hafi y'ikiyaga **Cohoha**, mu karere ka Kirundo.

Muri ako karere nyene hari imisozi ibiri izwi cane: **Shinge** na **Rugero**. Ntabwo ari abantu — ni imisozi, iri hafi y'igisagara ca Kirundo, mu karere ka Bugesera.

Imigenzo ivuga ko intambara ikomeye yabereye ng'aho, ingabo z'Uburundi zigabanijwe mu migwi itatu, umwe uhishe inyuma ya Rugero, uwundi hagati y'imisozi yombi, n'uwundi uzengurutse inyuma y'abanyarwanda — maze abarwanyi b'u Rwanda baratsindwa cane. Iyo ntsinzi yaje kuvamwo imvugo ikoreshwa gushika none: **"kubura Shinge na Rugero"** — ni ukuvuga gutsindwa bikomeye, kubura aho uhagarara.

Ariko hari amakuru abiri tugomba kuvuga uko ari: amasoko atanga imyaka ibiri itandukanye ku ntambara y'i Shinge na Rugero — **1763** na **1775**. Kandi amasoko atandukanya n'umwami wa Burundi wagabye izo ntambara: amwe avuga **Mutaga Senyamwiza** (uwo twize muri Season 1, EP15), ayandi akavuga **Ntare Rugamba**. N'umwami wo mu Rwanda na we aratandukana: Yuhi Gahindiro ku bamwe, Yuhi III Mazimpaka ku bandi.

Ntitwohitamwo umwami umwe tutabanje kugira gihamya ikomeye. Tuzogumya kuvuga: 🟡 **Shinge na Rugero ni ahantu h'intambara zikomeye hagati y'Uburundi n'u Rwanda, kandi amasoko atandukanye ayihuza n'abami batandukanye, harimwo Mutaga Senyamwiza na Ntare Rugamba.**

---

## Kinyaga: intsinzi idahoraho

Imigenzo n'ubushakashatsi vyerekana ko ingabo z'Uburundi zinjiye mu bice vya **Kinyaga**, ku nkengera z'ikiyaga Kivu.

Ariko dutegerezwa gushira umurongo utukura, nk'uko twabivuze kera: kwinjira mu karere ntibisobanura kugenzura burundu.

David Newbury avuga ko Ntare Rugamba yagize ubushobozi bwo kwinjira mu turere twinshi, ariko ntabwo yagize ubushobozi bwo kubugenzura bwose ku rugero rumwe.

---

## Ico dushobora kuvuga

Ntare Rugamba yaragiranye intambara na Gahindiro na Rwogera, biciye ku mpamvu y'ukwihora ku ntsinzi za kera. Ingabo z'Uburundi zinjiye mu bice vya Kinyaga. Ariko ubutegetsi buramba mu bice vyose ntibwashinzwe.

Ntitwovuga "Ntare Rugamba yigaruriye u Rwanda." Tuvuga: **yaragiranye intambara zikomeye na Gahindiro na Rwogera, akinjira mu bice vya Kinyaga, ariko ata butegetsi buramba bwashinzwe mu bice vyose yashikako.**

---
