# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP06 — ABAHUNGU N'UBUTEGETSI

Uburundi bwarakuze cane muri ingoma ya Ntare Rugamba.

Ariko igihugu kinini kirasaba abantu bo kukigenzura.

Ntare Rugamba yaronse inyishu: abahungu biwe ubwabo.

---

## Umuryango munini

Ntare Rugamba yarongoye abagore cumi na umwe.

Muri abo, yavyaye abana benshi. Ariko mu bahungu biwe bose, hari batanu imigenzo yagumije nk'ab'ingenzi mu mateka y'Uburundi:

**Ndivyariye.**

**Rwasha** — uwo twaramaze kubona, ari we yamufashije gutsinda Ubuyogoma.

**Birori.**

**Busumano.**

**Gisabo** — uwo, nyuma y'urupfu rwa se, azoba umwami, azitwa **Mwezi IV Gisabo**.

---

## Ndivyariye: umwungere w'iherezo ributse

Ndivyariye ni we mukuru muri bo.

Imigenzo ivuga ko ashinzwe kuba **umwungere** w'uwomusubira, mushiki wiwe Mwezi Gisabo, igihe uyu yari akiri muto.

Ariko iherezo rya Ndivyariye ryagumye rizwi cane kubera urupfu rwiwe, atari ubuzima bwiwe: imigenzo ivuga ko yanigiwe.

Ntitwovuga ngo turazi neza uwamwishe canke igituma. Ni imigenzo. Ariko yerekana ko imbere y'uko ingoma yiherereza ku muhungu umwe, hari amakimbirane akomeye hagati y'abandi.

---

## Gutanga uturere

Ntare Rugamba yashize abo bahungu bake mu turere dutandukanye, kugira babe abatware, abategetsi b'agace.

Iki gikorwa cafashije kwagura ababasha bw'ingoma: umwami akiri kure, ariko abahungu be, bemewe n'izina ryiwe, bakagenzura uturere yari amaze gutsinda.

---

## Basigaba

Icarimwe, yashinze uwundi murongo w'ubutegetsi: **basigaba** — abakuru b'uturere, bagashirwa munsi y'ubutegetsi bukuru, biteguye uburyo bwo gukoranya imisoro no gucungera ubutungane.

Amasoko menshi yemeza ko iyi nzego yakomejwe cane mu ngoma ya Ntare Rugamba, ituma ubutegetsi bwo hagati bugira uburinzi bukomeye ku turere twagutse.

---

## Paradox ya Newbury

Ariko David Newbury atubwira ikintu gikomeye, agikoresha mu buryo butomoye cane:

Ntare yari afise ubushobozi bwo gushira abahungu biwe mu turere.

Ariko ntiyari afise ubushobozi buhagije bwo kubinjiza bose mu butegetsi bukomeye bwo hagati.

### Iyo ni paradox nziza cane:

**Ntare Rugamba yubatse ubwami bunini → ariko ababasha yahaye abahungu biwe bazotuma habaho uguhiganwa nyuma yiwe.**

---

Aha ni ho urugendo rwa Ntare Rugamba ruberekana rudasanzwe: intambwe yamugejeje ku kuguka kw'ubwami ni yo yazoba intambwe izobera ibibazo abamukurikira — nk'uko turabibona muri EP09.

Ariko imbere yo kubandanya kuri ico kibazo, hari ikindi cane Ntare Rugamba yakoze, kijanye n'uburyo bw'ubutegetsi bwo hagati ubwabwo.
