# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP06 — BUGUFI NA FUMBIJE

Igitero ca nyuma Ntare Rugamba yagize kirimwo inkuru ebyiri z'abagabo babiri batumva ku bwami bwiwe.

Umwe yishwe n'umuvyeyi we.

Uwundi yishwe n'ikizinga.

---

## Fumbije

Inyuma y'intambara ya Bugesera, Ntare Rugamba yashinze umutware witwa **Fumbije**, amuha kuganza akarere ka **Bweru** hamwe n'igice ca Bugesera.

Yaramwizigiye cane: yamuhaye n'umukobwa wiwe mu bugore.

Ariko Fumbije yaje kwigomeka.

Yaretse kwiyereka ku kirimba. Yaranse gutumizwa n'umwami. Yiciye abatumwa umwami yamurungikiye.

Ntare Rugamba aca arungika umugabo umwe, uvuye i **Kumoso**, akorera umwami, ajanye **ikizinga**.

Fumbije yicaye ikizinga ku kuboko.

Aca aratumbagira, aracika, arapfa.

Iyi ni imwe mu nkuru zizwi cane, ariko ni imigenzo — ntitwoyifata nk'inyandiko y'ico gihe yemeza uko byagenze mu buryo bunononsoye.

---

## Baramba na Rugeyo

Ubugufi (uzwi na none nka Buhangaza), ku buraruko bw'Uburundi, hagati y'imigezi Ruvubu na Kagera, bwari bwarahawe kera n'umwami Ntare Rushatsi, agahabwa umukozi wiwe witwa **Mpehe**.

Mu ngoma ya Ntare Rugamba, umwami wo muri ako karere, **Baramba**, yigometse, anyaga n'inka z'ubwami.

Ntare Rugamba yaramwishe.

Umuhungu wa Baramba, **Rugeyo**, yahungiye ku bwami bw'u Rwanda. Amaherezo asubira mu Burundi, aza kuboneka, anicwa.

Ubugufi bwomekwa ku Burundi mu **1850** — umwaka Ntare Rugamba yagiye apfa.

Ariko iki gice cagiye gusubira gutandukanywa n'Uburundi: mu bihe vy'ubukoloni, Ababiligi barakigabanije, none kikaba kiri muri Tanzaniya.

---

## Rucebera

Hari izina rimwe rigaragara riherekeje iyi nkuru: **Rucebera**, uvuye i Bugufi.

Ariko uruhara rwiwe nyawo mu nkuru ntirurasobanurwa neza n'amasoko dufise. Duzokwibutswa aha, atari kwinjiza kandi mu nkuru.

---

## Kumoso

Twaramaze kubona ko umugabo yagejeje ikizinga kuri Fumbije yavuye i **Kumoso**. Amasoko ahuza ukwaguka kwa Ntare Rugamba na Kumoso, avuga ko cinjijwe mu Burundi hafi y'imyaka ya 1840 — igihe kimwe na Buyogoma. Ariko izina n'imbibe vya Kumoso vyagiye bihinduka uko ibihe biraheruka, ku buryo tutashobora gushira umurongo ukomeye ku mbibe zaco.

---

Aba bantu batatu — Fumbije, Baramba, Rugeyo — bose bagaragaza ikintu kimwe: ubwami bwa Ntare Rugamba, naho bwaguka, ntibwari bworoshe kubugenzura. Abatware yizigira, canke abo yatsinze, bashobora kwigomeka, kandi umwami akabakoresha uburyo butandukanye kugira ngo abagarure munsi y'ubutegetsi bwiwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP07 — IBIKANGA**, ni ho tuzoraba ingabo z'igihe cose Ntare Rugamba yashizeho, kugira ngo ata mutware yongere kwigomeka nk'uko Fumbije na Baramba babigenje.
