# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP11 — BATARE NA BEZI

Twarabibonye kera: Ntare Rugamba yashize abahungu biwe mu turere, kugira babe abategetsi.

Ico gikorwa cafashije kwagura ubwami.

Ariko ico gikorwa nyene ni co kizoba intango y'ikibazo gikomeye, inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe.

---

Uwamusubiriye ku ngoma ni **Mwezi IV Gisabo**, umwe mu bahungu biwe.

Ariko Mwezi Gisabo ntiyari we wenyene.

Hari abandi bavukana, bo mu bandi bahungu ba Ntare Rugamba, na bo bafise ubushobozi, abantu, amatungo, n'uturere twahawe na se.

---

Inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntare Rugamba, ubutegetsi ntibwahise buhagarara ku muntu umwe atagira ivyifuzo bindi.

Haciye habaho ihiganwa rikomeye, riva mu mashami abiri:

**Batare** — abakomoka kuri Ntare Rugamba.

**Bezi** — abakomoka kuri Mwezi Gisabo.

---

Jean-Pierre Chrétien yerekana ko ayo mashami yombi yabaye amasoko akomeye y'ubutegetsi mu Burundi bwo mu bihe vyakurikiye ingoma ya Ntare Rugamba.

Newbury na we avuga ko inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntare Rugamba habaye ihiganwa rikomeye hagati y'iyo mirongo yombi, cane cane mu gihe ca Mwezi Gisabo.

---

Aha nidusubira ku gitekerezo twabonye muri EP06:

Ntare Rugamba yari afise ubushobozi bwo gushira abahungu biwe mu turere.

Ariko ntiyari afise ubushobozi buhagije bwo kubinjiza bose mu butegetsi bumwe, bukomeye, bwo hagati.

Iryo tandukaniro, iyo aricyo cari intambwe y'ukwagura ubwami, ni na ryo rizoba intango y'amakimbirane azogumaho mu myaka myinshi izoza — kugeza n'igihe c'ubukoloni.

---

Ntitwovuga ngo Ntare Rugamba ni we yagize icaha c'ivyo bibazo. Ubutegetsi bugutse, ku ntwaro y'igihe, buri bwose bufise ico kibazo: ni gute ushira uburinzi ku bantu badashobora kugenzurwa n'ubutegetsi bukuru, ata gukura mu bushobozi bwo kugumya ubwo bwami bumwe?

Ntare Rugamba yaronse inyishu ku kibazo c'ukwagura.

Ariko ntiyaronse inyishu ku kibazo c'uwomusubira.

---

Ubu, twaramaze kubona ubwami bwiwe bwose: ukwagura, ibikanga, abahungu, ubugabire, Gishora, hamwe n'igitangira ry'ihiganwa rya Batare na Bezi.

Rasigaye ikibazo kimwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP12 — ISOZERO**, ni ho tuzoraba urupfu rwiwe, aho yahambwe, n'umurage yasigiye Uburundi — umurage udasanzwe, ufise ubugari n'ibibazo icarimwe.
