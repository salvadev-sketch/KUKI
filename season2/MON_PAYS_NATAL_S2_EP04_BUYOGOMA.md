# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP04 — BUYOGOMA

Intambara zimwe zitangurira ku bibazo binini: ubutegetsi, imbibe, ubutunzi.

Iyi ntambara yatanguriye ku kintu gito: umukobwa umwe.

---

## Ruhaga w'i Buyungu

Buyogoma yari ifise umwami wayo, **Ruhaga**, umwami w'ubwami bwitwa **Buyungu**, bwo mu buraruko bw'Ubuha.

Imigenzo ivuga ko Ntare Rugamba yasavye Ruhaga umukobwa wiwe, **Jururyakugwa**, ngo amurongore.

Ariko Ruhaga ntiyamwemeye uwo mukobwa. Yarungitse uwundi mukobwa, **Gikore**, ata co ari we Ntare yari yasavye.

---

## Insuzugu

Ntare Rugamba yabonye iryo nka insimburano y'akantu gato — ariko yari afise insobanuro ikomeye: ugusuzugura mu buryo bw'umwami.

Mu bwami bw'ico gihe, kwanga guha umwami umukobwa yasavye, ukamurungikira uwundi mu mwanya wiwe, wari umugambi w'ubwenge n'ugusuzugura icarimwe.

---

## Intambara

Aca atera Ubuyogoma, afashijwe n'umuhungu wiwe **Rwasha**.

Hagati ya 1840, Ubuyogoma bwomekwa ku Burundi.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Nduwamahoro (thesis) avuga ko "Ntare Rugamba yabaye umwami akomeye cane... yarigaruriye Ubuyogoma, Ubuha, igice kinini c'Ubugesera n'Obweru" — urutonde ruhuza Ubuyogoma n'ibindi bihugu Rugamba yaguye.

---

## Inyuma y'intambara: ukwimuka n'ukubaka

Ariko ukwaguka muri Buyogoma ntikwabaye intambara gusa.

Ubushakashatsi bwerekana ko harabaye **ukwimuka kw'abantu, ugushiraho abatware bashasha, no gukoresha ubutaka bushasha**. Imiryango ya kera yo muri ako karere — **Abakundo** na **Abashoka** — biyunga n'umwami mushasha, biyongera ku bushobozi bw'ingoma.

Ibi bituma tubona ikintu gikomeye: **Ntare Rugamba ntiyigaruriye ubutaka gusa. Yashizeho uburyo bwo kubucungera no kubutuzamwo abantu.**
