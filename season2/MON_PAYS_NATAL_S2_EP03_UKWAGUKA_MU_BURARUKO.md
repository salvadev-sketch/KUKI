# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP03 — UKWAGUKA MU BURARUKO

Uburundi Ntare Rugamba yaronse igihe yaja ku ngoma ntibwari nk'uko bumeze none.

Bwari nk'igice c'ubunini bw'Uburundi bw'ubu gusa.

Imbere y'uko ingoma ye irangira, ubwo bwami buzoba bwakubye kabiri.

Iyi episode ni yo iraba, umwe umwe, uturere yigaruriye — n'inkuru zibiherekeje.

---

## Bugesera: intango y'intambara (1796–1801)

Mu ntango z'ingoma yiwe, Ntare Rugamba ahangana na **Nsoro Nyabarega**, umwami w'u Bugesera, igihugu cari cimaze gucika intege mu ntango z'ikinjana ca 19.

Amasoko amwe atanga igihe kigereranijwe: intambara hagati ya Rugamba na Nyabarega zaba zarabaye hagati ya **1796 na 1801**.

Ntare arinjira mu bumanuko bwa Bugesera, arondera inka n'ivyatsi vy'amatungo ku bwami bwiwe bugutse. Ingabo z'Uburundi ziratsinda, ubwo butaka bugaca bwomekwa ku Burundi.

Icarimwe, abarwozi bo mu Rwanda barinjira mu bindi bice vya Bugesera bavuye mu buraruko, batagize akarusho gakomeye. Ni co gituma ubwami bwa Bugesera bwaje kugabanywa hagati y'Uburundi n'u Rwanda.

---

## Buyogoma: intambara y'urukundo rwanse (~1840)

Iyi ni imwe mu nkuru zishimishije kuri iyi documentaire, kuko itwereka ukuntu ibintu bito bishobora gutera intambara nini.

Buyogoma yari ifise umwami wayo, **Ruhaga**, umwami w'ubwami bwitwa **Buyungu**, bwo mu buraruko bw'Ubuha.

Imigenzo ivuga ko Ntare Rugamba yasavye Ruhaga umukobwa wiwe, **Jururyakugwa**, ngo amurongore. Ariko Ruhaga ntiyamwemeye uwo mukobwa. Yarungitse uwundi mukobwa, **Gikore**, ata co ari we Ntare yari yasavye.

Ntare Rugamba yabonye iryo nka insimburano y'akantu gato — ariko yari afise insobanuro ikomeye: ugusuzugura mu buryo bw'umwami.

Aca atera Ubuyogoma, afashijwe n'umuhungu wiwe **Rwasha**. Hagati ya 1840, Ubuyogoma bwomekwa ku Burundi. Imiryango ya kera yo muri ako karere — **Abakundo** na **Abashoka** — biyunga n'umwami mushasha, biyongera ku bushobozi bw'ingoma.

---

## Imbo: igitero c'umupfumu

Akarere ka **Imbo** na ko karigaruriwe, mu gitero imigenzo ivuga ko cerekanye ubutwari bw'umwami.

Imigenzo ivuga ko Ntare yakoresheje impanuro z'umupfumu witwa **Ndwano ya Runyota rwa Nyamigogo**, uwo bita "iturufu" ya Ntare Rugamba — ni ukuvuga ikintu Ntare yiyeguriyeko cane mu bihe bikomeye.

---

## Bugufi: igitero ca nyuma (1850)

Igitero ca nyuma Ntare Rugamba yagize ni ico kwomeka **Ubugufi** (izwi na none nka Buhangaza), ku buraruko bw'Uburundi, hagati y'imigezi Ruvubu na Kagera.

Ubwo bwami bwari bwarahawe kera n'umwami Ntare Rushatsi, agahabwa umukozi wiwe witwa **Mpehe**, kugira ngo abutunganye.

Mu ngoma ya Ntare Rugamba, umwami wo muri ako karere, **Baramba**, yigometse, anyaga n'inka z'ubwami. Ntare Rugamba yaramwishe. Umuhungu wa Baramba, **Rugeyo**, yahungiye ku bwami bw'u Rwanda, hanyuma amaherezo asubira mu Burundi, aza kuboneka, anicwa.

Ubugufi bwomekwa ku Burundi mu 1850 — umwaka Ntare Rugamba yagiye apfa.

Ariko iki gice cagiye gusubira gutandukanywa n'Uburundi: mu bihe vy'ubukoloni, Ababiligi barakigabanije, none kikaba kiri muri Tanzaniya.

---

## Ubugari bwaguwe

Amaherezo, Uburundi bwakubye kabiri ubunini bwabwo bwa mbere. Newbury na benshi mu bahinga bemeranya kuri iyi ngingo.

Ariko naho hari uturere twinjijwe, ubutegetsi bwo hagati ntibwashoboye kwinjira mu bice vyose ku rugero rumwe — nk'uko tuzobibona mu gice gikurikira.
