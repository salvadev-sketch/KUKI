# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP05 — INTAMBARA NA RWANDA

Mu bami b'u Rwanda Ntare Rugamba yahanganye na bo, hari babiri b'ingenzi:

**Gahindiro.**

**Rwogera.**

---

## Ukwihora

Imigenzo ivuga ko izo ntambara zari zifise intumbero idasanzwe: si ugukura ubutaka gusa.

Ntare Rugamba yashaka **kwihora** — guhora abamubanjirije ku ntsinzi u Rwanda rwari rwaronse ku Burundi mu bihe vyo hambere.

## Yuhi Gahindiro na Mutara Rwogera

**Yuhi IV Gahindiro** yaguye ku ngoma mu 1802 — amakimbirane hagati yiwe na Ntare Rugamba yabaye mu ntango cane z'ingoma ya Ntare, hafi hafi y'igihe kimwe n'intambara ya Bugesera.

**Mutara II Rwogera** (1802–1853) yakurikiye Gahindiro. Amakimbirane na we yagumye ari ay'igihe kirekire kurusha ayo na Gahindiro.

---

## Shinge na Rugero: intambara ya Kirundo

Hafi y'ikiyaga **Cohoha**, mu karere ka Kirundo, hari imisozi ibiri izwi cane: **Shinge** na **Rugero**.

Imigenzo ivuga ko ingabo z'Uburundi zubatse **umurongo uva ku Rusizi ugashika i Bugesera**. Ariko Abanyarwanda, babwiwe n'umutumwa w'ibanga, barategura umutego: bareka Abarundi binjira imbere mu gihugu, hanyuma bakabatera i **Butare**, babagose. Imigenzo y'u Rwanda ivuga ko ata n'umwe muri abo Barundi wagumye ari muzima.

Iyi ntsinzi, muri iyo migenzo nyene, yaje "gusiba intsinzi ya Kirundo" — ni ukuvuga ko yaje guhora ku ntsinzi Uburundi bwari bwaronse hambere ng'aho.

Iyo ntambara nyene niyo yavamwo imvugo ikoreshwa gushika none: **"kubura Shinge na Rugero"** — gutsindwa bikomeye, kubura aho uhagarara.

---

## Umurongo utukura ukomeye

Aha hari ibintu bibiri tudakwiye kwibagirwa:

**Ubwa mbere**: iyi nkuru y'intambara ya Kirundo/Shinge na Rugero, uko iri hejuru, ivuye ahanini mu **migenzo y'u Rwanda**. Uburundi na bwo bufise ivyo bwibuka ku vyerekeye izo ntambara, ariko ntibisa n'ivy'u Rwanda. Aho amasoko abiri atandukana, ntitwohitamwo iryo tubona ryiza kurusha irindi.

**Ubwa kabiri**: amasoko ntahurira ku mwami umwe wagabye izo ntambara canke igihe zabaye. Amwe atanga imyaka **1763** na **1775**. Amwe avuga ko ari **Mutaga Senyamwiza** (uwo twize muri Season 1, EP15), ayandi akavuga **Ntare Rugamba**.

Ntitwohitamwo umwami umwe tutabanje kugira gihamya ikomeye. Tuzogumya kuvuga: 🟡 **Shinge na Rugero ni ahantu h'intambara zikomeye hagati y'Uburundi n'u Rwanda, kandi amasoko atandukanye ayihuza n'abami batandukanye.**

---

## Kinyaga: intsinzi idahoraho

Amasoko y'amateka y'u Rwanda avuga ko Ntare yateye mu bice vya **Nyiginya**, agera i **Kinyaga**.

Ariko amwe muri ayo masoko avuga ikintu gikomeye: **ingabo z'Uburundi zarasubiye inyuma, inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntare Rugamba.**

Ntitwovuga "Kinyaga yose yabaye intara y'Uburundi igihe cose." Tuvuga: **Ntare Rugamba yaragabye ibitero mu karere ka Kinyaga, ariko ubwo butegetsi ntibwagumye inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe.**

---

Intambara zonyene ntizasobanura vyose. Ntare Rugamba yari akeneye uburyo bwo kugumya ivyo yaronse — harimwo n'uturere twinshi twari twaramaze kumurwanya.
