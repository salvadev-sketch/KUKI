# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP10 — ISOZERO

Twamukurikiranye kuva ku bwana bwiwe, ubwo yitwaga Ngambiri, agakura munsi ya regency.

Twaramubonye atsinda Ntibirangwa, ashinga Gishora.

Twaramubonye aguza ubwami mu buraruko, atsinda i Bugufi na Buyogoma, ashika mu bice vya Rusizi.

Twaramubonye ahangana n'u Rwanda, tukamenya ko intsinzi zonyene zitasobanuraga ubutegetsi buramba.

Twaramubonye ashira ibikanga, ashira abahungu biwe mu turere, akomeza ubugabire.

Ariko none, dutegerezwa kubaza ikibazo kizosoza urugendo rwiwe:

**Ntare Rugamba yapfuye ryari, kandi ahantu hahe?**

---

## Yapfuye ryari?

Chronology ikoreshwa cane n'abashakashatsi imushira hafi ya **1796–1850** — 1796 nk'igihe kigereranijwe c'ivuka ryiwe, 1850 nk'urupfu rwiwe.

Ariko hari amasoko amwe avuga **1852**, andi agatanga chronology itandukanye rwose.

Rero kuri iyi documentaire: **Ntare Rugamba yategetse mu gice ca mbere c'ikinjana ca 19, kandi urupfu rwiwe rusanzwe rushirwa hafi ya 1850; hari amasoko avuga 1852.**

Ntitwohitamwo imwe muri izo, kubera ko nta gihamya gihagije gitandukanya ukuri hagati yazo.

---

## Yahambwe hehe?

Kugeza ubu, nta soko rikomeye twaronse riduha imva yiwe mu buryo bwizewe cane.

Ntitugerageza gukeka.

**🔴 Aho Ntare Rugamba yahambwe: ntiturabifunga.**

Umwami yaguye ubwami bunini kuruta uko bwari bwigeze kuba imbere yiwe, ariko urupfu rwiwe rwaguma, nk'urw'abamubanjirije bose, mu mwijima w'ibisigo bitarabika ahantu heza.

---

## Umurage

Naho hari byinshi tutazi, dushobora kuvuga bimwe ku murage wiwe, ata guhimba:

Yaguye ubwami bukubye inshuro ebyiri.

Yashinze Gishora, ahantu tugishobora kwerekana none.

Yashizeho ibikanga, ingabo z'igihe cose.

Yashize abahungu biwe mu turere, agatuma ubwami buja hafi y'abantu — ariko ico gikorwa nyene ni co kizoba intango y'ihiganwa rya Batare na Bezi, ryogira uruhara mu bihe biza.

---

## Ntare batatu, na none

Nk'uko twabigenje kuri Ntare Rushatsi muri Season 1, reka tuvuge iki gitekerezo na none:

Hari **Ntare Rugamba w'imigenzo** — "second founder," uwaguye ubwami, umwami w'intambara n'ubukerebutsi.

Hari **Ntare Rugamba w'amateka** — uwo dufise amakuru arambuye kuri we, kuruta uko twari twabigize ku bandi bose babanjirije, ariko dukirekura vyinshi: itariki nyakuri y'urupfu rwiwe, aho yahambwe, n'imbibe nyazo z'ubutegetsi bwiwe.

Ntabwo dufise, kuri we, "Ntare w'imigani" nk'uko dwari dufise kuri Ntare Rushatsi — kuko inkuru ye ivuye mu bushakashatsi bw'amateka kurusha imigenzo mvugo.
