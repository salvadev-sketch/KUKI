# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP12 — ISOZERO

Twamukurikiranye kuva ku bwana bwiwe, ubwo yitwaga Ngambiri.

Twaramubonye atsinda Ntibirangwa, ashinga Gishora.

Twaramubonye aguza ubwami: Bugesera, Buyogoma, Intambara na Rwanda, Bugufi na Fumbije.

Twaramubonye ashira ibikanga, ashira abahungu biwe mu turere, akomeza ubugabire.

Ariko none, dutegerezwa kubaza ikibazo kizosoza urugendo rwiwe: **Ntare Rugamba yapfuye gute, kandi ahantu hahe?**

---

## Mugera: iherezo ry'intambara

Inyuma y'imyaka irenga mirongo itanu (50) y'intambara n'ukwagura, Ntare Rugamba yarushye.

Imigenzo ivuga ko yasubiye inyuma y'ubuzima bw'intambara, akaja kuba mu **rugo rwa Mugera**.

Ng'aho, mu myaka yiwe ya nyuma, yarongoye **Vyano** — uwo azoba nyina wa uwomusubira ku ngoma, **Mwezi Gisabo**.

Ibi bivuze ko Mwezi Gisabo yavutse mu myaka ya nyuma y'ubuzima bwa se, mu gihe Ntare Rugamba yari amaze gusubira inyuma y'intambara.

---

## Indwara

Imigenzo ivuga ko Ntare Rugamba yaguye ku ndwara y'uruhu izwi nka **pian** (mu kinyamerika: *yaws*), indwara yandura biciye ku ruhu, ikwirakwira mu turere dushyuha two mu Afrika.

Ntitwovuga ngo iyi ni yo mpamvu yonyene y'urupfu rwiwe. Ariko ni yo mvyerekano iboneka mu bushakashatsi bw'amateka.

---

## Buruhukiro

Kandi none dufise ikintu tutari dufise imbere: ahantu bavuga ko Ntare Rugamba yahambwe.

**Buruhukiro**, hafi y'umupaka w'u Rwanda.

Iri zina ryonyene rifise insobanuro ikomeye: "Buruhukiro" rifitaniye isano n'ijambo "kuruhuka" — ni nk'ahantu h'ipfundikizo, h'iruhuko rya nyuma.

Ariko turasaba ubwitonzi: iri zina ry'ahantu rishobora no kuba ryarahawe iyo mva **kubera** ko ari ho umwami yaruhukiye, aho kuba ari ryo ryaho hambere. Ntitwoshira iyi nkuru ku rugero rumwe n'ivyemejwe n'ubucukuzi bw'ivyabaye.

---

## Ryari?

Urutonde rw'ibihe rukoreshwa cane n'abashakashatsi rumushira hafi ya **1796–1850**. Ariko hari amasoko amwe avuga **1852**.

Ntitwohitamwo imwe muri izo, kubera ko nta gihamya gihagije gitandukanya ukuri hagati yazo.

---

## Umurage

Naho hari byinshi tutazi, dushobora kuvuga bimwe ku murage wiwe, ata guhimba:

Yaguye ubwami bukubye inshuro ebyiri.

Yashinze Gishora, ahantu tugishobora kwerekana none.

Yashizeho ibikanga, ingabo z'igihe cose.

Yashize abahungu biwe mu turere, agatuma ubwami buja hafi y'abantu — ariko ico gikorwa nyene ni co kizoba intango y'ihiganwa rya Batare na Bezi, ryogira uruhara mu bihe biza.

---

## Ntare batatu, na none

Nk'uko twabigenje kuri Ntare Rushatsi muri Season 1:

Hari **Ntare Rugamba w'imigenzo** — "second founder," uwaguye ubwami, umwami w'intambara n'ubukerebutsi.

Hari **Ntare Rugamba w'amateka** — uwo dufise amakuru arambuye kuri we, kuruta uko twari twabigize ku bandi bose babanjirije, ariko dukirekura vyinshi: itariki nyakuri y'urupfu rwiwe, n'imbibe nyazo z'ubutegetsi bwiwe.

Ubu, dufise n'ikindi kintu tutari dufise ku bandi: **ahantu bavuga ko yaruhukiye**, naho tutashobora kwemeza ko ari byo koko.
