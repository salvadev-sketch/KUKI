# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 2 — EP04B — INTAMBARA ZISIGAYE

Twaramaze kubona Bugesera, Buyogoma, Imbo, na Bugufi.

Ariko ukwagura kwa Ntare Rugamba kwarimwo ibindi bintu bike, bikwiye kwitondewe imbere yo gusozera iyi ngingo.

---

## Bugesera: si yose yabaye Uburundi

Twaravuze muri EP03 ko Ntare Rugamba yatsinze Nsoro Nyabarega, umwami wa Bugesera.

Ariko hari umurongo utukura tudakwiye kwibagirwa: **Bugesera ntiyahavuye yose iba Uburundi.**

Igice co mu buraruko ca Bugesera caragiye munsi y'u Rwanda. Igice kinini gusa ni co cagiye munsi y'Uburundi. Bugesera rero yagabanijwe hagati y'ibihugu bibiri — ntiyari intsinzi yuzuye, ku ruhande rw'Uburundi.

---

## Kumoso

Hari amasoko ahuza ukwaguka kwa Ntare Rugamba n'akarere ka **Kumoso**, avuga ko cinjijwe mu Burundi hafi y'imyaka ya 1840 — igihe kimwe na Buyogoma.

Ariko izina n'imbibe vya Kumoso vyagiye bihinduka uko ibihe biraheruka, ku buryo tudashobora kuvuga umurongo ukomeye ngo "Kumoso yose yigaruriwe uwo mwaka." Tuzobishira 🟡, kugeza turonse isoko ryihariye risobanura imbibe zaco neza.

---

## Kinyaga: ingabo zisubira inyuma

Iyi ni imwe mu nkuru zikomeye kuruta izindi, kuko itwereka umupaka w'ukwagura kwa Ntare Rugamba.

Amasoko y'amateka y'u Rwanda avuga ko Ntare yateye mu bice vya **Nyiginya**, agera i **Kinyaga**. Ariko amwe muri ayo masoko avuga ikintu gikomeye: **ingabo z'Uburundi zarasubiye inyuma, inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntare Rugamba.**

Ibi bituma tubona ikintu tutigeze twita ku kanya: ubugari bw'ubwami ntibwari bwose buhoraho. Bimwe mu bice ntare yashikako mu buzima bwiwe ntibyagumye munsi y'Uburundi inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe.

Ni co gituma tudashaka kuvuga "Kinyaga yose yabaye intara y'Uburundi igihe cose." Tuvuga: **Ntare Rugamba yaragabye ibitero mu karere ka Kinyaga, ubutegetsi bw'Uburundi bugera muri ico karere, ariko ubwo butegetsi ntibwagumye inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe.**

---

## Icizere: bishingiye ku isoko

Mu bibanza twize kugeza ubu, dushobora kubishiraho ivyiciro biteguye:

**🟢 Bihagije icizere**: Bugufi (1850), Buyogoma (~1840).

**🟡 Bikeneye ubwitonzi**: Bugesera (igabanijwe), Kumoso, Imbo/Rusizi, Kinyaga (idahoraho), Shinge na Rugero/Cohoha (amasoko atandukana ku mwami).

Iyi si intambwe yo gukumira agaciro k'intsinzi za Ntare Rugamba. Ni intambwe yo kuzitondekanya uko zikwiye — imwe zizewe, izindi zikiri mu bwitonzi bw'ubushakashatsi.
