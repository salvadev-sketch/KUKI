# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP17 — MIREREKANO N'UBUTEGETSI BW'ABABILIGI

Twaramaze kumenya Paul Mirerekano nk'umufasha wa Rwagasore mu makoperative.

Ariko imbere yo kuza kuba uwo, yari amaze kumenyekana ku giti ciwe — kandi ubutegetsi bw'Ababiligi bwari bwaramaze kumwitondera.

---

Mu **1955**, **Jean-Paul Harroy**, wari Gouverneur w'u Ruanda-Urundi, yaranditse ku bimwerekeye. Inyandiko yiwe imwerekana nk'umuntu ashobora guhinduka umuyobozi w'abasaba amahinduka.

Ivyo bisobanura: Mirerekano ntiyari yaje kuba umunyapolitike kuko Rwagasore yamuzanye muri politike. Yari amaze kugira izina imbere y'aho.

---

Ku wa **31 Rusama 1955**, Mwami Baudouin w'Ababiligi yasuye Uburundi.

Ubuhamya buvuga ko Mirerekano yamwegereye, amwereka ingorane z'abahinzi. Hari inkuru ivuga ko Baudouin yamurungikiye tractors zibiri, imwe muri zo ikaza gufatwa n'ubutegetsi bwo mu gihugu. Iyi si nsobanuro twashobora kwemeza nk'ukuri kudashidikanywako — dufise gusa ubuhamya.

---

Mu **1957–58**, Mirerekano yagize uruhara mu bikorwa vy'intango bya UPRONA, hamwe na Rwagasore, **Thaddée Siryuyumunsi**, **André Nugu**, na **Valentin Bankumuhari**. Hari amasoko avuga ku kintu bita **"Accord de Kavumu"** — ariko urutonde rw'ababigizemwo uruhara ntiwahurira ku isoko rimwe, ku buryo ntidushobora kwemeza uwari umukuru wa mbere wa UPRONA.

---

Mu 1955, ubutegetsi bwashaka kumurungika i Ruhengeri, mu Rwanda. Aranse, ava mu kazi ka Leta.

Uko imyaka yagenze, hari ubuhamya buvuga ko konti yiwe ya banki yafunzwe kubera ibikorwa vyiwe vya politike — ariko ni **ubuhamya**, atari inyandiko y'ibanze twaronse.

---

Hanyuma, hari umuntu, **Alphonse Busigo**, yamenyesheje Rwagasore ko Mirerekano ari mu kaga.

Mirerekano yaciye ava mu Burundi, aja muri Congo.
