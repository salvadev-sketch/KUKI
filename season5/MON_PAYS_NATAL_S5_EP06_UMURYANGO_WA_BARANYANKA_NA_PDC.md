# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP06 — UMURYANGO WA BARANYANKA NA PDC

Rwagasore ntiyari wenyene ku ruzitiro rwa politike.

Hari uwundi muryango, ukomeye ku buryo bwiwe — umuryango wa **Baranyanka**.

---

**Pierre Baranyanka** yari umutware akomeye cane, afitaniye imigenderanire ikomeye n'ubutegetsi bw'Ababiligi.

Yari afise abahungu babiri bazoza kugira uruhara rukomeye muri iyi nkuru:

**Jean-Baptiste Ntidendereza**, yavutse i Irabiro, ku wa 31 Rusama 1926.

**Joseph Biroli**, yavutse ku wa 28 Rusama 1929.

---

Bombi bari abanyeshure b'urugero rudasanzwe.

Ntidendereza yize i Astrida, hanyuma aba umutware wa Mutabo (1943), hanyuma Bwambarangwe (1944–1960). Yari mu bagize **Conseil Supérieur du Pays** kuva 1954.

Biroli we yize i Astrida, hanyuma Anvers, Louvain, Oxford, na Harvard — bamwe bakamwita Umurundi wa mbere yaronse inyigisho za kaminuza.

---

Ku wa **5 Ruhuhuma 1960**, Ntidendereza yafashije gushinga **Parti Démocratique Chrétien — PDC.**

Biroli, umuvukanyi wiwe, aba umwe mu bayobozi bakuru ba PDC — hanyuma **Président** wawo.

---

UPRONA na PDC ntibari bafise igitekerezo kimwe.

UPRONA ya Rwagasore yashaka ubwigenge bwihuse.

PDC yo yashimika ku vugururwa ry'ubutunzi n'imibano, hamwe n'inzira ijana ku bwigenge mu buryo butandukanye, butagira igihuti.

---

Abashakashatsi bamwe bavuga ko iryo tandukaniro ryafitaniye isano n'amashami ya kera: UPRONA na Bezi, PDC na Batare — amashami twize mu Season 3.

Ariko ntitwovuga ngo iyo migambwe yari ishingiye ku bwoko canke ku muryango gusa. Hari abantu bo mu mashami yose muri iyo migambwe yombi.

---

Umuryango umwe, Baranyanka, wari rero ufise abagabo bakomeye ku ruhande rumwe rwa politike.

Kandi mu myaka izokurikira, izo nzira zombi — iya Rwagasore n'iya Baranyanka — zizohura, mu buryo bubi cane.
