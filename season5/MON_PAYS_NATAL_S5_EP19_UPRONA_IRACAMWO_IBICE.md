# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP19 — INYUMA YA RWAGASORE — UPRONA IRACAMWO IBICE

Inyuma y'intsinzi ikomeye ya UPRONA mu matora yo ku wa 18 Nyakanga 1961 — 58 kuri 64 vy'imyanya — Rwagasore yasavye Mirerekano gusubira mu Burundi, avuye muri Congo.

Ariko igihe yashitse, yasanze Gouvernement yari yamaze gutunganywa, kandi izina ryiwe ritari muri yo.

---

Ntitwovuga ngo turazi neza igituma. Hari ubuhamya buvuga ko Rwagasore yashatse ko Mirerekano ayobora UPRONA ubwayo, aho kuba muri Gouvernement. Ariko ni interprétation, atari ikintu cemejwe.

---

Ku wa 13 Gitugutu 1961, Rwagasore yarishwe — iminsi mike gusa inyuma yo gutangura kuyobora Gouvernement.

Urupfu rwiwe rwaciye rutera ikibazo gikomeye: **ninde agiye gukomeza umurimo wiwe?**

---

**André Muhirwa** yaciye aba Premier ministre.

Ariko Mirerekano yavuze ko Rwagasore yari yamugize **président intérimaire wa UPRONA** mu 1961 — iyo claim ikaba yarahakanywe n'abandi, harimwo Muhirwa ubwiwe.

Ntitwovuga "Rwagasore yarasize Mirerekano ari président wa UPRONA." Tuvuga: "Mirerekano yavuze ko Rwagasore yari yamugize président intérimaire."

---

Ku wa 4 Mukakaro 1962, inama y'abakuru ba UPRONA yashigikiye André Muhirwa ku burongozi.

Mirerekano ntiyemeye ico cemezo.

---

Haciye havuka amashami abiri akomeye:

**Monrovia** — uwayoborwaga na Mirerekano.

**Casablanca** — uwayoborwaga na Muhirwa.

Amasoko amwe avuga ko ayo mashami yagiye ahuzwa n'amacakubiri y'ubwoko — ariko UPRONA yo mu 1958 ntiyari yaratanguye ari umugambwe w'ubwoko bumwe. Ayo macakubiri yagiye akura inyuma y'urupfu rwa Rwagasore, atari imbere yaho.
