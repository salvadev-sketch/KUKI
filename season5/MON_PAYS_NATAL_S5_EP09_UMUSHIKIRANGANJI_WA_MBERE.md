# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP09 — UMUSHIKIRANGANJI WA MBERE — IMINSI 16

Rwagasore yagenywe kuba umushikiranganji wa mbere w'Uburundi.

Ariko iyo ntambwe yamaze iminsi cumi na itandatu gusa.

---

Uburundi bwari bugiye kuronka ubwigenge, imyaka itari mike iri imbere.

Rwagasore yari umuntu abenshi babona ko azoyobora urwo rugendo.

---

Ariko mu gihe cari kigufi cane, ibintu vyagiye vyihuta:

reta nshasha ishingwa,

Ntidendereza na PDC bagumana ubushobozi mu buyobozi (Ntidendereza yari yaramaze kubona umwanya wa Ministre de l'Intérieur mu myaka ibiri imbere),

n'ihiganwa rya politike ryari rikigenda rikomera.

---

Ku wa **13 Gitugutu 1961**, ibintu vyahindutse burundu.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP10 — URUPFU**, ni ho tuzobibona.
