# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP28 — IPEREREZA N'ISOZERO

Inyuma y'ubwicanyi, umuntu yabaye hagati mu iperereza: **Gonsalve Muyenzi**, impunzi y'Umunyarwanda yakorera ku Buserukizi bwa Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika i Bujumbura.

Yarafashwe.

Ntitwovuga "Muyenzi ni we yishe Ngendandumwe." Tuvuga: yabaye umwe mu bakekwa cane mu iperereza.

---

Hari inkuru zivuga ku mbunda yakoreshejwe, ifitaniye isano na **Butera**, umukozi w'Ambassade ya Amerika, umuhungu wa **François Rukeba** — umunyapolitike w'Umunyarwanda yari mu buhungiro. Ariko ivyo ni ivyavuzwe mu iperereza, atari urubanza rwaciwe.

Ntitwovuga ko Amerika yari inyuma y'ubwicanyi. Nta gihamya ihagije dufise.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Thinkingafrica.org ivuga ko, nk'uko René Lemarchand abivuga, uwishe Ngendandumwe — impunzi y'Umunyarwanda — yakorera ku Buserukizi bwa Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika i Bujumbura, kubera ko ubwo buserukizi bwakekaga ko Ngendandumwe yari afitaniye isano na Komunisme, biciye ku bucuti yagiranye n'Ubushinwa. Iyi ni interprétation ya Lemarchand, atari ikintu cemejwe n'urukiko.

---

Inyuma y'ubwicanyi, abanyapolitike bo mu mugwi wa **Casablanca** barafashwe: Albin Nyamoya, Prime Niyongabo, Zénon Nicyenzi, n'abandi.

Ariko **nta muntu wo muri Casablanca yaciriwe urubanza nk'uwateguye ubwo bwicanyi**.

Mu 1967, Cour Suprême y'Uburundi yakuyeho ibirego kubera kubura ibimenyetso bihagije.

**Nta commanditaire w'ubwicanyi bwa Ngendandumwe twoshobora kuvuga nk'uwemejwe n'urukiko.**

---

Mwambutsa IV yarungitse ubutumwa ku gihugu, asaba abantu kuguma batekanye — nk'uko yabigize inyuma y'urupfu rwa Rwagasore.

Imipaka y'igihugu yarafunzwe mu gihe gito.

---

Ngendandumwe yahambwe ku **Vugizo**, i Bujumbura, hafi y'imva ya Louis Rwagasore. Amaziko yabaye ku wa 17 Nzero 1965.

Vugizo, ubu, ni ahantu hafise insobanuro ikomeye mu mateka ya politike y'Uburundi — kuko ari ho abakuru babiri b'Uburundi, bombi bagandagujwe imbere y'uko batangura imirimo yabo, baruhukira.

---

Twamukurikiranye kuva mu Season imwe: **Rwagasore**, umuganwa yashinze UPRONA, agandagurwa 1961. **Mirerekano**, umufasha wiwe, agwa mu makimbirane ya UPRONA yaje kwica, agandagurwa 1965, imva yiwe itazwi. **Ngendandumwe**, umukuru w'ubwenge wagerageje kwiyunga inshuro ebyiri, agandagurwa 1965.

Batatu, imyaka ine, umugambwe umwe — UPRONA, uwatsindiye ubwigenge bw'Uburundi.

---

None se, iyi nkuru itwigisha iki?

Ko ubwigenge, mu Burundi, ntibwavuye ata gahomerwa. Ko intambwe za mbere z'igihugu gishasha zaranzwe n'ubwicanyi bw'abanyapolitike, ata rubanza rukemuye neza ikibazo c'uburyozwe.

Kandi ko iyo migenzo — kwica umukuru w'igihugu, ata rubanza rusobanutse — izosubira kuboneka mu Burundi, imyaka irenga makumyabiri inyuma.
