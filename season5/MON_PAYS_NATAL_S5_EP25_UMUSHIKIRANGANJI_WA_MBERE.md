# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP25 — UMUSHIKIRANGANJI WA MBERE — "DU PAIN ET LA PAIX"

Ku wa 13 Gitugutu 1961, Rwagasore yarishwe.

André Muhirwa yaciye aba Umushikiranganji wa mbere. Ngendandumwe yagumye muri Gouvernement, ari Vice-Premier ministre na Ministre des Finances.

---

Inyuma y'ukwegura kwa Gouvernement ya Muhirwa, ku wa **18 Ruheshi 1963**, Ngendandumwe aba **Umushikiranganji wa mbere w'Uburundi** — aba **Umuhutu wa mbere** yigeze kuba ku wo mwanya.

---

Yashize imbere iciyumviro: **"du pain et la paix"** — umukate n'amahoro.

Ubutunzi (uburimyi bwa café, iterambere) hamwe n'amahoro (kugabanya amatati ya politike, kubungabunga ubumwe) byari ibice bibiri vy'ico gitekerezo.

Yasavye kandi abanyagihugu gutanga imisi ibiri y'akazi ku buntu ku gihugu.

---

Mu **1964**, yagize urugendo muri Kenya, Uganda, na Tanganyika, asuzuma uburyo Uburundi bwoshobora kwifatanya na East African Federation.

Ariko igihe Uburundi bwatanguye imigenderanire n'**u Bushinwa bw'Abakomunisiti**, Mwambutsa IV ntiyabyakiriye neza. Ntitwovuga ko ari yo mvo yonyene yatumye asezera — hari n'amatati ya Gouvernement n'abashikiranganji.

---

Mu mpera za Ntwarante/ntango za Ndamukiza 1964, Gouvernement ya Ngendandumwe yarasenyutse. **Albin Nyamoya** aramusubirira.

Ariko Ngendandumwe ntiyavuye muri politike. Mu Nyakanga 1964, yayoboye **conference de réconciliation nationale** i Gitega.

Iyo nyandiko y'Université du Burundi imwita **"l'homme de la dernière chance"** — umuntu w'amahirwe ya nyuma.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP26 — ASUBIRA KU BUTEGETSI — NZERO 1965**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
