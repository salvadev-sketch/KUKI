# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP10 — URUPFU

Ku wa 13 Gitugutu 1961, Louis Rwagasore yari ku terasi, i Usumbura — ubu ni Bujumbura — asangira n'abandi.

Yari amaze iminsi cumi na itandatu gusa ari Umushikiranganji wa mbere w'Uburundi.

---

Umuntu yaramurashe.

Yitwa **Jean Kageorgis**, Umugereki.

---

Rwagasore yarapfuye.

Umuganwa yavutse mu bwami, yigishijwe mu Bubiligi, yatanguje amakoperative, yashinze UPRONA, yaronse intsinzi ikomeye mu matora, akaba umushikiranganji wa mbere — yaguye ku terasi, imbere y'uko ubwigenge bw'igihugu ciwe bushika.

---

Ntitwoshaka kwihuta kuvuga uwateguye iryo yicwa. Ico gice, dutegerezwa kucigenza mu bwitonzi, twitondera amasoko yose.

Ariko ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe ni ico gisigaye: **umukuru w'ejo hazoza h'Uburundi yaraguye, ku wa 13 Gitugutu 1961.**

---

Igihugu cose caratangaye.

Kandi ubwo bwicanyi ntibwazoguma buri ikibazo c'umuntu umwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP11 — UBWABUZE BWA KAGEORGIS**, ni ho tuzoraba icatanguye kugaragara.
