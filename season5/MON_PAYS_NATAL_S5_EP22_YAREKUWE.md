# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP22 — YAREKUWE — UBWIGENGE BW'IMYAKA IBIRI, N'IHIGANWA RYA 1965

Inyuma y'ugusenyuka kwa Gouvernement ya Albin Nyamoya, Mirerekano yasubiye mu Burundi mu ntango za 1965.

Ariko mu gihe c'amatora, akiri mu kwiyamamaza, yari akiri hanze y'igihugu.

---

Umugore wiwe, **Catherine Siniremera**, ni we yamukoreye campagne.

IWACU ivuga ko Catherine yagenda ku banyagihugu afise ifoto ya Mirerekano, akababwira ko umugabo wiwe abasaba amajwi.

Iyi ni imwe mu nkuru nziza cane muri iyi documentaire: Catherine yari Umututsikazi, mu gihe Mirerekano yari azwi cane nk'umunyapolitike w'Abahutu. Umuryango wiwe ubwawo wari ufise isano yambukiranya ayo macakubiri yaje kurushiriza gukomera muri politike y'igihugu.

---

Ku wa **10 Rusama 1965**, habaye amatora y'Inteko Ishinga Amategeko.

Mirerekano yarahiganiye Bujumbura — naho yari hanze y'igihugu, yaratsinze ku majwi menshi.

Gouvernement yari imurwanya. Ariko abanyagihugu baramutora.

---

Ku wa **20 Mukakaro 1965**, Inteko yaramutoye aba **Premier Vice-Président** wayo — icegera ca mbere c'Umukuru w'Inteko Ishinga Amategeko.

Mirerekano yari ageze ku rwego rwo hejuru rwa Leta.

---

Ariko igihugu cari kimaze kwinjira mu bihe bibi.

Urupfu rwa Pierre Ngendandumwe, amatati ya UPRONA, ikibazo hagati y'Umwami n'abanyapolitike, n'ikibazo c'ubwoko carushiriza kwiyongera.

Mu **Gitugutu 1965**, habaye igerageza ryo gutembagaza ubutegetsi ry'abasirikare.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP23 — COUP YA 1965, IFATWA N'URUPFU**, ni ho tuzoheza ubuzima bwa Mirerekano.
