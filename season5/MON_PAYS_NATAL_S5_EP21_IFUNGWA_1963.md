# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP21 — IFUNGWA RYA 1963

Mu 1963, ikibazo ca Mirerekano carushiriza kuba ic'umutekano w'igihugu.

Ubutegetsi bwaramuregereye ko afitaniye imigenderanire na **PARMEHUTU**, umugambwe w'Abahutu wo mu Rwanda, kandi ko yashaka gutanguza mu Burundi impinduka nk'izo zari zimaze kuba mu Rwanda.

Ntitwovuga ngo turazi ko ivyo ari vyo yakoze. Ni allegation. Nta preuve dufise.

---

Ku wa **26 Ruhuhuma 1963**, Umushikirizamanza mukuru w'Umwami yategetse ko afatwa.

Mirerekano yajanywe mw'ibohero rya **Mpimba**.

---

Ariko icakurikiye ni cyo gitangaje: mu gihe yari mw'ibohero, habaye **mutiny y'abajandarume**.

Bamwe muri bo baramurekuye.

Ikibazo ntabwo cari kikiri hagati ya Mirerekano na Gouvernement gusa — cari cimaze gushika no ku gisirikare ubwaco.

---

Ariko ntitwovuga ngo Gendarmerie yose yari ku ruhande rwiwe. Igice gusa ni co cagize gutyo — impamvu nyayo iracakenewe gusuzumwa vyimbitse.

---

Mirerekano yarasubijwe mw'ibohero.

Ku wa **17 Mukakaro 1963**, arekuwe rwose — inyuma y'intervention ya **Mwambutsa IV Bangiricenge**.

Umwami, naho atari umunywanyi wa Monrovia canke Casablanca, yagumye afise uruhara mu bibazo bikomeye vya politike y'igihugu.

---

Ariko kurekurwa ntibisigura ko ikibazo cari kirangiye. Mirerekano yabonye ko ubuzima bwiwe buri mu kaga.

Mu **1964**, yasubiye mu buhungiro — aja i **Uvira**, muri Congo, hanyuma i **Butare**, mu Rwanda.

---

Muri Ruheshi 1964, yararegwe kandi ko yagize uruhara mu mugambi wo guhirika Gouvernement ya **Albin Nyamoya**. Ntitwovuga ko yarateguye coup — tuvuga ko yararegwe.

---

Kandi hari uwundi muntu w'ingenzi tutokwirengagiza: **Pierre Ngendandumwe**, umunyapolitike wa UPRONA, umwe mu bantu bakomeye b'Abahutu — uwo, na we, azoguye mu buryo bubi cane, mu 1965.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP22 — YARAREKUWE — UBUHUNGIRO BWA MWAMBUTSA IV**, ni ho tuzoraba icakurikiye.
