# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP26 — ASUBIRA KU BUTEGETSI — NZERO 1965

Mu ntango za Nzero 1965, Mwambutsa IV yakuyeho Gouvernement ya Albin Nyamoya, asaba Ngendandumwe gutunganya Gouvernement nshasha.

Ku wa **7 Nzero 1965**, Ngendandumwe yaremeye — inyuma yo kubanza kugira amakenga menshi.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Pierre Ngendandumwe") ivuga ko "On 7 January 1965, Mwambutsa called on Ngendandumwe to replace Nyamoya and form a new government" — bihuza neza n'itariki EP26 ivuga.

---

Yari azi ko igihugu kirimwo imigwi itamushigikiye. Inyuma y'itorwa ryiwe, abarwanya iryo genwa ryiwe barungikiye Mwambutsa ikete bamumenyesha ko badashigikiye Ngendandumwe.

Ntiyemeye kubera atazi ico bizamugeraho. Yaremeye naho yari azi ko igihugu kirimwo ababuranira.

---

Yagize Gouvernement y'abantu 13: Abatutsi 7, Abahutu 6.

Naho yari ahuzwa n'umugwi wa Monrovia, Gouvernement yiwe ya nyuma ntiyari iy'itsinda rimwe gusa.

---

Ariko Mwambutsa IV yagumana ububasha ku mashami abiri akomeye: **Michel Micombero** (igisirikare) na **Pascal Magenge** (Gendarmerie).

Naho Ngendandumwe yari Umushikiranganji wa mbere, inzego z'umutekano zari zikiri mu maboko y'abandi.

---

Ku wa **8 Nzero 1965**, yashikirije discours-programme, avuga:

**"Mon Gouvernement sera l'instrument authentique de la politique de réconciliation."**

Iciyumviro c'iyunga cari hagati mu mugambi wiwe, kurenza uko cari cimeze mu 1963.

---

Ku wa **15 Nzero 1965**, Gouvernement yiwe yaramenyeshejwe ku mugaragaro, saa sita.

Amasaha ane inyuma yaho, habaye imyiyerekano yo kwiyamiriza iyo Gouvernement.

Ngendandumwe ntiyabonye umwanya wo gutangura iyo programme.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP27 — URUPFU RWA NGENDANDUMWE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
