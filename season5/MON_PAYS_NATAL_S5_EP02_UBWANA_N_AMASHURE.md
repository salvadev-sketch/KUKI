# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP02 — UBWANA N'AMASHURE

Umwana w'umuganwa ntiyakuze mu bwoba bw'ibwami gusa.

Yakuze mu mashure.

---

Afise imyaka irindwi, yatanguye amashure abanza mu mashure ya Gatolika.

Yize i **Bukeye**, i **Kanyinya** no i **Gitega**.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Louis Rwagasore", ikurikiza Weinstein 1976, p. 251) ivuga ko yatanguye amashure afise imyaka irindwi, mu mashure ya Gatolika i Bukeye, Kanyinya na Gitega.

---

Mu **1945**, yaciye ajana kuri **Groupe Scolaire d'Astrida**, i Butare y'ubu — ishure ryari rikomeye cane mu karere kose, ryatoje abagabo benshi bazoza kugira uruhara mu buyobozi bw'u Rwanda n'Uburundi mu mpera z'ubukoloni. Yamaze aho imyaka itandatu.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia (ikurikiza Weinstein 1976) yemeza 1945 n'imyaka itandatu i Astrida. BlackPast ivuga ko iryo shure ryashinzwe mu 1929 n'Abafratere b'Urukundo (Frères de la Charité), Ababiligi, kugira ngo batoze Abarundi n'Abanyarwanda bafasha ubutegetsi bw'abakoloni.

---

Ni ukuvuga: yize mu mashure yo mu turere dutandukanye, ahagurukanye n'abandi bahungu bo mu miryango itandukanye y'igihugu, atari mu muryango w'ubwami gusa.

---

Ariko ubwo bwana bw'amashure bwari intango gusa.

Kuko imbere yiwe, hari indi ntambwe: **Uburaya**.
