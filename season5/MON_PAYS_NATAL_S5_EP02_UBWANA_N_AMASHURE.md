# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP02 — UBWANA N'AMASHURE

Umwana w'umuganwa ntiyakuze mu bwoba bw'ibwami gusa.

Yakuze mu mashure.

---

Afise imyaka itandatu, mu **1938**, Rwagasore yatanguye amashure abanza.

Yize i **Bukeye**, hanyuma i **Kanyinya**.

---

Amaze imyaka icumi, yaciye ajana mu mashure yisumbuye: i **Gitega**, ku Bafratere b'Urukundo, aho amaze imyaka ibiri.

Hanyuma aja kuri **Groupe Scolaire d'Astrida**, i Butare y'ubu — ishure ryari rikomeye cane mu karere kose, ryatoje abagabo benshi bazoza kugira uruhara mu buyobozi bw'u Rwanda n'Uburundi mu mpera z'ubukoloni. Yamaze aho imyaka itandatu.

---

Ni ukuvuga: mu myaka cumi na ine, Rwagasore yari amaze kwiga ahantu hane hatandukanye — kandi ahagarukanye n'abandi bahungu bo mu miryango itandukanye y'igihugu, atari mu muryango w'ubwami gusa.

---

Ariko ubwo bwana bw'amashure bwari intango gusa.

Kuko imbere yiwe, hari indi ntambwe: **Uburaya**.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP03 — AMASHURE MU BUBILIGI**, ni ho tuzoraba iyo ntambwe.
