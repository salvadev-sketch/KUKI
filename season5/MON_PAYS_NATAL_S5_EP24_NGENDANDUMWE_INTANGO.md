# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP24 — PIERRE NGENDANDUMWE: INTANGO

**Pierre Ngendandumwe** yavutse mu **1930**, mu karere ka Ngozi, mu buraruko bw'Uburundi — colline nyayo n'inkomoko y'umuryango wiwe biracakenewe kwitondera.

Yakuriye mu muryango munini w'Abahutu wo muri Ngozi.

---

Yagiye kwiga i Kinshasa, muri **Université Lovanium**, aronka **licence en Sciences Administratives et Politiques** — aba umwe mu Barundi ba mbere bararonse inyigisho za kaminuza.

---

I Kinshasa niho yahuriye na **Thérèse**, umunyeshure w'ubuforomakazi, mu 1958. Baje kwubakana mu 1961. Thérèse yari umukobwa w'umukuru w'umuryango wo mu buraruko, Grand Chef Coya.

---

Ngendandumwe yagiye gukorera **administration coloniale belge**, aba umufasha w'umutegetsi w'intara — ariko icarimwe, mu mpisho, yari afise uruhara muri UPRONA.

Thérèse yavuze ko Rwagasore yamurindaga, atashaka ko ibikorwa vya Pierre vyo muri politike bimushira mu kaga — bombi bakunze guhurira mw'ijoro kugira birinde amaso.

---

Thérèse yibuka Rwagasore na Pierre nk'**"abagabo b'inyabubasha, abakozi bakomeye, bombi batavuga bikwiye."**

---

Ngendandumwe na **Paul Mirerekano** bari mu ba lieutenant ba mbere bizerwa ba Rwagasore muri UPRONA.

Mu 1961, UPRONA imaze gutsinda amatora, Ngendandumwe yinjiye muri Gouvernement, aba **Ministre des Finances**.
