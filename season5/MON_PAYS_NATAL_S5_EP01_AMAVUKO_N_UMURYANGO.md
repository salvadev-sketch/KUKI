# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP01 — LOUIS RWAGASORE: AMAVUKO N'UMURYANGO

Uburundi buracari munsi y'ubutegetsi bw'Ababiligi.

Umwami ni Mwambutsa IV, uwo twize mu Season 4 — akiri muto, ategekwa na regency.

Kandi mu muryango w'uwo mwami, havuka umwana azoza guhindura amateka y'igihugu, mu buryo yaba akiriho canke atakiriho.

---

**Louis Rwagasore** yavutse ku wa **10 Nzero 1932**, i Gitega.

Yari umuhungu wa Mwambutsa IV Bangiricenge n'umugore wiwe, **Thérèse Kanyonga**.

---

Kuvukira mu muryango w'ubwami, mu gihe c'ubukoloni, vyari bifise insobanuro ikomeye: umwana yari azoza gukura afise urumuri rw'icubahiro ry'ingoma, ariko akaba n'umwe mu bantu Ababiligi bazoshaka kugenzura, bakamwigisha inyigisho zabo.

---

Ariko iyo nkuru y'umwana azoza kuba umunyapolitike w'ingenzi, atari umuganwa w'izina gusa, iracatangura.
