# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP18 — MIREREKANO MURI CONGO

Mirerekano yari amaze kuba mu kaga mu Burundi.

Mu **1959**, aja muri Congo Belge.

Ariko ivyo ntibisobanura ko yahagaritse politike. Yari akiri mu bikorwa vya UPRONA, ariko ubu ari kure y'igihugu ciwe.

---

Ku wa **30 Ruheshi 1960**, Congo yararonse ubwigenge.

Mirerekano na Rwagasore bari i **Léopoldville**, mu birori — bavugwa nk'abaserukira UPRONA.

Iki ni ikintu c'ingenzi: UPRONA ntiyagumye ari umugambwe w'imbere mu Burundi gusa. Yari yatanguye kugira imigenderanire n'abandi baharanira ubwigenge muri Afrika.

---

Hari amasoko avuga ko, muri ivyo birori, Rwagasore yashikirije Mirerekano kuri **Patrice Lumumba**, Premier Ministre wa Congo, kandi ko bagiranye ibiganiro birebire ku rugamba rwo kwikukira. Ariko ntidufise inyandiko y'ibanze y'icyo kiganiro — ni ubuhamya, atari gihamya y'ibanze.

---

Mirerekano ntiyaciye asubira mu Burundi ako kanya. Yagumye muri Congo, kugira ntagaruke mu minwe y'ubutegetsi bwa Harroy.

---

Mu ntango za **1961**, yaranditse akantu k'inyandiko: **"Mbwire gito canje, gito c'uwundi cumvireho."**

Muri iyo nyandiko, ntiyavugaga ku bwigenge gusa. Yavugaga ku bantu bato, ku bahinzi, ku butungane hagati y'abanyagihugu.

Ni co gituma social justice iba igice kikomeye c'inkuru yiwe, atari politike y'ubwigenge gusa.

---

Ariko igihe yasubiraga mu Burundi, ibintu vyari bimaze guhinduka — kandi bizohinduka kurushiriza, mu myaka ikurikira.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP19 — INYUMA YA RWAGASORE — UPRONA IRACAMWO IBICE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
