# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP13 — UBWIGENGE BURAZA — URUBANZA RURASUBIRWAMWO

Ku wa 1 Mukakaro 1962, Uburundi bwararonse ubwigenge.

Hashizweho uburyo bushasha bw'ubutungane, bushingiye ku Itegeko Nshinga rishasha ry'igihugu cigenga.

---

Icaza gukurikira ni ikintu kidasanzwe: urubanza rwa Ntidendereza n'abandi baregwa rwarasubiwemwo.

Kuko hari ikibazo c'uko abaregwa batari bararonse uburenganzira bwo kuburanishwa hakurikijwe uburyo bushasha bwateganijwe n'Itegeko Nshinga.

---

Ku wa 27 Gitugutu 1962, **Supreme Court** yarafashe ingingo yo gusubiramwo urubanza.

---

Ubutungane bw'Uburundi bwigenga, bwari bugiye gufata ingingo ku kibazo cari kimaze kubaho mu gihe c'ubukoloni.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP14 — URUBANZA RWA KABIRI — IBIHANO VY'URUPFU**, ni ho tuzoraba icavuyemwo.
