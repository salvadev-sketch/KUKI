# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP17 — UMURAGE / ISOZERO

Twamukurikiranye kuva ku mavuko, i Gitega, mu 1932.

Twaramubonye akura mu mashure, agenda i Bukeye, Kanyinya, Gitega, Astrida.

Twaramubonye aja mu Buraya, ariko aza gusubira, atari umwigishwa gusa, ariko afise iciyumviro c'igihugu ciwe kizoza.

Twaramubonye ashinga amakoperative, hanyuma UPRONA, aronka intsinzi ikomeye mu matora, akaba umushikiranganji wa mbere w'Uburundi.

Kandi twaramubonye apfa, ku wa 13 Gitugutu 1961, iminsi cumi na itandatu gusa akiri ku murimo — hanyuma tugakurikirana urubanza rwaje, iherezo ry'abaregwa, n'ikibazo cagumye gifunguye ku ruhara rw'Ababiligi.

---

None se, ico Rwagasore yasize Uburundi, ni iki?

Uyu musi, imva yiwe — **mausolée y'i Vugizo, i Bujumbura** — igumye ahantu hakomeye mu mihango y'igihugu.

Leta y'Uburundi imwita **héros de l'indépendance** — intwari y'ubwigenge.

---

Ariko ubwigenge yaharaniye, ntiyabubonye.

Umugambwe yashinze wagumye ari intwaro ikomeye mu vya politike, ariko urupfu rwiwe rwasize igikomere kitigeze gikira burundu.

---

Uburundi bwararonse ubwigenge, umwaka umwe inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe.

Ariko igihugu cabuze umuntu abenshi babona ko yari azoyobora urwo rugendo — kandi Uburundi, mu myaka igera kuri 60 izokurikira, buzobandanya buhura n'ibindi bwicanyi bikomeye vy'abanyapolitike bakuru.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — NTARE V NDIZEYE**, ni ho tuzokomeza — umwami wa nyuma w'Uburundi, uwakuye se ku ngoma.
