# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP07 — 1960: URUGAMBA N'IFUNGWA I BURURI

Mu **Munyonyo 1960**, habaye amatora ya mbere manini — ay'inzego z'ibibanza.

Ariko UPRONA ntiyari kubona amahirwe angana n'ay'abandi.

---

Kuva ku wa **27 Gitugutu gushika ku wa 9 Kigarama 1960**, Rwagasore yashizwe mu rugo rwagenzurwa — résidence surveillée — i Bururi.

Ubutegetsi bw'Ababiligi bwari bwarabonye muri UPRONA ikintu gishobora kubangamira, kandi bugerageza kuyigabanyiriza ubushobozi bwo gukora imyiyerekano ya politike ata ngorane.

---

Muri ico gihe, umugambwe wa **PDC** — uwo Ntidendereza na Biroli bari bamaze gushinga — waratsinze bikomeye mu matora ya komine.

PDC yaronse intebe nyinshi kurusha UPRONA.

---

Kuri Biroli na PDC, iyo ntsinzi yabatumye babona ko bari bafise amahirwe akomeye mu matora y'igihugu yari gukurikira.

Ariko ibintu vyari bigiye guhinduka mu buryo batari bibiteze.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP08 — INTSINZI YA UPRONA (18 NYAKANGA 1961)**, ni ho tuzobibona.
