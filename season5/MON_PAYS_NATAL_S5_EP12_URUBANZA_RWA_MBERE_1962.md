# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP12 — URUBANZA RWA MBERE — 1962

Ku wa 2 Ndamukiza 1962, tribunal y'i Usumbura, igizwe n'abacamanza b'Ababiligi, yafashe ingingo.

---

**Ntidendereza, Kageorgis, na Nahimana** baraciriwe urwo gupfa.

Abandi baregwa barahawe ibihano bitandukanye.

---

Ariko ku wa 7 Rusama 1962, **Cour d'Appel** yarahinduye ibihano:

Kageorgis aguma aciriwe urwo gupfa.

**Ntidendereza n'abandi bamwe, ibihano vyabo biragabanywa gushika ku myaka 20 y'igifungo.**

---

Ni ukuvuga: urubanza rwa mbere ntirwaciye ruhita rutuma Ntidendereza yicwa.

Ariko ikibazo ntabwo cari kirangiye.

---

Ku wa 30 Ruheshi 1962 — umusi umwe imbere y'uko Uburundi bwikukira — **Jean Kageorgis yarashwe i Usumbura.**

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP13 — UBWIGENGE BURAZA — URUBANZA RURASUBIRWAMWO**, ni ho tuzoraba ivyahavuye.
