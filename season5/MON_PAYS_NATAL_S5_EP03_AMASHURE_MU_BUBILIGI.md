# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP03 — AMASHURE MU BUBILIGI

Mu **1951**, Rwagasore yagiye i **Anvers**, kwiga mu **Institut universitaire des Territoires d'Outre-Mer** (ishure ry'ubumenyi bw'ubukoloni).

Inyuma y'umwaka umwe, yaciye ajana muri **Université catholique de Louvain**, aho yamaze imyaka itatu, arangiza afise diplome muri **political economy**.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Louis Rwagasore", ikurikiza Lemarchand 1970, pp. 328 na 335, na Akyeampong & Gates 2012, p. 229) ivyemeza. 🟡 Andi masoko aratandukanye: BlackPast ivuga 1952; The Citizen ivuga ko yize "Management and Agriculture". Twahinduye inyandiko ya mbere (1953; sciences agronomiques 1954-1956) kuko nta soko ryayishigikira.

---

Ariko amashure y'iciyumviro ntiyari yo yonyene. Inyandiko ya mbere yavuga ko mu 1955 yari gérant w'ishirahamwe ry'ubudandaji i Anvers.

🔴 *(Iyo nkuru y'ubudandaji i Anvers ntitwabonye isoko ryayemeza; tuyikuyeho nk'ukuri kwemejwe.)*

---

Mu **1956**, yarandikiye inyandiko yitwa **"Projet de constitution murundi."**

Muri iyo nyandiko, Rwagasore yaravuze ko itegeko-shingiro ry'Uburundi ryagombye kuba nk'ico igihugu gito, ari uko kiri, gifise ukuri n'ivyifuzo bikomeye byo gushikira — atari ikintu cavuye hanze gusa.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 BlackPast ivuga ko mu 1956 Rwagasore yasavye Guverineri Harroy gushiraho itegeko-shingiro "rya Murundi", ariko ntitwabonye itariki nyayo (6 Ruhuhuma) canke aho yari ari. Inyandiko ye ubwayo iri mu gitabo *Paroles et écrits de Louis Rwagasore* (Karthala, 2012, Christine Deslaurier); turacyakeneye kugisuzuma imbere yo kuvuga ayo magambo nk'ayiwe ijambo ku ijambo.

---

Mu **Kigarama 1956** (Ukuboza), yasubiye mu Burundi.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia (ikurikiza Akyeampong & Gates 2012, p. 229) ivuga ko yasubiye i Urundi muri Ukuboza 1956. Twakuyeho inzira ya Léopoldville n'itariki ya Gitugutu, kuko nta soko ryabyemeje.

Ntiyasubiye nk'umwigishwa yarangije amashure gusa.
