# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP03 — AMASHURE MU BUBILIGI

Mu **1953**, Rwagasore yari i **Anvers**, ariko kwiga **Sciences coloniales.**

Kuva mu **1954 gushika 1956**, yaciye ajana muri **Université catholique de Louvain**, aho yiga **sciences agronomiques.**

---

Ariko amashure y'iciyumviro ntiyari yo yonyene.

Mu **1955**, yaciye aba gérant w'ishirahamwe ry'ubudandaji i Anvers. Yasuye amashirahamwe y'inganda.

Ubutunzi bwari bumaze kumuraje ishinga — atari ikintu yatanguye kwitwararika mu buzima bwiwe bwa politike gusa, ariko kimwe mu bintu yasanzwe yiyumvira kuva mu bwana bwiwe.

---

Ku wa **6 Ruhuhuma 1956**, imbere y'uko asubira mu Bubiligi kurangiza amashure, yarandikye inyandiko yitwa **"Projet de constitution murundi."**

Muri iyo nyandiko, Rwagasore yaravuze ko itegeko-shingiro ry'Uburundi ryagombye kuba nk'ico igihugu gito, ari uko kiri, gifise ukuri n'ivyifuzo bikomeye byo gushikira — atari ikintu cavuye hanze gusa.

Iyi si inkuru y'undi muntu ku bimwerekeye. Ni amagambo yiwe ubwiwe.

---

Mu **Gitugutu 1956**, amashure yiwe mu Bubiligi arangira.

Anyura i Léopoldville, muri Congo, asubira mu Burundi.

Ntasubira nk'umwigishwa yarangije amashure gusa.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP04 — AMAKOPERATIVE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
