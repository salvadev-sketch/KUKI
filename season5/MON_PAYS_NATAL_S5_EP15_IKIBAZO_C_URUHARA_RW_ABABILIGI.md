# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP15 — IKIBAZO C'URUHARA RW'ABABILIGI

Commission y'Umuryango w'Abibumbye yarakoze iperereza, ironka ibirego vy'uko bamwe mu bategetsi b'Ababiligi bari bafise uruhara mu kibazo.

Ariko ico gihe, iyo Commission ntiyashitse ku mwanzuro wo kwemeza ko ubutegetsi bw'Ababiligi ari bwo bwateguye ubwo bwicanyi.

---

Imyaka myinshi inyuma, hari inyandiko zaje kuboneka, zitumye abashakashatsi nka **Ludo De Witte** bavuga ko uruhara rw'abategetsi bamwe b'Ababiligi rwari rukomeye cane kurusha uko vyari vyemejwe mu gihe c'urubanza.

Uyu mushakashatsi avuga ko hari raporo y'Umushikirizamanza wa Bruxelles itigeze isohorwa ku mugaragaro, kandi ko bamwe mu bakozi b'ubutegetsi b'Ababiligi bavuze ko **Roberto Régnier**, wari Resident w'Ababiligi, yari yavuze ko Rwagasore akwiye kwicwa.

---

Ariko ibi ntibisobanura ko Ababiligi bose bategetse urupfu rwa Rwagasore.

Ntitwovuga gutyo.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga: hari ubushakashatsi bushasha n'inyandiko z'ibanga zishira urumuri ku ruhara rw'abategetsi bamwe b'Ababiligi, ariko uruhara nyarwo rwa buri muntu ruracari ikibazo c'ubushakashatsi n'impaka.

---

Ntidendereza, ku ruhande rwiwe, ntiyigeze arekurwa.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP16 — 15 NZERO 1963 — IHEREZO**, ni ho tuzoraba iherezo ry'urubanza.
