# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP20 — IKORANIRO N'IFATWA RYA 1962

Mu mpera za Myandagaro 1962, Mirerekano yahamagaye ikoraniro rinini ku kibuga **Prince Louis Rwagasore**, i Bujumbura — asaba ko ikibazo c'uburongozi bwa UPRONA gikemurwa biciye mu matora yisanzuye, atari mu mvo za guverinoma.

---

Gouvernement ya Muhirwa ntiyabibonye nk'ikoraniro risanzuye.

**Jean Ntiruhwama**, Ministre de l'Intérieur, yategetse ko Mirerekano afatwa.

Hano hari amasoko abiri atavuga rumwe: rimwe rivuga ko yafatiwe ku kibuga ubwaco, irindi rikavuga ko yafashwe inyuma yaho, ku Commissariat — Mirerekano atari yaragiye ku kibuga icogihe. Ntitwovuga rimwe muri byo nk'ukuri kwose.

---

Icakurikiye ni cyo gitangaje cane: **abajandarume benshi baranse ko Mirerekano aguma mw'ibohero.**

Baramurekuye. Bamwe baramuherekeza gushika iwe.

Ariko ntitwovuga ngo Gendarmerie yose yari ku ruhande rwiwe — igice cayo gusa ni co cagize gutyo.

---

Ico kibazo caciye kigera ku rwego rukomeye ku buryo Umwami **Mwambutsa IV** ubwiwe yinjiyemwo, agashaka uburyo bwo gukemura ikibazo cy'uburongozi bwa UPRONA biciye muri Congrès.

---

Muri Nyakanga 1962 (Nyakanga y'ico gihe = September), Congrès yabereye i Muramvya. Amasoko atavuga rumwe ku vyavuye muri yo: hari avuga ko Mirerekano na Muhirwa bombi baba vice-présidents, izindi zikavuga ko **Joseph Bamina** ari we yatorewe kuyobora, Mirerekano na Muhirwa bakaba vice-présidents biwe.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Joseph Bamina," asubiramwo Lemarchand 1970:411-412) ivuga ko muri icyo gikorwa cyo mu 1962 UPRONA yaguye mu macakubiri, kandi ko habonetse "a cross-party Hutu caucus growing in strength" — bimwe mu bisobanuro vy'aho ibintu vyagiye bigenda.

---

Hari ubuhamya buvuga ko abashigikira Mirerekano mu turere twa Rutana na Ruyigi batashoboye gushika ku matora — inzira zarafunzwe, imodoka zarabujijwe. Ariko iyi ni allegations, atari gihamya y'ibanze.

---

Mirerekano ntiyemera ubuyobozi bushasha. Atangura gukwiragiza inyandiko zinenga Muhirwa, asaba mbere n'iperereza rishasha ku rupfu rwa Rwagasore.

**Monrovia** (Mirerekano) na **Casablanca** (Muhirwa) baragumana amashami menshi — ariko ntitwovuga ngo iryo tandukaniro ryari ku bwoko bwonyene: Monrovia yari irimwo Abahutu benshi n'Abatutsi bamwe, Casablanca yari iyiganjemwo Abatutsi. Yari kandi ifitaniye isano n'inzira ya politike mpuzamakungu — Monrovia moderate/pro-Uburayi, Casablanca progressive.

---

Ikibazo ntikirarangira. Kandi mu myaka ikurikira, kizoshika ku rwego rurenze politike y'umugambwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP21 — IFUNGWA RYA 1963**, ni ho tuzoraba.
