# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP27 — URUPFU RWA NGENDANDUMWE

Ku mugoroba wo ku wa 15 Nzero 1965, umugore wiwe, **Thérèse**, yari ari ku bitaro, Clinique Prince Louis Rwagasore. Yari amaze kwibaruka umwana wa kane w'umuryango.

Ngendandumwe yaje kumuraba, hamwe n'umwana, hanyuma aragenda.

---

Thérèse yavuze ubuhamya budasanzwe: **"mon mari conduisait lui-même sa voiture, sans garde du corps"** — umugabo wiwe ubwiwe ni we yatwaraga imodoka yiwe, ata barinzi.

Ntitwovuga ko kuba atagira umurinzi ari co catumye yicwa — nta gihamya dufise. Ariko ivyo bituma dutahura igituma uwashaka kumutera yari afise akaryo.

---

Amasoko atavuga rumwe ku isaha nyayo: hagati ya saa mbiri n'igice n'iyindi z'ijoro.

Ngendandumwe yasohotse, hanyuma mu mwijima hakumvikana amasasu.

Le Monde yaranditse ko yarashwe amasasu menshi mu **mugongo**.

**Pierre Ngendandumwe yarapfuye ku wa 15 Nzero 1965, imbere ya Clinique Prince Louis Rwagasore.**

---

Igihugu carongeye kubona umukuru wo mu ntambwe zo hejuru z'ubutegetsi bwaco agandagurwa — nk'uko cari camaze kubibona kuri Rwagasore, imyaka ine imbere yaho.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP28 — IPEREREZA N'ISOZERO**, ni ho tuzoraba icakurikiye.
