# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP11 — UBWABUZE BWA KAGEORGIS

Ku wa 31 Gitugutu 1961, Kageorgis yarabajijwe na Commission y'Umuryango w'Abibumbye, yari yaje gukora iperereza ku rupfu rwa Rwagasore.

Ubuhamya bwiwe burafise akamaro kanini.

---

Kageorgis yavuze ko we ubwiwe yari **"instrument of execution"** — ni ukuvuga uwakoze igikorwa co kurasa — ariko ko igitekerezo cari camushikiye biciye kuri **Biroli na Iatrou**.

Yavuze ko yasezeranywe amafaranga y'amafaranga 1.000.000, igihe cose yoba yiciye Rwagasore.

---

Ariko inyandiko nyayo ya Commission y'Umuryango w'Abibumbye (raporo yabo, iboneka ku mugaragaro) ivuga ikindi: **Ntidendereza na we aravugwa cane cane** muri ubwo buhamya — Biroli avugwa ko yasavye Kageorgis "on his own behalf and on behalf of Ntindendereza and Iatrou," kandi Ntidendereza ubwiwe ni we wabwiye Kageorgis aho Rwagasore yari ari ico gihe.

---

Ntitwovuga ngo abantu bose bafatanywe uruhara rumwe — Iatrou ubwiwe yarahakanye uruhara rwiwe, naho Ntidendereza we yaciye avuga ko yagize uruhara (ku ntango), hanyuma inyuma aravuguruza ivyo yavuze.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP12 — URUBANZA RWA MBERE — 1962**, ni ho tuzoraba icavuye mu ruvunga rw'iperereza.
