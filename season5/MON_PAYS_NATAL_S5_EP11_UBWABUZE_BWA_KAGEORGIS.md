# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP11 — UBWABUZE BWA KAGEORGIS

Ku wa 31 Gitugutu 1961, Kageorgis yarabajijwe na Commission y'Umuryango w'Abibumbye, yari yaje gukora iperereza ku rupfu rwa Rwagasore.

Ubuhamya bwiwe burafise akamaro kanini.

---

Kageorgis yavuze ko we ubwiwe yari **"instrument of execution"** — ni ukuvuga uwakoze igikorwa co kurasa — ariko ko igitekerezo cari camushikiye biciye kuri **Biroli na Iatrou**.

Yavuze ko yari yasezeranywe amafaranga menshi hamwe n'akazi, igihe PDC yoba iri ku butegetsi.

---

Ariko hano hari ikintu gikomeye tugomba kwitonderako:

Muri ubwo buhamya, Kageorgis ntiyavuze **Ntidendereza** nk'umuntu yamuhaye amafaranga canke nk'umuntu ubwiwe yamusavye kurasa Rwagasore.

Yavuze cane cane **Biroli na Iatrou.**

---

Ivyo ni ngirakamaro kuko bitwereka ko uruhara rwa buri muntu mu mugambi rutari rworoshe, uko rimwe na rimwe ruvugwa.

Ntitwovuga ngo abantu bose bafatanywe uruhara rumwe. Ubushakashatsi bugomba gutandukanya, umuntu ku wundi.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP12 — URUBANZA RWA MBERE — 1962**, ni ho tuzoraba icavuye mu ruvunga rw'iperereza.
