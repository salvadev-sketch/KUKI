# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP08 — INTSINZI YA UPRONA — 18 NYAKANGA 1961

Ku wa **18 Nyakanga 1961**, habaye amatora y'abashingamateka.

Ivyavuye muri ayo matora vyaratangaje benshi.

---

**UPRONA — imyanya 58 kuri 64.**

**PDC n'abandi bafatanyabikorwa — bake cane.**

---

Amezi make imbere yaho, PDC yari yaratsinze mu matora ya komine, PDC ikaba yariyumvira ko ifise amahirwe menshi.

Ubu, iyo ntsinzi yari yarazimiye.

UPRONA, umugambwe wa Rwagasore, yaratsindiye ku rugero rutangaje.

---

Ku wa **28 Nyakanga 1961**, Louis Rwagasore aba **Premier Ministre designate** w'Uburundi.

Umuganwa, umuhungu w'umwami, umuyobozi wa politike — ubu, na none, umukuru w'igihugu ku bwenegihugu.

---

Kuri Ntidendereza na Biroli, iyi yari intsinzi ikomeye ibabujije amahirwe bari bariyumvira.

Kandi mu myaka mike gusa, iryo hombo rizoza kugira ingaruka zibabaje.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 5 — EP09 — UMUSHIKIRANGANJI WA MBERE — IMINSI 16**, ni ho tuzoraba iherezo ry'ubwo butegetsi.
