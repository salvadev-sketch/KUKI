# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP22 — 1972: GUFATWA

Twize kuri 1972 vyimbitse, mu ma-episode 14–16 y'iyi Season.

Michel Kayoya, umusaserdoti n'umwanditsi azwi, yari mu bantu benshi cane bashikiwe n'ubwicanyi bwo muri ico gihe.

---

Muri Ndamukiza 1972, hari amakuru avuga ko yakuwe ku mirimo imwe yari afise muri diocèse ya Muyinga, agasubizwa muri diocèse ya Gitega. Hari kandi inkuru zivuga ko yari afise abatavuga rumwe na we muri Kiliziya.

Ntitwovuga ko Kiliziya ari yo yatumye Kayoya yicwa — nta kimenyetso gihagije dufise. Ico dushobora kuvuga ni uko yari amaze kugira amatati mu bikorwa vya Kiliziya, hanyuma ibintu vya 1972 bigatangura.

---

Ku wa **13 Rusama 1972**, Kayoya yarafashwe i Gitega.

CVR (Commission Vérité et Réconciliation) ivuga ko, muri ico gihe, abasirikare, abategetsi bo mu ntara no mu makomine, hamwe n'urwaruka rwa JRR n'abandi, bakoreshwa mu gufata abantu — cane cane Abahutu benshi bari bafise amashure, bari mu nzego z'ubuyobozi, mu mashure, canke muri Kiliziya.

Ntitwovuga ko abo bose bagize uruhara mu gufata Kayoya ku giti ciwe, tutabifitiye ibimenyetso vyihariye.

---

Yajanywe mw'ibohero rya Gitega. Yari kumwe n'abandi bantu benshi bari bafashwe — abasaserdoti, abanyeshure, abandi banyagihugu.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP23 — IBOHERO N'URUPFU**, ni ho tuzoraba iminsi yiwe ya nyuma.
