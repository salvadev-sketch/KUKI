# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP08 — ARTÉMON SIMBANANIYE

Inyuma y'igikorwa co gutembagaza ubutegetsi co ku wa 28 Munyonyo 1966, Micombero yamugize **Procureur général de la République**.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Artémon Simbananiye," ikurikiza Weinstein 1976:258-259) na IWACU ("28 novembre 1966: adieu la monarchie") bemeza ko Micombero yamugize Procureur Général ako kanya inyuma ya coup. Wikipedia ivuga ko we na Shibura (umukuru wa Sûreté) bagize ububasha bukomeye ku butegetsi bw'imbere, kandi ko yafashije abandi Batutsi b'i Matana (akarere kiwe) kuronka imyanya minini muri Leta, mu gisirikare no muri UPRONA.

Ku wa 6 Kigarama 1966, amugira **Ministre de la Justice.**

Mu 1967, arahabwa n'ubushobozi ku **Sûreté Nationale** — urwego rw'umutekano rwari rufise uruhara runini mu kugenzura ibibazo vya politike.

---

**Artémon Simbananiye** yari umwe mu bantu abashakashatsi bita **"Groupe de Bururi"** — hamwe na Albert Shibura na Sylvère Sota, bose bakomoka mu karere kamwe, bakaba bafise ubushobozi bukomeye mu gisirikare, muri Leta, no muri UPRONA.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Yaga-Burundi ("Micombero face à l'exercice du pouvoir") ivuga ko igitekerezo co gushiraho CNR cavuye ku bagabo batatu: Simbananiye, Shibura (yize Saint-Cyr, Ubufaransa), na Sota. Wikipedia ("Ikiza") ivuga ko "Groupe de Bururi" ari ijambo ryakoreshejwe n'abashakashatsi ku bakuru b'igihugu bakomoka i Bururi. 🟡 EP08 na EP09 zivuga François Gisamare nk'uwo mu bo batatu b'ibanze aho kuba Sota; amasoko atatandukanya ku bagize uwo murwi w'ibanze.

---

Ariko ntitwokwihutira guhindura "Bururi lobby" ngo ibe nk'ishirahamwe ryanditse rifise abanyamuryango bamenyekanye. Ni ijambo abashakashatsi bakoresha mu gusobanura urunani rw'abantu bafise aho bahurira.

---

Ubucuti hagati ya Micombero na Simbananiye ntibwahora butekanye.

Mu 1967, Simbananiye yarakuwe mu mabanga yiwe, mbere aranafungwa, agirizwa gukoresha nabi amahera ya Leta.

Ivyo vyerekana ko Micombero atari yemera ko abo bari hafi yiwe baba ibigo vy'ububasha bishobora kumurenga.

---

Ariko iyi si iherezo rya Simbananiye.

Azoza kugaruka, mu myaka izokurikira, afise ubushobozi bukomeye kandi kurushiriza.
