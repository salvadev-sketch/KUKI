# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP13 — URUPFU RWA NTARE V

Ku wa **29 Ndamukiza 1972**, hari intureka ikomeye yatanguye mu bice vya Rumonge na Nyanza-Lac, mu bumanuko bw'Uburundi.

Ico gihe nyene, ikibazo ca Ntare V cari kikiri imbere y'ubutegetsi.

---

Hari amasoko avuga ko ubutegetsi bwatanguye kwikeka ko abashaka ubwami bashobora gukoresha iyo ntureka kugira ngo bamubohoze canke basubizeho ingoma.

Ariko ntitwovuga ko Ntare ari we yari inyuma y'iyo ntureka. Isano hagati yiwe n'iyo ntureka ritigeze risobanuka neza.

**Ntare V yishwe ku munsi nyene intureka yo mu 1972 yatangura — ariko ivyo ntibihagije kwemeza ko yari umuyobozi wayo.**

---

Ku wa 29 Ndamukiza 1972, Ntare V yajanywe kuri **Camp Commando de Gitega**.

Umuyobozi w'iyo camp yari Commandant Jérôme Sinduhije, ariko ivyabona vyinshi vyerekana ko atari ahari igihe Ntare yicwa.

Commission Vérité et Réconciliation ivuga ko igikorwa co kumwica cayobowe n'umwungirije wiwe, **Capitaine Onesphore Ntabiraho**, azwi kandi nka **Rwabisazi** — amasasu atatu amukubita mu ruhanga.

---

Ubushakashatsi bumwe bwaje mu nyuma buvuga ko yarashwe n'umugwi w'abasirikare bayobowe na Ntabiraho, atari umuntu umwe gusa.

Ntitwovuga ko Micombero ubwiwe ni we yarashe Ntare. Icodushobora kuvuga: **urupfu rwa Ntare rwabereye mu maboko y'abasirikare b'ubutegetsi bwa Micombero, kandi CVR hamwe n'ubushakashatsi bwakurikiyeho burushiriza guhuza urupfu rwiwe n'ubutegetsi bw'ico gihe.**

---

Umurambo wiwe washizwe mu ngobyi, hanyuma ubikwa mu bubiko bw'ibirwanisho bw'ikambi.

Mu 2023, Agence Burundaise de Presse yatangaje ko imva rusangi bavuga ko umurambo wa Ntare V wajemwo itari bwamenyekane.

**Aho yahambwe: ntiharamenyekana neza.**

---

Umwami wa nyuma w'Uburundi yaraguye, umusi umwe intureka ikomeye cane mu mateka y'igihugu yatanguriye.

Iyo ntureka, na yo, izoba ikimenyetso c'ikintu kigiye kuba kibi cane kurusha ibindi vyose iki gihugu cari kimaze kubona.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP14 — IBIBANZIRIZA N'UBWIGOMEKE BWO KU WA 29 NDAMUKIZA**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
