# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP14 — IBIBANZIRIZA N'UBWIGOMEKE BWO KU WA 29 NDAMUKIZA

1972 ntiyatanguriye bukwi na bukwi.

Uburundi bwari bumaze imyaka burangwa n'ukutizerana gukomeye hagati y'imirwi ya politike n'abo mu nzego z'ubutegetsi — Hutu na Tutsi ku ruhande rumwe, Tutsi-Hima na Tutsi-Banyaruguru ku rundi.

Martin Ndayahoze yari amaze imyaka aburira ubutegetsi ku kaga k'amacakubiri — kandi ubuhanuzi bwiwe bwari bugiye kuba impamo.

---

Ku wa **29 Ndamukiza 1972**, mu gisagara ca **Rumonge**, ku nkengera y'Ikiyaga Tanganyika, hatanguye ubwigomeke burwanya ubutegetsi.

Ubwo bwigomeke bwaciye bukwira mu bindi bibanza vyo mu bumanuko, harimwo **Nyanza-Lac**.

---

Abigometse benshi bari Abahutu.

**Abatutsi benshi barishwe muri ico gihe.** I Bururi na ho, abategetsi ba gisivile n'abasirikare barishwe.

---

Ni bande bari inyuma y'ubwigomeke?

Amazina akunze kuvugwa: Célius Mpasha, Albert Butoyi, Daniel Ndaburiye, Ezechias Biyorero (azwi kandi nka Yussuf Ibrahim). Abenshi muri abo bari baragiye kuba muri Tanzania kuva mu 1969.

Ariko amakuru yizewe ku bateguye ubwo bwigomeke ni make. Ntitwoshobora kuvuga ko uruhara rwa buri wese rwamaze kwemezwa ata mpaka.

---

Mu ntango za Rusama 1972, abigometse barigeze gutangaza ico bita **"Martyazo Republic,"** i Vyanda.

Ariko iyo ntwaro ntiyamaze igihe kirekire. Ingabo za Leta zaratanguye ibikorwa vyo gusubiza inyuma abigometse, kandi mu minsi mikeyi ubwigomeke bwo mu bumanuko bwaratsinzwe.

---

Ku wa 30 Ndamukiza, umusi umwe inyuma y'ubwigomeke butanguye, **Martin Ndayahoze arafashwe, hanyuma arishwe.**

Uwari amaze imyaka aburira ubutegetsi ku kaga kari kaje, ni we nyene uwo kaga kahitanye.

---

Ubwigomeke bwari bumaze gutsindwa.

Ariko ico gikurikira ntikizoguma ku kurwanya abitwaje ibirwanisho.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP15 — INYISHU YA LETA N'UKWAGUKA KW'UBWICANYI**, ni ho tuzoraba icahavuye.
