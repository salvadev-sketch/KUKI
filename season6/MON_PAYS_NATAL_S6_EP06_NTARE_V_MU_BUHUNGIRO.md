# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP06 — NTARE V MU BUHUNGIRO

Umwami yakuwe ku ngoma yagiye mu buhungiro.

Yabaye i Buraya, cane cane mu **Budagi**, i **Munich**.

---

Imyaka itandatu, Ntare V yabaye kure y'igihugu ciwe.

Uburundi, muri ico gihe, bwari bwarabaye Repubulika, Michel Micombero akaba Perezida wayo wa mbere — kandi mu myaka izokurikira, Micombero azogenda yubaka ubutegetsi bushasha, twize muri ma-episode akurikira.

---

Mu ntango za **1972**, Ntare V yaragiye muri **Uganda**, aho yari afise imigenderanire na Perezida Idi Amin Dada.

Kuba yari uwahoze ari umwami, naho atakigira ubutegetsi na bumwe, vyatuma ubutegetsi bwa Micombero bumubona nk'umuntu ashobora kuba ikimenyetso c'abashaka gusubizaho ubwami.

---

Ariko imbere yo kubandanya inkuru ya Ntare V, dukwiye kuraba icabaye mu Burundi mu myaka itandatu yari yamaze mu buhungiro — kuko ni ho tuzosanga ikintu gikomeye kizosobanura ivyo vyose bizoza.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP07 — REPUBULIKA YA MBERE — UBWUBATSI BW'UBUTEGETSI**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
