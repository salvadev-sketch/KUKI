# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP20 — MICHEL KAYOYA: INTANGO

Hari umuntu, mu Burundi bw'imyaka ya 1960 na 1970, yizeraga ko ijambo rishobora gukura igihugu mu bukene bw'ivyiyumviro, atari ubukene bw'amahera gusa.

Yari umusaserdoti.

Ariko yari kandi umwanditsi, umwigisha, n'umuntu wiyumvira ku Burundi bushasha.

---

**Michel Kayoya** yavutse ku wa **8 Kigarama 1934**, i Kibumbu, mu karere kubu kazwi nka Mwaro.

Yize amashure ya Kiliziya Gatolika, anyura mu maseminari ya Mugera na Burasira.

---

Mu **1958**, yagiye mu Bubiligi gukomeza amashure y'ubupatiri na tewolojiya — mu gihe nyene Uburundi bwari mu bihe vy'impinduka zikomeye: imigambwe ya politike ivuka, ibiganiro vy'ubwigenge.

---

Mu **1962**, yasubiye mu Burundi, Uburundi bumaze kwikukira.

Ku wa **8 Mukakaro 1963**, yaronse ubupatiri, mu archidiocèse ya Gitega — afise imyaka hafi 28.

---

Yakoze ibikorwa vya pastorale, harimwo i Rusengo, ashinga Centre culturel Buyogoma, agashira imbere inyigisho n'iterambere ry'urwaruka.

Ntiyabona ubupatiri nk'ugukorera gusa ibikorwa vy'amasengesho.

Yabona ko Kiliziya ikwiye gufasha umuntu: kwiga, gukora, kwiteza imbere, kubana neza n'abandi.
