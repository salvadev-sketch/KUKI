# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP07 — REPUBULIKA YA MBERE — UBWUBATSI BW'UBUTEGETSI

Ku wa 28 Munyonyo 1966, Michel Micombero yakuyeho ubwami, atangaza Repubulika ya mbere y'Uburundi.

Ariko Micombero ntiyaciye yubaka ubutegetsi bushasha nk'ubutegetsi bwa gisivile busanzwe.

---

Ubutegetsi bushasha bwari bushingiye cane ku gisirikare.

Inyuma yo gufata ubutegetsi, Micombero yabaye umutware wa **Conseil National de la Révolution (CNR)**, wari ugizwe n'abasirikare cumi na barindwi, muri bo cumi na babiri bakaba bari Abatutsi.

---

Micombero yashize abasirikare mu mabanga yo kuganza intara — abagavana b'abasirikare, atari abagavana basanzwe.

Ubutegetsi bushasha bwashaka ko igisirikare kiba igikoresho nyamukuru co kugenzura igihugu.

---

**UPRONA**, umugambwe wa Rwagasore, ntiyavuyeho igihe ubwami bwakurwaho.

Ahubwo, uko imyaka yakurikiye yagiye irengana, yarushirije kuba igice nyamukuru c'ubutegetsi — kugeza aho yaje gutangazwa nk'**parti unique**.

---

Rero: Repubulika ya mbere yashinzwe ku murongo umwe: **Micombero → igisirikare → UPRONA → ubutegetsi bushasha.**

Ariko Micombero ntiyari wenyene mu kuyubaka.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP08 — ARTÉMON SIMBANANIYE**, ni ho tuzomenya umwe mu bantu bakomeye bari bamwegereye.
