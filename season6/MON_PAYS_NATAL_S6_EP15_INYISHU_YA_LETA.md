# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP15 — INYISHU YA LETA N'UKWAGUKA KW'UBWICANYI

Ku wa 30 Ndamukiza, ingabo za Leta zatanguye kurwanya abigometse.

Ariko igikorwa nticagarukiye ku kurwanya abitwaje ibirwanisho.

Ibice bimwe vy'ingabo vyatanguye guhiga Abahutu bakekwa kuba baragize uruhara mu bwigomeke — hanyuma ico gikorwa kiraguka, kirenga Bururi, gishika mu ntara zitandukanye z'igihugu.

---

Abasirikare bakuru bari mu kuyobora ibikorwa barimwo: Thomas Ndabememye (Chef d'État-Major), Albert Shibura, André Yanda, na Joseph Rwuri.

**Artémon Simbananiye**, na we, yarahawe ubushobozi bunini cane: ku wa 12 Rusama 1972, yarahawe ubushobozi bwo kuba **ambassadeur itinérant à pouvoirs plénipotentiaires**, akaba yari afise umwidegemvyo munini mu gutunganya ibikorwa vyo mu ntara.

Ntitwovuga ngo Simbananiye ni we wenyene yategetse ubwicanyi bwose. Ico dushobora kuvuga ni uko yari umwe mu bantu bakomeye cane mu gutunganya no gukomeza ibikorwa vyo mu ntara.

---

Igikorwa ntikigumye mu gisirikare gusa. **Jeunesses Révolutionnaires Rwagasore (JRR)**, urwaruka rwafitaniye isano na UPRONA, na rwo rwagize uruhara mu bikorwa vyo gufata abantu no kubica.

---

Hafi abasirikare b'Abahutu **750** bari mu gisirikare ca Burundi bishwe n'abakuru babo b'Abatutsi inyuma y'ubwigomeke.

Muri bo harimwo **Martin Ndayahoze**, uwo twaramaze kubona.

---

Ibikorwa vyibasiye cane abanyapolitike b'Abahutu, abanyeshure, abigisha, abakozi ba Leta, abakozi b'amashure y'amadini, n'abandi bantu bashobora kuba abategetsi b'ejo.

Ni co gituma abashakashatsi bamwe bakoresha imvugo nka **"genocide of Hutu elites"** canke **"selective genocide."**

---

Ubwicanyi bwakomeje amezi menshi, gushika muri Myandagaro.

Igereranyo c'abahitanywe gitandukanye cane bivanye n'isoko: amasoko amwe avuga hafi 200.000, ONU yo mu nyandiko yayo y'inyuma y'imyaka myinshi ishira igereranyo c'abantu nibura **100.000** bishwe, hamwe n'abashika **200.000** bahungiye hanze y'igihugu.

Ntituzokwandika umubare umwe nk'aho ari wo w'ukuri kudashidikanywako.

---

Naho ubwicanyi bw'Abahutu bwabaye bunini cane, ntitukwiye guhanagura ubwicanyi bwakozwe n'abigometse ku Batutsi mu ntango. Ni ibintu bibiri bifitaniye isano, ariko si ikintu kimwe — kandi ubwaguke n'ibihe vyabyo biratandukanye.

---

Uburundi bwari bumaze kubona ikintu kibi kurusha ibindi vyose twari twarize, muri iri tangazo ryose.

Ariko hasigaye ikibazo kimwe gikomeye: uruhara rwa Micombero ubwiwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP16 — URUHARA RWA MICOMBERO UBWIWE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
