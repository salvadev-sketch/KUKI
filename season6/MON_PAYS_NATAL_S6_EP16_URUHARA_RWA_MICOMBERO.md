# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP16 — URUHARA RWA MICOMBERO UBWIWE

Ku wa 29 Ndamukiza 1972, Micombero yasheshye gouvernement yiwe.

Impamvu nyayo y'iyo ngingo ntirasobanuka neza. Bishobora kuba vyari bifitaniye isano n'ugushaka kugira ububasha bwisanzuye bwo guhangana n'ubwigomeke. Ntitwovuga ko yabikoze kubera ko yari amaze gutegeka ubwicanyi — nta kimenyetso gihagije dufise co kubivuga gutyo.

---

Ariko inyuma y'iyo ngingo, Micombero yahavuye aba umuntu mukuru mu gutunganya ibikorwa vy'inyishu ya Leta — kuva ku guhasha Abahutu bakekwa mu Bururi, gushika ku gukuraho abasirikare b'Abahutu mu gisirikare, hanyuma repression iraguka gushika ku gihugu cose.

Abakuru b'ingabo bari hafi yiwe — Ndabememye, Shibura, Yanda, Rwuri — ni bo bari mu gutunganya ibikorwa vya gisirikare. Simbananiye we yari afise umwidegemvyo munini mu ntara.

Ntitwovuga ngo Micombero wenyene ni we yategura buri gikorwa cose. Ariko ntitwovuga na ngo ntaco yari azi.

---

Mu gihe ubwicanyi bwariko buraba, hari abantu bo hanze babibona bakabimenyesha abategetsi babo.

Umudiplomate w'Amerika i Bujumbura, Michael Hoyt, mu butumwa bwo ku wa 26 Rusama 1972, yaranditse ati: **"No respite, no letup. What apparently is genocide continues."**

Ku wa 21 Mukakaro, raporo yavuze ko hafi y'ikibuga c'indege ca Bujumbura hari ibinogo bishasha birimwo imivyimba y'Abahutu.

Abamisiyonari na bo baratangaje ibintu bisa: abantu bafatwa ku bwinshi, bamwe bahambwa bakiri bazima.

---

Ariko hano dutegerezwa kwitonda cane, kuko iki ni co kibazo gikomeye kuruta ibindi vyose muri iyi season:

**Mbega Micombero yatanze itegeko ryanditse ryo kwica Abahutu?**

Nta nyandiko yizewe iriho yerekana ivyo. Ntitwohimba.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga, dushingiye ku masoko:

🟢 Micombero yari Perezida, ubutegetsi bwari mu maboko yiwe, kandi inyishu y'igihugu yaguka mu gihe yari ku butegetsi.

🟢 Abakuru b'ingabo n'abategetsi bari hafi yiwe baragize uruhara mu bikorwa.

🟢 Nta enquête officielle na rimwe yakurikiye ubwo bwicanyi ngo ababukoze bahanwe.

🟡 Uburyo nyabwo abategetsi bakuru b'ingabo bafashe ingingo, hamwe n'ivyo Micombero yamenye ryari, biracari mu bushakashatsi.

🔴 Ntidushobora kwemeza ko ari we wenyene yategetse buri bwicanyi bwose ku giti ciwe.

---

Imvugo ikomeye, ariko yemewe n'ubushakashatsi, ni iyi:

**Micombero yari ku mutwe w'ubutegetsi bwateguye kandi bugashigikira inyishu y'ubwicanyi yakurikiye ubwigomeke bwo mu 1972, kandi abashakashatsi bamushira mu bantu bakuru mu gutunganya no kwagura iyo repression.**

---

Uburundi bwari bwaguye mu kimwe mu bihe bibi kurusha ibindi vyose mu mateka yabwo.

Kandi Micombero yagumye ku ngoma, imyaka ine izokurikira.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP17 — INYUMA YA 1972**, ni ho tuzoraba uko igihugu cagerageje gusubira ku murongo.
