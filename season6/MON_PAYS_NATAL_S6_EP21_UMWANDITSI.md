# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP21 — UMWANDITSI N'UMWIYUMVIRO

Kayoya yasize inyuma ibitabu bibiri bikomeye.

**"Sur les traces de mon père"** — kijanye n'urwaruka rw'Uburundi, kurondera agaciro mu vyo abakurambere basize.

**"Entre deux mondes — Sur la route du développement"** — ivuga ku kibazo Kayoya yagumye yibaza: **umuntu w'Uburundi ashobora gute gutera imbere atatakaje ubuntu n'imico yiwe?**

---

Ntiyabona iterambere nk'ugukwega imigenzo y'Uburaya gusa.

Yashaka iterambere ryongera agaciro k'umuntu — atari amafaranga n'inyubakwa gusa.

---

Hari abamushira mu nkuru z'amacakubiri y'ubwoko. Ariko inyandiko ziwe bwite ntizigaragara nk'izubatswe kuri urwo ruhande — ubushakashatsi bwerekana ko ikibazo c'ubwoko kitigeze kiba umutwe nyamukuru w'ibikorwa vyiwe.

Yari umuntu yandika cane ku muntu, ubukene, iterambere, ubuntu n'igihugu.

---

Kayoya yarakunda cane abantu bo hasi — aboro n'abarimyi. Yashaka ko umwigisha, umurimyi, umukozi, umwana, n'umusaza, bose babona agaciro kabo mu gihugu.

---

Mu ntango za 1972, Kayoya yari amaze kuba umuntu azwi cane muri Kiliziya no mu buzima bw'imibano y'Uburundi.

Kandi yari agifise umugambi w'igitabu ca gatatu — mu Kirundi, ubwaco — kitari kirarangira.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP22 — 1972: GUFATWA**, ni ho tuzoraba icamugezeko.
