# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP02 — NTARE V AKUYE SE KU NGOMA

Uburundi bwari bukiri mu bibazo, inyuma y'ibyabaye mu 1965: umushikiranganji Léopold Biha yari yararashwe muri coup yagerageshejwe, umwami Mwambutsa IV yari yagiye mu buhungiro.

Charles Ndizeye yasubiye mu gihugu, afise umugambi.

---

Ku wa **8 Mukakaro 1966**, Charles Ndizeye yahagaritse Itegeko Nshinga, asheshe reta ya Léopold Biha, aca afata ubutegetsi nk'umukuru w'igihugu.

Ntiyavuze ko ari umuhemu w'ubutegetsi bwa se.

Yavuze ko akoze gutyo kubera ukudashobora kwihanganira ukutaba kwa se, hamwe n'ikibazo c'uko igihugu cari gikeneye reta ihamye.

---

Mu nyuma, aca yimika ubutegetsi bw'ingoma, afata izina rya **Ntare V.**

---

Se, Mwambutsa IV, yari mu buhungiro, ku mugaragaro akiri umwami, uwafashe ingingo azomuserukira.

Ubu, ico kibanza catomotse burundu: Ntare V ni we mwami mukuru.

---

Kandi hari icemezo kimwe cakomeye, cazoza kugira ingaruka zikomeye cane, mu myaka mike gusa: uwo azoshira ku kibanza c'umushikiranganji wa mbere.
