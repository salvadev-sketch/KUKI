# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP19 — UBUHUNGIRO N'URUPFU / ISOZERO

Inyuma yo gukurwa ku butegetsi, Micombero ntiyishwe.

Yaragiye mu buhungiro, muri **Somaliya**.

Ni ho yamaze imyaka yiwe ya nyuma, gushika apfuye mu **1983**.

---

Twamukurikiranye kuva i Rutovu, mu gisirikare c'Ababiligi, gushika ku ntebe y'Umushikiranganji wa mbere w'Ntare V, hanyuma ku ntebe ya Perezida wa Repubulika ya mbere.

Twaramubonye ashinga ubutegetsi bushingiye ku gisirikare no kuri UPRONA, twaramubonye ahangana n'abamurwanya mu gisirikare, hanyuma tugataha mu bihe bibabaje kurusha ibindi vyose: ubwicanyi bwo mu 1972.

---

None se, twomufata dute mu mateka?

Michel Micombero yari umukuru w'igihugu igihe cabaye igihe kibi kurusha ibindi vyose mu mateka ya Repubulika y'Uburundi.

Ntitwovuga ko ari we, ku giti ciwe, yategetse buri gikorwa co kwica umuntu ku muntu. Ariko yari umukuru w'ubutegetsi bwateguye kandi bwashigikiye iyo nyishu, kandi nta na rimwe habaye enquête yaciye imenya abanyabwenge n'abagizi ba nabi bakwiye guhanwa.

---

Aho ni ho amateka y'Uburundi, muri iyi documentaire, agezeko: umwami wa nyuma yishwe, igihugu kigwa mu bwicanyi bunini, Repubulika ya mbere igasozerwa n'igisirikare kongera gufata ubutegetsi.

Ariko urugendo rwa Uburundi ntirwarangiye.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — JEAN-BAPTISTE BAGAZA**, ni ho tuzokomeza.
