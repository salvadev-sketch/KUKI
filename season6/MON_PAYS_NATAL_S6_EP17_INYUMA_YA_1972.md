# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP17 — INYUMA YA 1972

Igihugu cari kimaze kubona ikintu kibi kurusha ibindi vyose.

Ariko Micombero yagumye ku butegetsi, imyaka ine izokurikira.

---

Mu **1974**, hashizweho Itegeko Nshinga rishasha, rigashiraho **UPRONA nk'umugambwe umwe wemewe** mu Burundi.

Ubutegetsi bwose — bwa Leta, bw'umugambwe, n'ubwa gisirikare — bwaragiye burushiriza kwegerana mu maboko ya Micombero.

---

Umuhinga Lemarchand avuga ko, uko imyaka yaja igenda, ukudashirwa hamwe kw'Abahutu mu nzego zikomeye kwagiye kuba ikibazo gikomeye — kimwe mu ntandaro z'ukutumvikana kwaje kurushiriza gukomera kugeza mu 1972 no mu nyuma yaho.

---

Ariko ikibazo gikomeye ntabwo cari hagati ya Micombero n'igihugu gusa.

Ku mpera z'ubutegetsi bwiwe, hari hatanguye amakimbirane, imbere y'igisirikare ubwaco, hagati y'abakuru bakomeye.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP18 — IHIRIKWA RYA MICOMBERO — 1976**, ni ho tuzobibona.
