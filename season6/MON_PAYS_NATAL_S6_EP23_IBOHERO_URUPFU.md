# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP23 — IBOHERO N'URUPFU

Mw'ibohero rya Gitega, abari kumwe na Kayoya bavuze ko yagumye atekanye, ataboneka afise ubwoba.

Yararemesha abandi. Yarabatera akamo ko gusenga no kuririmba.

---

Umwe mu banyeshure bari bafashwe hamwe na we yashoboye guhunga mw'ibohero ku musi w'ubwicanyi. Uwo muntu ni we yatanze ubuhamya bwinshi ku vyabaye — avuga ko Kayoya yashoboye guhuriza hamwe abandi mu ndirimbo.

---

Ubuhamya buvuga ko Kayoya yaguma avuga amajambo yerekeza ku Mana: **"Tugende mu Ngoro kwa Data."**

Ayo majambo ntiyari indirimbo gusa — yari uburyo Kayoya yakoresha ukwemera kwiwe kugira aremeshe abandi bari bazi ko bari mu kaga.

---

Ku wa **17 Rusama 1972**, Michel Kayoya yishwe — amaze imisi ine ari mw'ibohero, kuva yafashwe ku wa 13.

CVR na Archidiocèse de Bujumbura bombi bashigikira iyo tariki, naho amasoko amwe ya kera avuga 15 Rusama.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Amasoko menshi y'inyuma (Wikipedia y'Icitaliyano, musabyimana.net, Soumbala.com) avuga ko yishwe ku wa 15 Rusama 1972. Iki gitandukaniro (15 canke 17) ntigishobotse gukemuka muri ubu bushakashatsi; tuvuga gusa ko aba ari mu minsi mike y'inyuma yo gufatwa (13 Rusama).

---

Hari ubuhamya buvuga ko yaririmvye Magnificat mu masaha ya nyuma, akongera avuga amajambo y'ikigongwe ku bari bagiye kumwica. Hari n'ubuhamya buvuga ko abasirikare bamurashe bariko bararira.

Ivyo ni **ubuhamya bw'ababibonye** — tubibika uko biri, atari nk'inyandiko y'ubuyobozi y'ico gihe canke nk'ikintu twiboneye ubwacu.

---

Umubiri wiwe wajanywe ku kibanza kiri hafi y'uruzi **Ruvubu**, ku mutumba wa Bukirasazi, mu karere ka Nyabibuye, komine Shombo, intara ya Karusi.
