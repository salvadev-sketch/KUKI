# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP11 — 1970–1971: BUMANUKO NA BANYARUGURU

Mu 1970, Micombero yarongeye kugerageza gusubiza hamwe igihugu.

Yaratangaje politike yo gusubiza hamwe Abahutu n'Abatutsi — inyuma y'igikomere ibihano vya 1969 vyari vyasize.

---

Ariko Ndayahoze, umwe mu bantu bake bari bashobora guhuza ubutegetsi n'igice c'Abahutu, yaratakaje ubushobozi.

Mu Kigarama 1970, ubutegetsi bwaramukuye ku buyobozi bwa UPRONA. Muri Ntwarante 1971, aravanwa no muri guverinoma.

---

Ariko ikibazo kikomeye cane co mu 1971 ntabwo cari Abahutu vs Abatutsi gusa.

Hari kandi ikindi kibazo, kiduka imbere y'Abatutsi ubwabo: **Abatutsi bo mu bumanuko** (aho benshi mu bategetsi bakomeye ba Repubulika bakomoka) **vs Abatutsi bo mu buraruko, bazwi nka Banyaruguru.**

---

Abantu bamwe bo mu nzego zo hejuru, harimwo abahoze ari abategetsi n'abasirikare, baragirijwe umugambi wo kurwanya canke guhirika ubutegetsi — abo bakekwa benshi bari Banyaruguru.

Ku wa 14 Nzero 1972, tribunal militaire yaraciriye urwo gupfa abasirikare bane b'Abanyaruguru n'abasivile batanu, abandi barindwi baciriwe umunyororo w'ubuzima bwose.

---

Ku wa 4 Ruhuhuma 1972, Micombero yaragiriye imbabazi abantu icenda muri bo, harimwo abaminisitiri babiri bahoze mu migenderanire n'amahanga.

Ivyo vyerekana ko Micombero atari ku murongo umwe wo kwica bose.

---

Ariko igihugu cari kimaze kuba icifuza, gikeneye ikintu kimwe gito gusa kugira kirundure.

Kandi icarangwa nyene, kizoboneka kure y'ubutegetsi bwo hagati — mu bumanuko, ku nkengera y'Ikiyaga Tanganyika.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP12 — NTARE V AGARUKA — 1972**, ni ho tuzoraba icakurikiye ku wahoze ari umwami.
