# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP09 — ALBERT SHIBURA NA FRANÇOIS GISAMARE

**Albert Shibura** yari umusirikare akomeye, yabaye umuyobozi wa Sûreté Nationale.

Amasoko amushira hamwe na Simbananiye nk'abantu bari bafise uruhara runini mu bibazo vy'igihugu mu ntango z'ubutegetsi bwa Micombero.

---

**François Gisamare** na we ari mu mazina atatu y'ingenzi ahuza na "Groupe de Bururi."

Abo bantu batatu bari bafise inkomoko ya Tutsi-Hima, kandi bari mu bantu bakomeye bari bagize uwo murwi.

---

Ariko ubwo bushobozi ntibwabakingiye igihe cose.

Mu 1967, Albert Shibura na we yarakuwe ku buyobozi bwa Sûreté Nationale, ku wa 7 Rusama, ahabwa umwanya w'umujanama wihariye wa Perezida — icubahiro kiri hejuru, ariko kiri kure y'ububasha nyakuri.

---

Mu Munyonyo 1967, Micombero yahinduye guverinoma. Iryo hinduka ryari rifise intumbero yo gutuma habaho **"ouverture politique"** hamwe n'umugambi wo kongera uruhara rw'Abahutu.

---

Ico 1967 itwigisha ni iki: Micombero yari agerageza kugumana ububasha bwo hejuru, no gucungera abo bari hafi yiwe, naho bamwe muri bo bari bafise ubushobozi bukomeye.

Ntitwovuga ko ivyo vyose vyabaye ku mvo imwe gusa.

---

Ariko ikindi kibazo kikomeye kirakurikira — atari mu bakuru b'ubutegetsi, ariko mu bwoko bw'abagirizwa umugambi wo kurwanya Leta.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 6 — EP10 — 1969: UMUGAMBI WO GUHIRIKA UBUTEGETSI NA MARTIN NDAYAHOZE**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
