# MON PAYS NATAL — ABANTU 100 BAGIZE AMATEKA Y'UBURUNDI
## EPISODE 1B — JABWE NA NSORO

**Igitigiri:** Episode 1B (igice gikurikira Ntwero, imbere ya Cambarantama/Ntare Rushatsi)
**Umutwe nyamukuru:** Ubwami bugabuwe hagati y'abahungu babiri — kandi guhemuka kugatera ivuka ry'umwami mushasha
**Ururimi:** Kirundi
**Ibipimo:** 🟢 Ikimenyamenya gikomeye · 🟡 Imigenzo mvugo/disputed · 🔴 Ntibihamye

---

### IGICE 1 — INTANGAMARARA (Bridge kuva kuri Episode 1A)

**[VISUAL: Amazina Ntwero, Jabwe, Nsoro agaragara ku mugaragaro umwe umwe, hanyuma Jabwe na Nsoro bakagumaho]**

**NARATION:**

Muri Episode 1A, twaboneye Ntwero — umwe mu bimenyetso vy'uko imbere ya Ntare Rushatsi hariho utwigenge twinshi, atari igihugu kimwe.

None uyu musi, turaraba abahungu biwe babiri: **Nsoro na Jabwe**. Kuko ari bo, mu migenzo, bagabuwe ubwami bwa se — kandi ni hagati yabo ho havutse umwana uzoba Ntare Rushatsi ubwiwe.

---

### IGICE 2 — UBWAMI BUGABUWE (🟡)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata y'akarere ka Nkoma igabanurwamwo ibice bibiri]**

**NARATION:**

Nk'uko imigenzo ya Ruvugazinaniwe ibivuga — iyo nkuru yavuzwe kabiri, mu 1954 no mu 1979, itagira impinduka nyinshi hagati y'ivyo bihe bibiri — Ntwero yatanguje ubwami, hanyuma ubwo bwami buragabuka hagati y'abahungu biwe babiri: Nsoro na Jabwe.

Uku kugabuka ntibisobanura ngo bombi baraganje mu mahoro. Ni imbere y'ico kigabuka ho hazovuka ikibazo gikomeye kizoshira ku barongoye ingoma.

🟡 **Ico dushobora kuvuga:** Imigenzo yose ihuriza ku vyo Nsoro na Jabwe bari abahungu ba Ntwero, bagabuwe ubwami. Ariko uburyo nyakuri iyo migabane yakozwe — ni ingoma imwe igabuwemwo mu turere tubiri, canke ni ubutware butandukanye — ntibisobanuwe neza mu masoko dufise.

---

### IGICE 3 — GUHEMUKA: UMWANA W'IGISHIKA (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Igishushanyo cebe cerekana umuryango — nta ma-portrait, ikarata gusa]**

**NARATION:**

Aha ni ho inkuru itangura gukomera. Hagati ya Jabwe n'umugore wa Nsoro, havuka umwana. Uyu mwana ni **umwana w'igishika** — ni ukuvuga, umwana avutse mu buryo budasanzwe, mu buhemu bw'umuvyeyi umwe ku wundi.

Uyu mwana ni we mu nyuma azitwa **Cambarantama** — uwo, nk'uko tumaze kubona muri Episode 1A, azoba Ntare Rushatsi.

🟢 **Ico twemeza (citation ihamye):** Mu gitabo *Burundi: L'histoire retrouvée* ca Jean-Pierre Chrétien, iki gikorwa cerekanwa nk'igikorwa ca gatatu mu rukurikirane rw'inkuru: *"la naissance d'un enfant, fils adultérin de Jabwe et de la femme de Nsoro."* — bisobanura: "ivuka ry'umwana, umwana w'igishika hagati ya Jabwe n'umugore wa Nsoro." (Hafi ya paji 37-38 mu PDF dufise; birakenewe kwemeza urupapuro nyarwo mu gitabo canditse.)

🟡 **Ico dutandukanya:** Iki ni igikorwa co mu migenzo mvugo (Ruvugazinaniwe, 1954 na 1979), kirimwo mu "cycle du Nkoma" — atari raporo y'ivyabaye ku itariki n'ahantu bihamye.

---

### IGICE 4 — IMPAMVU IYI GIHEMU IRAKOMEYE MU NKURU (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Igishushanyo c'imigenzo y'akarere ka Ibiyaga Bigari — Ntare atari wenyene mu bwoko bw'iyi nkuru]**

**NARATION:**

Iyi mice y'ubuhemu ntabwo ari iy'Uburundi bwonyene. Chrétien avuga ko iyi mice — umwana avutse mu buhemu, agasubira kuba umwami — ari mu **mwidudu w'imigenzo y'ibwami ikwiragiye mu karere kose k'Ibiyaga Bigari**, harimwo n'imigenzo ya Cwezi (Uganda, Rwanda).

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Iyi nkuru y'ivuka ry'umwami biciye ku buhemu si ivumburwa ry'Uburundi bwonyene, ariko ni igice c'urwego rw'imigenzo y'akarere kose ku vyerekeye kuvuka kw'abami.

🟡 **Ico dutandukanya:** Ivyo ntibisobanura ko inkuru ya Nsoro na Jabwe ari "impfano" y'iyindi migenzo — ishobora kuba yavuye ku vyabaye nyabyo mu buryo bwihariye bw'akarere ka Nkoma, hanyuma bigahuza n'imigenzo y'ahandi.

---

### IGICE 5 — CAMBARANTAMA ARAHUNGA (Bridge kuri Episode 1C)

**[VISUAL: Umuhungu w'umwana ahagaze ku mupaka w'akarere, ubuseruko/ubuseshero]**

**NARATION:**

Uwo mwana w'igishika ntiyashoboye kuguma mu karere yavukiyemwo. Nk'uko imigenzo ibivuga, **Cambarantama arahunga**, aja kwa tante wiwe — Namabuye, mushiki wa Jabwe na Nsoro, uwari yararongowe na Ruhaga, umwami w'i Buha mu Bumanuko. (Uku kugenda kwakozwe ku bufasha bwa Jabwe ubwiwe n'abapfumu bamufashije.)

Ni ng'aho, ku Buha, ho inkuru izokwongera guhinduka — kuko na ho azohura n'ikindi kibazo c'urupfu n'intambara.
