# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP01 — MUTAGA IV MBIKIJE: INTANGO

Ku wa 21 Myandagaro 1908, umwami yari amaze hafi igice c'ikinjana ategeka yarapfuye.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Wikipedia ("List of kings of Burundi") ivuga ko Mwezi IV Gisabo (c. 1840–1908) yategetse "c. 1850 – 21 August 1908," kandi ko Mutaga IV Mbikije (c. 1892–1915) yamusubiye "21 August 1908 – 30 November 1915" — bihuza neza n'itariki n'imyaka EP01 ivuga.

Uwamusubiriye ntiyari umugabo mukuru, ufise ubunyaruka bwo gutwara igihugu kinini se yari yaragusanye.

Yari umusore w'imyaka nka cumi na itanu.

---

Yitwa **Mutaga IV Mbikije**.

Se yari **Mwezi IV Gisabo Bikata-Bijoga** — uwo twize mu Season 3, umwami yaguye imyaka mirongo itanu ategeka, arwanya abamurwanya bo mu muryango wiwe, ahangana n'Abadagi, agasinya Amasezerano ya Kiganda, akongera agasubizwa kumenyekana.

Uwo mwami akomeye, agapfa, agasiga ubwami ku mwana.

---

Igihe yavutse, ntabwo tuzi neza.

Amasoko menshi amushira hafi y'imyaka ya 1890, naho hari isoko rimwe ritanga 1903 — ridahuye n'ayandi. Kuri iyi documentaire, ntituzoshira itariki y'ivuka nk'ukuri kutavuguruzwa: **Mutaga IV yavukiye hafi y'imyaka ya 1890, akiri muto cane igihe yaja ku ngoma mu 1908.**

---

Nyina, na we, ni ikibazo.

Amasoko atandukanye atanga amazina atandukanye — Ririkumutima, canke Ntibanyiha Bizama. Ntitwovanga abo babiri, kandi ntitwoshira umwe muri bo nk'ukuri kudashidikanywako, imbere yo kubona neza uko ivyo bigenda.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe ni ikintu kimwe: **umwami mushasha yari akiri umwana.**

Kandi umwana ntashobora gutegeka wenyene.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP02 — RIRIKUMUTIMA NA NTIBANYIHA**, ni ho tuzoraba abari bafise ubushobozi bwo kuyobora igihugu mu izina ryiwe.
