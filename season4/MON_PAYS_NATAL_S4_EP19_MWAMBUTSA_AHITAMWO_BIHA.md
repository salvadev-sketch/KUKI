# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP19 — MWAMBUTSA AHITAMWO BIHA

Igihe amatora yo mu Rusama 1965 abaye, Uburundi bwari bumaze kugira umushikiranganji: **Joseph Bamina**, Umuhutu, yari amaze imyaka itari mike ku murimo, atangura ku wa 26 Nzero 1965 — iminsi mikeya gusa inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ngendandumwe.

Bamina rero ntiyari umukandida mushasha inyuma y'amatora — yari asanzwe ari we mushikiranganji, akomeza umurimo wiwe naho amatora abaye.

---

Naho abakandida b'Abahutu bari bararonse imyanya myinshi mu Nteko (21 kuri 33), ivyo ntibyahinduye ubutegetsi bw'igihe cose: Bamina yagumye ku murimo amezi menshi inyuma y'amatora.

Ariko ku wa **30 Nzeri 1965**, Bamina yavuwe ku mwanya wiwe.

---

Mwambutsa IV, muri iyo minsi, yahisemwo **Léopold Biha** — Ganwa, afitaniye isano ikomeye n'ibwami — ngo abe umushikiranganji mushasha, ku wa **13 Ukwakira 1965**.

Iki cemezo carashavuza abatari bake mu Bahutu: inyuma y'intsinzi yabo mu matora, ubutegetsi bwaguma mu maboko y'umuntu udakomoka muri bo.

---

Ariko ntitwoshaka gucira urubanza vy'ubusa, nk'uko twari twarabikoze mbere. Inzira yo guhitamwo Biha ntiyari iy'igitutu c'umwami gusa: yakurikiye amezi y'amakimbirane hagati y'imigwi itandukanye ya UPRONA, inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ngendandumwe.

---

Ariko naho ico cemezo casobanuwe gute, ingaruka zaco ntizateganijwe — kandi Bamina ubwiwe, uwavuwe ku mwanya gusa, ntiyari yaragera ku iherezo ry'urugendo rwiwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP20 — COUP YA GITUGUTU 1965**, ni ho tuzobibona.
