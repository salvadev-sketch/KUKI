# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP19 — MWAMBUTSA AHITAMWO BIHA

Naho abakandida b'Abahutu bari bararonse imyanya myinshi, Mwambutsa IV yahisemwo **Léopold Biha** ngo abe umushikiranganji wa mbere.

Biha yari Ganwa, afitaniye isano ikomeye n'ibwami.

---

Iki cemezo carashavuza abatari bake mu Bahutu, kuko babona ko amajwi y'amatora atari yahindutse ububasha bwo kuyobora reta.

---

Ariko ntitwoshaka gucira urubanza vy'ubusa, nk'uko twari twarabikoze mbere.

Isoko ry'ingenzi ryerekana ko ibintu vyari bigoye kurusha uko byari byavuzwe: mbere y'uko Biha atorwa, amazina ya **Joseph Bamina** na **Gervais Nyangoma** yagiye mu biganiro vyo gushinga reta — bombi bakaba batemeye canke batabashije.

Rero: **inyuma y'amatora yo mu 1965, Mwambutsa IV yagize ingorane zo gushinga reta nshasha, maze Léopold Biha aba umushikiranganji wa mbere** — atari ikintu coroshe c'igitutu c'umwami gusa.

---

Ariko naho ico cemezo casobanuwe gute, ingaruka zaco ntizateganijwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP20 — COUP YA GITUGUTU 1965**, ni ho tuzobibona.
