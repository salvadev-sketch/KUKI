# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP06 — AMASHURE

Umwami w'ejo hazoza yatanguye ubuzima bwiwe yigishwa mu buryo bubiri butandukanye.

Ubwa mbere, inyigisho za kera z'Uburundi — izo abami bagiye bahabwa kuva kera na kera.

Ubwa kabiri, inyigisho z'Ababiligi.

---

Mu **1925**, Ababiligi barashizeho ishure i **Muramvya**, kugira ngo umuganwa Mwambutsa aronke inyigisho z'ishure.

Mu **1927**, hashinzwe mission gatolika i **Bukeye**, ifise umugambi utoroshye: kwigisha Mwambutsa, ariko na none kumwinjiza mu bukatolika.

---

Ariko ico gikorwa ca kabiri ntikigezweko.

Amasoko avuga ko Mwambutsa atabaye umunyeshure w'umwete cane, kandi ko **atigeze ahinduka umukatolika**.

Iyi ni imwe mu nkuru zishimishije: Umuganwa yigishwa n'abamisiyonari, ariko akaguma ku vyo yari afise, adasubiye inyuma imbere y'ivyo bashaka.

---

Nyuma yaho, azoshaka umugore w'umukristu. Ariko ivyo ntibisobanura ko Mwambutsa ubwiwe yabaye Umukatolika. Ni ibintu bibiri bitandukanye, kandi tudakwiye kubivanga.

---

Umuganwa akura.

Kandi hari umunsi umwe uzoza, aho regency izorangira, umuganwa akaba umwami mu buryo bwuzuye.
