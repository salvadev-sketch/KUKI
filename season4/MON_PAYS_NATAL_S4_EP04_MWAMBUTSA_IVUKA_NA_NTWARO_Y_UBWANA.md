# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP04 — MWAMBUTSA IV: IVUKA NA NTWARO Y'UBWANA

Yavutse ari **Bangiricenge**, ku wa 6 Rusama 1912, ahantu amasoko amwe yita Nyabiyogi (canke Nyabitogi).

Se yari Mutaga IV Mbikije. Nyina, amasoko menshi amwita **Ngenzahago** (canke Ngenzahayo), umuganwakazi wo mu bwoko bwa Munyagisaka — naho amazina y'ahantu n'imyandikire biratandukanye gatoya mu masoko, ata co bihindura ku nkuru nyamukuru.

---

Se, Mutaga IV, amaze gupfa ku wa 30 Munyonyo 1915, Bangiricenge yaciye ashirwa ku ngoma.

Ku wa 16 Kigarama 1915, yarimikwa aba **Mwambutsa IV**.

Yari afise imyaka itatu gusa.

---

Umwana w'imyaka itatu ntashobora gutwara igihugu.

Ni co gituma, nka se imbere yiwe, habayeho **regency**.

**Ririkumutima** — uwo twari twaramaze kubona mu ngoma ya Mutaga — yagumye ari umwe mu bantu bakomeye muri ubwo butegetsi bw'ubwana. Ntitwovanga Ririkumutima na nyina wa Mwambutsa — ni bantu babiri batandukanye, naho bombi bakomotse mu muryango umwe w'ubwami.

---

Ubu, Uburundi bwari bufise umwami wa gatatu akurikirana ategekwa n'abandi kubera ubwana — Mwezi Gisabo, Mutaga IV, na ubu Mwambutsa IV.

Ariko iyi ngoma, ku bwabo bose, izoba iyirekire kurusha izindi zose.

Kandi mu gihe iryo bwana rizoba ricungwa n'abandi, hari ikintu kimwe kikomeye kigiye guhindura Uburundi burundu — kikaza atari kuvuye mu ngoma ubwayo, ariko kuvuye kure, mu ntambara y'Uburayi ubwaco.
