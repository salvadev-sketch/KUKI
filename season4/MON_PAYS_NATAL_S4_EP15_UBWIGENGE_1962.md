# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP15 — UBWIGENGE — 1 MUKAKARO 1962

Ku wa 1 Mukakaro 1962, Uburundi bwararonse ubwigenge.

Ariko iyi ntsinzi yashitse, umuhungu wari yarayiharaniye kurusha abandi ntiyari akiriho kuyibona.

---

Uburundi bwabaye **ubwami bugendera ku mategeko-shingiro** — constitutional monarchy — Mwambutsa IV akaba umukuru w'igihugu.

Mwambutsa rero si umwami w'igihe c'ubukoloni gusa.

Ni na we mwami igihe Uburundi bwikukira.

---

Yari umwami:

mu gihe c'Abadagi,

hanyuma mu gihe c'Ababiligi,

hanyuma mu bwigenge bw'Uburundi.

Imyaka mirongo itanu, ibihe bitatu bitandukanye.

---

Ariko ubwigenge ntibwasobanuye ko ibibazo vya politike vyari biheze.

Ahubwo, ibintu vyari bigiye guhinduka mu buryo butoroshye kurusha uko byari byigeze kuba.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP16 — INYUMA Y'UBWIGENGE — UPRONA IRACAMWO IBICE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
