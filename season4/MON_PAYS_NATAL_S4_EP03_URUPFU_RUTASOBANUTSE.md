# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP03 — URUPFU RUTASOBANUTSE

Mu 1912, ikirimba c'ubwami carimuwe.

Kuva kera na kera, ubwami bwari bufise ahantu hazwi — Muramvya, Mbuye, Gishora.

Ubu, umurwa mushasha ni **Gitega**.

---

Ingoma ya Mutaga IV yaranzwe n'ivyo bishasha: ukwimuka kwa résidence royale i Gitega, ukwiyongera kw'amafaranga mashasha y'amanyaburaya — **roupie na heller** — n'ukwiyongera kw'abamisiyonari.

Uburundi bwari bugenda buva ku ngoma y'imigenzo, bujya mu buryo bushasha bw'ubutegetsi.

---

Ariko ingoma ya Mutaga IV ubwayo yari ngufi.

**1908 — 1915.**

Imyaka indwi gusa — bitandukanye cane n'imyaka mirongo itanu za se, Mwezi Gisabo.

---

Ku wa **30 Munyonyo 1915**, Mutaga IV yarapfuye.

Ariko igituma yapfuye ntikizwi.

Hari **inkuru ebyiri zitandukanye**.

Iya mbere ivuga ko yagiranye amakimbirane n'umuvukanyi wiwe, **Bangura**, akomerekera muri ayo makimbirane, hanyuma agapfa.

Iya kabiri ivuga ko yapfuye azize **malaria**, inyuma yo kwinjira mu **Murangara**, mu kibira ca Kibara.

---

Nta gihamya gikomeye gitandukanya izo nkuru zombi.

Ku bw'ivyo, kuri iyi documentaire, ntituzemeza imwe muri zo nk'ukuri: **Mutaga IV yapfuye ku wa 30 Munyonyo 1915 — ariko igituma yapfuye kigumye kitasobanutse.**

---

Umubiri wiwe wahambwe i **Ramvya**, muri zone Nkonge — ahantu tuzi neza, bitandukanye n'abami ba kera aho amakuru make gusa yatugezeko.

---

Uwamusubiriye ni umuhungu wiwe.

Na we, akiri umwana.

Ariko iyo ngoma, itandukanye cane n'iy'iwe se, izomara **imyaka irenga mirongo itanu** — igihe cirekire kurusha izo twize kugeza ubu, muri iri tangazo rya Uburundi.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP04 — MWAMBUTSA IV: IVUKA NA NTWARO Y'UBWANA**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
