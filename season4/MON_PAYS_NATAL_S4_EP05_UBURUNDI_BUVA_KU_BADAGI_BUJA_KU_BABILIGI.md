# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP05 — UBURUNDI BUVA KU BADAGI BUJA KU BABILIGI

Mwambutsa yari amaze umwaka umwe gusa ku ngoma, igihe Uburayi bwose bwinjiye mu ntambara.

Intambara ya Mbere y'Isi Yose.

Ibwami rya Uburundi, ryari ryarahoze ari igice c'Ubudagi bw'Afrika y'Uburasirazuba, ryari rigiye guhinduka rikagirwa.

---

Mu **1916**, Ababiligi barafashe Uburundi.

Uburundi bwahavuye buva ku butegetsi bw'Abadagi, buja ku bw'Ababiligi.

---

Iyi mpinduka ntiyari iyo ku ngoma yonyene: yari impinduka ku gihugu cose.

Kuva ubu, ni Ababiligi bazogira uruhara runini mu Burundi — kugeza igihe cizoza cy'ubwigenge, imyaka irenga mirongo ine iri imbere.

---

Mwambutsa IV rero, akiri umwana, yari amaze kubaho mu buhinduke bw'abakoroni babiri:

**Ubudagi**, mu ntango y'ubuzima bwiwe.

**Ububiligi**, uko azokura, ategeka, akageza igihugu ku bwigenge.

---

Ariko ivyo bizoza vyose bikiri kure.

Muri iyo myaka, umwana yari akigenda akura munsi ya regency, ata co ari we ubwiwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP06 — AMASHURE**, ni ho tuzobona uburyo Ababiligi bamwigishije.
