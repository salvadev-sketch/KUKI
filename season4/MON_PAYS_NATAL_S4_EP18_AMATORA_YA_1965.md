# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP18 — AMATORA YA 1965

Ku wa 10 Rusama 1965, habaye amatora mashasha y'Inteko.

Aya yari ayambere kuva Uburundi bubaye igihugu cigenga.

---

Inteko yari ifise imyanya 33.

UPRONA yararonse 21.

Abakandida b'Abahutu bararonse umubare munini w'imyanya.

---

Aha ni ho ikibazo gikomeye catanguye.

Kuko amajwi y'amatora, mu vyiyumviro vy'abenshi, yagombye gushira umuntu runaka ku ntebe y'umushikiranganji wa mbere.

Ariko Mwambutsa yari afise ubushobozi bwo guhitamwo, kandi ico bushobozi bwiwe cari giteza umwuka mubi.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP19 — MWAMBUTSA AHITAMWO BIHA**, ni ho tuzobibona.
