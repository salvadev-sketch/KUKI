# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP24 — IKIBAZO C'IMVA / ISOZERO

Umwami yari yarasavye ko ahambwa mu Busuwisi.

Yahambwe i Meyrin.

Ariko Uburundi ntibwabyemeye burundu.

---

Mu **2012**, ibisigarira vya Mwambutsa IV vyaracukuwe, hagerageza kubirungika mu Burundi.

Ariko habaye impaka z'amategeko n'umuryango wiwe.

Maze mu **2016**, bisubizwa mu mva, mu Busuwisi.

---

Mu **2017**, urubanza rwarangiye — Mwambutsa asigara ari mu mva yiwe ya Meyrin.

Ivyo vyerekana ko, n'inyuma y'imyaka mirongo ine y'urupfu rwiwe, ikibazo c'aho umwami akwiye kuruhukira cabandanije guteza impaka — hagati y'igihugu yategetse, umuryango wiwe, n'ivyifuzo yasize.

---

Rero, none, twomufata dute mu mateka?

Mwambutsa IV Bangiricenge ntabwo ari umuntu twovuga gusa ngo "yabaye umwami kuva 1915 gushika 1966."

Ni umuntu wabayeho mu bihe bitatu bikomeye: Ubudagi, Ububiligi, n'ubwigenge.

Yatakaje umuhungu wiwe w'ingenzi mu vya politike (Rwagasore), abona igihugu ciwe kigwa mu bwicanyi bw'amoko, akuwe ku ngoma n'uwundi muhungu wiwe, hanyuma apfa kure y'igihugu ciwe, mu buhungiro.

Kandi n'inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe, umubiri wiwe ubwawo wabaye ikibazo c'ihiganwa hagati y'igihugu n'umuryango.

---

Ubwami bw'Uburundi bwarangiye muri we, na we.

Ariko urugendo rw'Uburundi ntirwarangiye.
