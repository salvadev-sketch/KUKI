# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP11 — UPRONA IRASHINGWA

Mwambutsa ntiyari wenyene mu rugamba rwo kwikukira.

Kandi imbere y'uko urwo rugamba rutangura, hari umurwi tugomba kwibuka: **Bezi na Batare** — imigwi ibiri y'abaganwa, twize inkomoko yayo mu Season 3, twavuze ko yavuye ku matati ya Ndivyariye.

Mu myaka ya 1950, iyo migwi yari ikiriho, igifise uruhara mu vya politike.

---

Ubushakashatsi bwa Christine Deslaurier ku Burundi bwerekana ko hagati ya 1956 na 1961 habaye ihinduka rikomeye ry'imigambwe n'imigwi ya politike.

Mu **1958**, hashinzwe **UPRONA — Union pour le Progrès National.**

Iyi mugambwe uzoba ukomeye cane mu rugamba rwo gusaba ubwigenge.

---

Kandi mu bayobozi bayo bakomeye, hari **Umuganwa Louis Rwagasore** — umuhungu wa Mwambutsa IV.

Ariko aha hari ikintu tugomba kwitonda: ntitwovuga ngo "Mwambutsa IV ni we yayoboye UPRONA." Oya.

UPRONA yari ifise abayobozi bayo bwite, kandi Rwagasore ni umwe muri bo. Mwambutsa yari **Mwami**, kandi ikibanza ciwe cari gitandukanye n'ic'umukuru w'umugambwe.

---

Ubushakashatsi bumwe buvuga ko Rwagasore yari mu mugwi wa Bezi, mu gihe Batare yari ifise ubundi buyobozi n'ubufatanye n'ubutegetsi bw'abakoroni.

Ni ukuvuga: n'urugamba rwo kwikukira ubwarwo, rwagumye rufitaniye isano n'amashami y'ibwami yavuye kera, muri Season 3.

---

**[CITATION — 🟢/🟡]**

Warren Weinstein (1976, biciye kuri Wikipedia "Union for National Progress") yemeza ko UPRONA yari, mu ntango, ifitaniye isano ikomeye n'umugwi wa Bezi — bihuye n'ivyavuzwe hejuru. Ubwo bufatanye ntibwaguma: bwararanguwe inyuma y'amakimbirane hagati ya Rwagasore na se, Mwambutsa.

Ku bijanye n'itariki UPRONA yashingiweko: nta ntumbero imwe rukumbi ihari mu bahinga. Leopold Biha na René Lemarchand (1970) bavuga 1957; Ellen Eggers (2006) avuga impera y'imyaka ya 1950; Ludo De Witte (2021) avuga inama za mbere za Nzero-Gitugutu 1958; naho guverineri Jean-Paul Harroy avuga impera y'1959. Christine Deslaurier na yo (biciye kuri Cairn.info) avuga "créé à la fin de l'année 1958" — ni yo dukurikije aha, ariko ikwiye gufatwa nk'imwe mu tariki zishoboka, atari nk'ukuri kutavuguruzwa.
