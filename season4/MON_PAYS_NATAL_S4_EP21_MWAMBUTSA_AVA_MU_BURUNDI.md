# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP21 — MWAMBUTSA AVA MU BURUNDI

Inyuma y'ivyo vyose, Mwambutsa IV yagiye mu buhungiro, mu Busuwisi.

Ntiyari kuzosubira gutwara Uburundi ari ku butaka bw'Uburundi.

---

Mu **Ntwarante 1966**, yahaye umuhungu wiwe **Charles Ndizeye** ububasha bwo kumuserukira mu gihugu.

Charles Ndizeye yaciye afata izina ry'ingoma:

**Ntare V.**

---

Ariko Mwambutsa, ku mugaragaro, yari akiri umwami.

Yari yasize umuhungu wiwe amuserukira, atari amuha ingoma burundu.

---

Uwo mwanya utomoye — se ari umwami, umuhungu akora mu izina ryiwe — ntiwari kumara igihe kirekire.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP22 — NTARE V ATEMBAGAZA SE — IHEREZO RY'UBWAMI**, ni ho tuzobibona.
