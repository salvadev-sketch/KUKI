# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP08 — UMUGORE WIWE

Ku wa 24 Kigarama 1930, Mwambutsa IV yubatse.

Umugore wiwe yitwa **Thérèse Kanyonga**, avuga ko yari uwo mu muryango w'Abasine, kandi ari umukristukazi w'umukatolika.

---

Bararonse abana.

Muri bo, bane bazoba ingenzi cane muri iyi nkuru:

**Louis Rwagasore.**

**Rosa Paula Iribagiza.**

**Régine Kanyange.**

**Charles Ndizeye**, uwaje kuba **Ntare V**.

---

Muri abo bane, ni **Louis Rwagasore** azoba, mu myaka izokurikira, umuntu w'ingenzi cane mu rugamba rwo gusaba ubwigenge bw'Uburundi.

Kandi ni **Charles Ndizeye** azoba umwami wa nyuma w'Uburundi — kandi azofata iyo ngoma, biciye ku buryo budasanzwe, avuye ku se ubwiwe.

---

Ariko ivyo vyose bikiri kure.

Ubu, Mwambutsa afise umuryango — kandi Uburundi, na bwo, buri hafi guhinduka.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP09 — UBUTEGETSI BW'ABABILIGI**, ni ho tuzoraba uburyo Ababiligi bagenzuye igihugu, bagumya ubwami mu izina.
