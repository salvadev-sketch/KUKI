# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP07 — ATANGURA GUTEGEKA WENYENE

Ku wa 28 Myandagaro 1929, regency council yemeje ikintu abantu bari bamaze imyaka bategereje.

Mwambutsa IV yari amaze kugera ku myaka imwemerera gutwara igihugu ku giti ciwe.

---

Aha, dutandukanya ibihe bibiri by'ingoma yiwe.

**1915 — 1929:** umwami w'umwana, igihugu gifashijwe na regency.

**1929 — 1966:** Mwambutsa ari we mwami mukuru, ategeka ku giti ciwe.

---

Igihe cose azomara ku ngoma — imyaka irenga mirongo itanu — imyaka cumi na ine ya mbere azoyimara ari umwami w'izina gusa. Ni imyaka mirongo itatu na indwi gusa azomara ategeka nk'umwami nyawe.

Uyu mwaka, **1929**, rero, ni umwaka w'ingenzi cane kuri iyi documentaire — n'ubwo utazwi cane nk'uwo w'ubwigenge canke uw'urupfu rwa Rwagasore.

---

Ariko igihugu Mwambutsa yari agiye kuronka ku giti ciwe ntabwo cari igihugu cimwe n'ico se yari yarazwe.

Ubutegetsi bw'Ababiligi bwari bumaze imyaka bufise imizi mu Burundi.

Kandi mu myaka izokurikira, Mwambutsa azoshira umutima ku kindi kintu gikomeye, kitari ubutegetsi bw'igihugu gusa: umuryango wiwe bwite.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP08 — UMUGORE WIWE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
