# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP16 — INYUMA Y'UBWIGENGE — UPRONA IRACAMWO IBICE

Igihugu kimaze kwikukira, harabaho amahiganwa hagati y'imigwi ya politike.

Muri ayo, harimwo ibibazo vyerekeye: UPRONA ubwayo, ubutegetsi, Abahutu n'Abatutsi, imigwi ya Bezi na Batare, n'uruhara umwami akwiye kugira mu guhitamwo reta.

---

Ubushakashatsi bw'akaminuza bwerekana ko, hagati ya 1962 na 1967, UPRONA yaracitsemo ibice, maze politike y'igihugu iragenda ivanga n'ibibazo vy'imigwi n'amoko.

Ubutegetsi bwa Mwambutsa bwagerageje kuguma hagati y'iyo migwi. Ariko ibintu vyagiye birakomera.

---

**[CITATION — 🟢]**

René Lemarchand (1970) na Ellen Eggers (2006), biciye kuri Wikipedia ("Thaddée Siryuyumunsi," "André Muhirwa"), bemeza ko UPRONA yaracitsemo ibice bibiri: "groupe Casablanca" (Muhirwa, uburinganire n'Abatutsi) na "groupe Monrovia" (Mirerekano, uburinganire n'Abahutu). Mwambutsa yagerageje kubahuza mu nama nkuru ya UPRONA yo muri Nzeri 1962, ariko ibibazo vyagumye. Emmanuel Ngayimpenda (1994, biciye kuri francegenocidetutsi.org) na we yemeza iryo terana ry'imigwi ibiri.
