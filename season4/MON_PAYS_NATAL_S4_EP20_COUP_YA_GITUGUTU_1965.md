# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP20 — COUP YA GITUGUTU 1965

Ku wa 18–19 Gitugutu 1965, abasirikare n'abapolisi baragerageje gutembagaza ubwami.

Ingabo z'abigometse zarateye ibwami i Bujumbura.

---

Hari imirwano ikomeye.

**Léopold Biha yarakomerekejwe.**

Coup yaratsinzwe.

---

Ariko gutsindwa kwa coup ntikwazanye amahoro.

Ahubwo hakurikiyeho uguhamwa n'ubwicanyi bwinshi, cane cane ku banyepolitike n'abandi Bahutu.

Umwe muri abo yari **Joseph Bamina** ubwiwe — uwahoze ari umushikiranganji, uwavuwe ku mwanya gusa iminsi mikeya imbere yuko coup ibaho — yarishwe ku wa 15 Kigarama 1965, mu ngeso z'ubwicanyi zakurikiye iryo gerageza ry'igometero.

Iyi ni imwe mu mice ibabaje kandi ikomeye mu mateka y'Uburundi — kandi ni ngombwa kuyivuga dukoresheje amasoko yizewe, atari kuvuganya vy'ubusa.
