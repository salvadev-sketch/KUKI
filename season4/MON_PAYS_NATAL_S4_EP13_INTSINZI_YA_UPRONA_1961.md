# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP13 — INTSINZI YA UPRONA 1961

Ku wa 18 Nyakanga 1961, habaye amatora y'Inteko.

Ubu bwari ubwigenge butarashika — ariko ni intambwe y'ingenzi cane mu nzira ijana ku bwigenge.

---

Ivyavuye mu matora vyaratangaje:

**UPRONA — imyanya 58 kuri 64.**

**Front Commun — imyanya 6 gusa.**

Iyi ntsinzi yari ikomeye kuko yerekanye ko UPRONA yari ifise ubushobozi bwo kuyobora igihugu igihe ubwigenge bwari bugiye kuza.

---

Ku wa 28 Nyakanga 1961, Louis Rwagasore aca aba umushikiranganji wa mbere w'Uburundi.

Umuhungu wa Mwambutsa IV, umuganwa, umuyobozi wa UPRONA — ubu, na none, umushikiranganji wa mbere.

Kuri Mwambutsa, iyi yari intsinzi ikomeye ku muryango wiwe.

---

Ariko iyo ntsinzi ntiyari kumara igihe kirekire.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP14 — URUPFU RWA RWAGASORE**, ni ho tuzoraba icahavuye.
