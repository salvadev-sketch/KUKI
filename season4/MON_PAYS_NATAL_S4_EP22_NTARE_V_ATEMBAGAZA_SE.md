# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP22 — NTARE V ATEMBAGAZA SE — IHEREZO RY'UBWAMI

Ku wa 8 Mukakaro 1966, Charles Ndizeye yakuye se, Mwambutsa IV, ku ngoma.

Aba **Ntare V** — umwami mukuru, atari uwuserukira se gusa.

---

Mwambutsa ntiyasubiye ku ngoma. Umuhungu yari amaze kwikuza ku bushobozi se yari yaramusigiye, kugira ngo abe umwami ubwiwe.

Ariko Ntare V yamaze igihe gito cane.

---

Mu **Gushyingo 1966**, **Michel Micombero** yaciye akuraho ubwami muri Uburundi bwose.

Uburundi buba **Repubulika**.

---

Aha, amateka y'ingoma y'abami b'Uburundi — urugendo twagize kuva ku Ntwero na Ntare Rushatsi, imyaka amajana menshi imbere, gushika kuri uyu musi — yahagarariye burundu.

Umwana yakuye se ku ngoma. Uwundi mugabo yakuye ubwami ku bwami bwose.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — EP23 — UBUZIMA BWO MU BUHUNGIRO N'URUPFU**, ni ho tuzoraba iherezo ry'ubuzima bwa Mwambutsa ubwiwe.
