# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP08 — 1984: AMATORA — CANDIDAT UMWE, 99,6%

Mu 1984, Bagaza yarongeye kwiyamamaza nka Perezida wa Repubulika.

---

Ariko yari **candidat umwe gusa**.

Yararonse hafi **99,6%** by'amajwi.

---

Aha hari contradiction ikomeye dukwiye kwerekana: amatora yarabaye, ariko nta mpari nyakuri y'abakandida bahari.

Umuntu adafise uwo bahiganwa ntashobora gutsindwa.

---

Iyi event ituma tubaza ikibazo ca ngenzi kuri iyi season yose: mbese "démocratisation contrôlée" Bagaza yavuze mu ntango y'ingoma yiwe, yari koko inzira ija ku bwisanzure bw'amatora, canke yari uburyo bwo gukomeza ubutegetsi bumwe, buserukirwa mu buryo bw'amatora?

---

Inyuma y'iyi ntsinzi, Bagaza yaronse icubahiro cy'ubutegetsi bushasha — ariko mu myaka ikurikira, ubwo butegetsi buzohura n'ikindi kibazo gikomeye, kitari ic'amatora.
