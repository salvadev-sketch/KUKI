# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP12 — MWOROHA MURI 1972

Twize kuri 1972 vyimbitse, muri Season 6.

Hari umurwi tutari twaravuze cane: **Jeunesses Révolutionnaires Rwagasore — JRR**, urwaruka rwafitaniye isano na UPRONA.

---

Émile Mworoha yari **Secrétaire Général wa JRR**, mu gihe c'ubwicanyi bwo mu 1972.

Muri Myandagaro 1972, yaciye avamwo uwo mwanya, aja i Buraya gukora études doctorales.

---

Ariko hari ikindi kintu gikomeye cane: mu gihe c'ubwicanyi, **Mworoha yarandikiye Perezida Michel Micombero**.

Intumbero y'iryo kete yari ukumenyesha Micombero ko, ku bw'iwe, uburyozwe bw'ivyari biriko biraba mu gihugu butari ubwa JRR — ahubwo bwari bufitaniye isano n'**appareil administratif et militaire**.

---

Iyi ni source ikomeye, ivuye kuri Commission Vérité et Réconciliation.

Ariko dutegerezwa kwandika neza: **"Mworoha yarandikiye Micombero..."** — ntitwovuga **"Mworoha yaciye ahagarika ubwicanyi."** Nta gihamya dufise yerekana ko iryo kete ryahinduye politike ya Micombero.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga: Mworoha yari mu nzego za JRR mu gihe c'ubwicanyi bwo mu 1972, kandi yaragerageje kwerekana ko uburyozwe bwari ku bandi, atari ku rwaruka.

Ntitwovuga ko yari mu bakoze ubwicanyi. Ntitwovuga na ngo ata co yari azi.

---

Inyuma y'ivyo, aja i Buraya, arangiza amashure, hanyuma asubira mu Burundi, aja muri gouvernome ya Bagaza.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP13 — MWOROHA MURI GUVERINOMA YA BAGAZA**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
