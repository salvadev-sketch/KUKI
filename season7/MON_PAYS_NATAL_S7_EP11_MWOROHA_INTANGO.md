# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP11 — ÉMILE MWOROHA: INTANGO

Hari izina rigaragaye kenshi muri iyi documentaire, kuva mu ma-Season y'intango: **Mworoha**.

Ni we twaramaze kwifashisha, inshuro nyinshi, nk'umuhinga w'amateka atwereka ivyabaye.

Ariko uwo muhinga, na we, afise inkuru yiwe bwite — kandi ntiyagumye ku mwanya w'umuhinga gusa.

---

**Émile Mworoha** yavutse mu **1940**, ku mutumba wa **Mutana**, muri commune Muruta, mu karere ka Kayanza. Se yari Nyenibamfu Raphaël, nyina Mpuhuye Bernadette.

---

Yize muri **École Normale Supérieure** i Bujumbura, aronka diplôme mu 1968, mu géographie, histoire, na français.

Uwo mwaka nyene, yaronse bourse, aja mu Bufaransa kubandanya amashure y'amateka.

Mu **1975**, yararangije thèse yiwe muri **Université de la Sorbonne**, i Paris.

---

Inzira yiwe rero ntiyatanguriye muri politike.

Yatanguriye mu burezi n'ubushakashatsi: umwigisha, umushakashatsi, hanyuma professeur muri ENS, kuva 1975.

---

Ariko mu **1976**, ibintu vyahindutse. Jean-Baptiste Bagaza yari amaze gufata ubutegetsi.

Kandi Mworoha yari amaze kugira uruhara mu bindi bintu, kera imbere y'aho — uruhara rukomeye tugomba kuraba imbere yo kubandanya inkuru yiwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP12 — MWOROHA MURI 1972**, ni ho tuzobibona.
