# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP06 — IVYAHINDUWE 1977–78

Mu 1977, Bagaza yakuyeho **impôt de capitation** — amatagisi yari asanzwe yishurwa n'abanyagihugu bose.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 André Guichaoua (*Burundi: A Country in Search of Its History*, Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne) yemeza ko ukuraho impôt de capitation kwabaye kuva mu mwaka w'ingengo y'imari wa 1977, hamwe no gukuraho ubugererwa. IWGIA (*The Indigenous World 2022*) yemeza itegeko ryabikuyeho: Decree-Law n° 1/19 ryo ku wa 30 Ruheshi 1977.

---

Yakuyeho kandi **ubugererwa** — uburenganzira bwa kera bw'abatware bwo gusaba abanyagihugu gukora imirimo ku buntu, canke gutanga igice c'umwimbu wabo.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Inyandiko ya UNHCR (archive) igira iti: « 1977 | President Bagaza initiates land reforms ending the system of Tutsi feudal landlords » — arivyo bisobanura mu Kirundi ngo: "1977 | Perezida Bagaza atangura impinduka ku vy'ubutaka, akuraho uburyo bw'abatware b'Abatutsi bo mu bwoko bw'ubugererwa."

Iyi ni imwe mu mpinduka zikomeye cane ku buzima bw'abahinzi.

---

Leta yarasubiye kwigarurira amatongo yari yarafashwe n'abanyapolitike b'ubutegetsi bwa kera, harimwo amatongo y'abari barahunze mu 1972–73. Ariko ntitwovuga ko buri muntu wese yari yarahunze yarasubijwe umutungo wiwe wose.

Administration y'amakomine na yo yararongowe.

---

Bagaza yatanguye politike y'iterambere. Mu kiganiro yagiranye na Iwacu, yavuze ko yabanje kwibaza "twotangura hehe?", hanyuma agashira imbere amabarabara, amashanyarazi, amazi, na barrages.

Yavuze ko yakoranye n'Ubudagi, Abashinwa, Abafaransa, na Banque mondiale.

---

Mu **1978**, hatanguye **Premier Plan Quinquennal** (1978–1982) — igishushanyo c'iterambere ry'imyaka itanu, atari ibikorwa bitandukanye gusa.

Ariko dutegerezwa kwitonda: reforms izo zose ntizakemuye ibibazo vyose. Sources zerekana ko modernisation ya Bagaza yari ifise imbibe, cane cane ku bijanye n'uburinganire bw'uturere n'uko abanyagihugu bato bungukira kuri ayo ma investissements.

---

Ico gihe cose, ubutegetsi bwavuze ku "démocratisation" — ariko UPRONA yagumye ari parti nyamukuru, igisirikare kigumana uruhara runini. Ntabwo cari démocratie yuzuye.
