# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP03 — BAGAZA AKURA MU GISIRIKARE (1972–1975)

Kuva 1972 gushika 1975, Bagaza yagumye azamuka mu gisirikare.

---

Ku wa **28 Ruheshi 1975**, Bulletin Officiel du Burundi, mu itegeko n° 520/126, yatangaje:

**Thomas Ndabemeye** aba Général-Major.

**Jean Bagaza** aba Colonel.

**Sylvère Nzohabonayo** aba Colonel.

---

Iyo ni yo nyandiko ya Leta twashoboye kuronka, atari isoko ya kabiri gusa. Rero iyi promotion ni ivyanditswe ku mugaragaro.

Ariko dutegerezwa gutandukanya ibintu bibiri: promotion ubwayo ni fact, ariko impamvu yayo nyakuri ntitwayemeza.

---

Muri ico gihe, Bagaza yari amaze kuba umwe mu bagabo bakomeye cane mu gisirikare c'Uburundi — hafi cane ya Michel Micombero.

Ariko ubucuti hagati yabo bwari bugiye guhinduka.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP04 — COUP YA 1 MUNYONYO 1976**, ni ho tuzobibona.
