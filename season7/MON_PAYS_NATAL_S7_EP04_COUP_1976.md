# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP04 — COUP YA 1 MUNYONYO 1976

Ku wa **1 Munyonyo 1976**, igisirikare carafashe ubutegetsi i Bujumbura, Micombero arakurwa — twarabibonye muri Season 6, EP18. Ubu turabona ico gikorwa duhereye kuri Bagaza, uwabaye Perezida.

Ibijanye n'ibivugwa kuri **Thomas Ndabemeye** twabirondoye muri Season 6, EP18. Ico dushobora kuvuga hano: Bagaza ni we yafashe intambwe ya nyuma.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Amasoko atandukanye (IWACU, Wikipedia y'Igifaransa n'Icongereza, Perspective Monde) yose ahuriza ku nkuru imwe: ko impfune z'ibirego ku Ndabemeye zategura coup ari zo zatumye Bagaza afata intambwe ya mbere, ariko ata na kimwe muri ayo masoko gitanga ibimenyetso bihagije byerekana ko Ndabemeye ubwiwe yari afise umugambi mutomoye w'ico gikorwa.

---

Ku wa 2 Munyonyo, Bagaza yarasomye itangazo asigura impamvu z'ico gikorwa: ububasha bwose bwari bwarakoranirijwe ku muntu umwe, inzego zari zarahagaze, "nouvelle monarchie" yari itanguye kuboneka, UPRONA yari yaratakaje uruhara rwayo, kandi Itegeko Nshinga ntiryari rikiburahwa.

Ijambo rye ryagumye: **« Reculer devant une telle situation aurait été un acte de défaillance... »**

Ariko ntidukwiye gufata izo mvo nk'ukuri kudashidikanywako — ni ivyo ubutegetsi bushasha bwatanze, atari preuve.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 IWACU isubiramwo iyi mvugo ivuye muri iryo tangazo — ivyerekana ko koko Bagaza yasobanuye ico gikorwa nk'ikitagira uburyo bundi.

---

Ku wa **9 Munyonyo 1976**, Conseil Suprême Révolutionnaire yashize Colonel Bagaza ku mwanya wa Perezida wa Repubulika.

Micombero, Ndabemeye, na Joseph Rwuri — bose barakuwe mu nzego.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Itariki (9 Munyonyo) ihuye n'uko IWACU ubwayo yise ingingo yayo ("le 9 novembre 1976, Bagaza est désigné Président"). IWACU ivuga kandi ko Bagaza yakuye mu mabanga abasirikare batatu bakuru: Micombero, Ndabemeye, na Joseph Rwuri. Ariko amasoko amwe (Wikipedia y'Icongereza n'Igifaransa) avuga ko yashinzwe ("investi") ku wa 10 Munyonyo — bishoboka ko ari itariki y'igikorwa co gutorwa na CSR (9), indi ikaba iy'ihambwe ry'ubutegetsi (10).
