# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP15 — MWOROHA — PEREZIDA WA ASSEMBLÉE NATIONALE (1982–87)

Ku wa 22 Gitugutu 1982, habaye amatora y'abashingamateka.

Assemblée Nationale nshasha yaragizwe n'abashingamateka 65.

**Émile Mworoha aba Perezida wa Assemblée Nationale.**

---

Muri abo bashingamateka 65, **10 gusa bari Abahutu**.

Iyi data igaragaza neza ikibazo c'urwego rw'uko amoko yari ahagarikiwe mu nzego z'ubutegetsi, mu gihe ca Bagaza.

---

Mworoha rero yaguma, icarimwe, ku ntebe zombi zikomeye: Secrétaire Général wa UPRONA, na Perezida wa Assemblée Nationale — kugeza ku wa 3 Nyakanga 1987, igihe coup ya Pierre Buyoya yahirikaga Bagaza.

---

Ni ukuvuga: urugendo rwa politike rwa Mworoha muri Deuxième République rwaje kurangira umunsi umwe n'urwa Bagaza.

Ariko imbere yo kuraba ico gikorwa ca nyuma, dutegerezwa gusubira kuri Bagaza ubwiwe — kuko hari ikibazo kimwe kikomeye cagumye kigenda mu myaka ya nyuma y'ingoma yiwe, tutarakibona neza.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP16 — COUP YA BUYOYA — 3 NYAKANGA 1987**, ni ho tuzobibona.
