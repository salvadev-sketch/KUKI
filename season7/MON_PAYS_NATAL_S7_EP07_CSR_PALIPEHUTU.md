# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP07 — 1979–1982: CSR IRASEZWA, PALIPEHUTU IRASHINGWA

Mu **1979**, Conseil Suprême Révolutionnaire yaraseswa.

Mu **1980**, Comité Central ca UPRONA gitangura gufata ibikorwa vyari bisanzwe bikorwa na CSR. Bagaza aba hejuru y'iyo nzego, kandi **Émile Mworoha** aba umuntu akomeye cane muri UPRONA — uwo tuzomumenya vyimbitse mu ma-episode akurikira.

Iyi ni intambwe ikomeye: ubutegetsi bwa gisirikare buragenda buhinduka one-party system ishingiye kuri UPRONA.

---

Ariko mu wundi mwaka umwe nyene, **1980**, hari ikindi kintu kikomeye: Abahutu bahunze igihugu batanguye gutunganya opposition ikomeye — **PALIPEHUTU** irashingwa.

Ntitwovange iki kintu na 1972. Ariko dukwiye kubona: mu ntango, Bagaza yavuze ku vyo yita reforms na reconciliation. Ariko opposition y'Abahutu yo hanze yariko irakura.

---

Mu **Munyonyo 1981**, Itegeko Nshinga rishasha ryaratorewe muri referendum. Ryateganya National Assembly, amatora, n'uburyo bushasha bw'inzego za Leta.

Ariko Uburundi bwagumye ari **one-party state** iyobowe na UPRONA.

---

Mu **Gitugutu 1982**, habaye amatora ya mbere y'abashingamateka mu gihe c'ico Tegeko Nshinga rishasha.

Ivyo bidufasha kwibaza: Bagaza yari ariko aragendera kuri démocratie, canke kuri parti imwe yifashisha imigenge ya démocratie?

---

Igisubizo, twobibona vyimbitse mu myaka ikurikira.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP08 — 1984: AMATORA — CANDIDAT UMWE, 99,6%**, ni ho tuzobibona.
