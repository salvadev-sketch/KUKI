# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP14 — MWOROHA — UPRONA SECRÉTAIRE GÉNÉRAL (1978–82)

Mu 1978, Émile Mworoha aba **Secrétaire Général wa UPRONA.**

Iyi ni yo turning point ikomeye cane mu buzima bwiwe bwa politike.

---

Kuko UPRONA yari parti unique mu Burundi muri ico gihe, uwo mwanya wari ukomeye cane.

Mu Kigarama 1979, habaye congrès ya mbere y'igihugu ya UPRONA. Central Committee nshasha irarongorwa. Kuva muri Nzero 1980, iyo Central Committee itangura gufata ibikorwa vyari vyasanzwe bikorwa na Conseil Suprême Révolutionnaire — twize kuri iyo mpinduka muri EP07.

---

Hari amasoko amwe avuga ko Mworoha yari "umuntu wa kabiri mu gihugu," inyuma ya Bagaza. Ariko iyo ni characterization ya secondary sources, si titre officiel.

Ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe: **Mworoha yabaye umwe mu bantu bakomeye cane ba politike ba Deuxième République.**

---

Mworoha yaguma kuri uwo mwanya gushika 1987.

Ariko mu **1982**, azoronka indi ntebe ikomeye cane.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP15 — MWOROHA — PEREZIDA WA ASSEMBLÉE NATIONALE (1982–87)**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
