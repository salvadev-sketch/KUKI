# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP14 — MWOROHA — UPRONA SECRÉTAIRE GÉNÉRAL (1978–82)

Mu 1978, Émile Mworoha aba **Secrétaire Général wa UPRONA.**

Iyi ni yo ntambwe nyamukuru ikomeye cane mu buzima bwiwe bwa politike.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Académie des sciences d'outre-mer (2018) ivuga ko kuva 1978 gushika 1987 ni secrétaire général wa UPRONA, umugambwe mukuru w'ubwenegihugu 'w'Abatutsi' wari ku butegetsi.

---

Kuko UPRONA yari umugambwe umwe rukumbi mu Burundi muri ico gihe, uwo mwanya wari ukomeye cane.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Guichaoua (Panthéon-Sorbonne) avuga ko UPRONA yavuguruwe kuva mu 1977. Ariko ntitwabonye itariki n'ubwoko bwa congrès ya mbere y'igihugu (ico dutangaza mu nyandiko ya mbere ni 1979), ni co gituma tutayivuga nk'ukuri kwemejwe. 🟡 Ivyo tuzi: Central Committee nshasha yarashizweho, kandi kuva muri Nzero 1980, iyo Central Committee itangura gufata ibikorwa vyari vyasanzwe bikorwa na Conseil Suprême Révolutionnaire — twize kuri iyo mpinduka muri EP07.

---

Hari amasoko amwe avuga ko Mworoha yari "umuntu wa kabiri mu gihugu," inyuma ya Bagaza. Ariko iyo ni insobanuro ivuye mu masoko adari intango nyamukuru, atari izina ryemewe ku mugaragaro.

Ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe: **Mworoha yabaye umwe mu bantu bakomeye cane ba politike ba Deuxième République.**

---

Mworoha yaguma kuri uwo mwanya gushika 1987.

Ariko mu **1982**, azoronka indi ntebe ikomeye cane.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP15 — MWOROHA — PEREZIDA WA ASSEMBLÉE NATIONALE (1982–87)**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
