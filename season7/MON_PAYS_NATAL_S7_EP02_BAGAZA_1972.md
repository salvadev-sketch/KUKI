# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP02 — BAGAZA MURI 1972

Bagaza yari mu Burundi mu 1972.

Kandi mu nyuma y'itangura rya repression, yararonse umwanya ukomeye cane muri General Staff.

---

Ariko ivyo vyonyene ntibihagije kuvuga ko yagize uruhara mu bwicanyi.

Hari amasoko avuga ko yagize uruhara muri repression yo mu 1972, ariko akongerako ko urugero n'imiterere nyayo y'uruhara rwiwe bitatomoye neza.

Ariko hari isoko rimwe rikomeye, iry'umwanditsi wa Le Monde yo mu 2016, rivuga ikintu gitandukanye: ko Bagaza **"ne fait pas partie des 'bouchers' identifiés"** — ni ukuvuga ko atari mu bantu bari baramenyekanye nk'abategetsi b'ingenzi b'ubwicanyi.

---

Hano hari contradiction nyayo hagati y'amasoko.

Ntitushobora kuvuga ngo "Bagaza yari umwicanyi wo mu 1972." Ntitushobora kandi kuvuga ngo "nta ruhara na rumwe yagize." Vyose birarengeje evidence dufise.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga: mu 1972, Jean-Baptiste Bagaza yari umusirikare akiri muto, ariko yari amaze gushika mu nzego nkuru za General Staff. Uruhara rwiwe bwite muri ubwo bwicanyi ntirurasobanuka neza.

---

Bagaza ubwiwe, mu kiganiro yagiranye na Iwacu mu myaka ya vuba, yavuze ko mu 1972 abasirikare benshi b'Abahutu bishwe, maze igisirikare gisigara ahanini kigizwe n'Abatutsi — akongerako ko ubwoba mu miryango y'Abahutu bwatumye benshi batipfuza ko abana babo binjira mu gisirikare.

Ubwo buhamya bwiwe si intahengano y'ukuri kwose. Ariko ni ngirakamaro.

---

Ku wa 28 Munyonyo 1972, azamurwa mu mapete, aba Lieutenant-Colonel, hanyuma Chef d'État-Major Général Adjoint.

Ntitwovuga ko iyo promotion yavuye ku ruhara mu bwicanyi. Ariko ntitwovuga na ngo iyo promotion ata co ifitaniye isano na yo.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP03 — BAGAZA AKURA MU GISIRIKARE (1972–1975)**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
