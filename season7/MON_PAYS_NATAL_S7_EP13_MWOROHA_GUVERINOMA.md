# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP13 — MWOROHA MURI GUVERINOMA YA BAGAZA

Mu 1976, Jean-Baptiste Bagaza amaze gufata ubutegetsi, Émile Mworoha yagizwe **Ministre de la Jeunesse, des Sports et de la Culture.**

Uwo mwanya yawugumyeho gushika mu 1978.

---

Iyi ni intambwe ya mbere ya Mworoha muri gouvernome — ariko atari iya nyuma.

Kuva ku burezi, aja mu bushakashatsi, aja muri politike y'urwaruka n'umuco — Mworoha yari agenda yubaka inzira igana ku bubasha bukomeye kurushiriza.

---

Muri ico gihe, twibuke: Bagaza yari Perezida, Mworoha yari Ministre.

Ariko urwo rugendo rwa Mworoha rwari rukigenda rutera imbere.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 7 — EP14 — MWOROHA — UPRONA SECRÉTAIRE GÉNÉRAL (1978–82)**, ni ho tuzobibona.
