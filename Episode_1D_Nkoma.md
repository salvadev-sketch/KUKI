# MON PAYS NATAL — ABANTU 100 BAGIZE AMATEKA Y'UBURUNDI
## EPISODE 1D — NKOMA: IVUKA RYA KARYENDA

**Igitigiri:** Episode 1D (ikurikira Ku Buha, imbere y'Ubwami bwa Ntare bukomeye)
**Umutwe nyamukuru:** Ku musozi wa Nkoma, umugina n'inzoka bivyara ingoma y'ubwami — hanyuma Ntare arica Jabwe
**Ururimi:** Kirundi
**Ibipimo:** 🟢 Ikimenyamenya gikomeye · 🟡 Imigenzo mvugo/disputed · 🔴 Ntibihamye

---

### IGICE 1 — INTANGAMARARA (Bridge kuva kuri Episode 1C)

**[VISUAL: Umuhungu ariko atembera ku nzira, umusozi wa Nkoma uragaragara kure, ijoro ririko riraza]**

**NARATION:**

Muri Episode 1C, twabonye Cambarantama yikijije urupfu ku Buha, arengana uruzi rwa Malagarazi, arasubira mu gihugu ciwe. Uyu musi, urugendo rwiwe ruragera ku musozi umwe uzosubira guhindura amateka y'Uburundi: **Nkoma**.

---

### IGICE 2 — ITUNGA N'UMUGINA (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Umugina (termitière) uragaragara ku mugina, ku ruhande rw'inzu, urukoba rw'ishereka rushirwa hejuru]**

**NARATION:**

Ku Nkoma, hafi y'ahitwa Banege, Cambarantama afise itungo (inka n'ishereka) yaronse ku Buha. Ivyo bimenyetso bimuvuye ku bapfumu vyaramushigikiye. Urukoba rw'ishereka rurambitswe ku mugina.

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 34**, hivugwa hati:

> « Une peau de taureau étendue sur une termitière... inaugure le tambour dynastique Karyenda. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Urukoba rw'ishereka rurambitswe ku mugina... ni ho havuka ingoma y'ubwami Karyenda."*

---

### IGICE 3 — INZOKA IRAKUBITA (🟢🟡)

**[VISUAL: Ijoro, umugina uragaragara mu mwijima, urumuri rw'ukwezi rwonyene, inzoka igaragara nk'igicucu]**

**NARATION:**

Nk'uko Ruvugazinaniwe abivuga mu nkuru yiwe, ku Nkoma hafi ya Banege, inzoka yari ituye muri uwo mugina irakubita ku rukoba rw'ishereka. Iryo jwi n'urwo rukorwa vyafatwe nk'ikimenyetso c'uko Cambarantama yemerwa n'ivyaduka nk'umwami.

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 41**, hivugwa hati:

> « Arrivés au Nkoma près de Banege, un serpent... »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Bageze ku Nkoma hafi ya Banege, inzoka..."* (igikorwa kirakomeza kivuga ko iyo nzoka ari yo yamwemeje/yamwomekeye nk'umwami).

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Chrétien avuga ko iki gikorwa (umugina, urukoba, inzoka) ari co gitangura ku mugaragaro **Karyenda**, ingoma y'ubwami bw'Uburundi — ni ikimenyetso gikomeye c'imigenzo, gikoreshwa n'abenshi mu batanze inkuru.

🟡 **Ico dutandukanya:** Iyi ni imigenzo mvugo yerekana insanamu (symbolisme) y'ubwami, atari raporo y'ivyabaye ku buryo bw'amateka.

---

### IGICE 4 — IKIRIMBA KU KIBIRA (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Ikarata y'Uburundi yerekana umusozi wa Kibira, hamwe n'ikimenyetso c'ikirimba]**

**NARATION:**

Amaze kwemerwa nk'umwami, Cambarantama — uzoba Ntare Rushatsi — ashiraho ikirimba ciwe **ku murambi wa Kibira**.

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 34**, hivugwa hati:

> « Il installe sa capitale sur la crête de la Kibira. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Ashiraho ikirimba ciwe ku murambi wa Kibira."*

Nyuma yaho, Ntare yagiye yongera gushiraho ibindi birimba ku bibaya vyo hejuru vy'i Mugamba, hafi ya Bukeye na Muramvya, gushika kuri Banga no ku nkombe z'uruzi rwa Kanyaru.

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Akazi ka Ntare karimwo **guhuza ibibanza vyinshi vy'ubutware bwo ku bibaya vyo hejuru** — atari mu gihugu cose kimwe co hambere, ahubwo kwaguka gahoro gahoro.

---

### IGICE 5 — NTARE ARICA JABWE (🟢🟡)

**[VISUAL: Imvura iratontoma, umuntu ahunga, aja mu nzu y'undi muntu]**

**NARATION:**

Ntare ntiyabaye umwami mu mahoro. Imigenzo ivuga ko yatanguje kwica Jabwe, se wiwe (uwo yavutseko binyuze mu buhemu). Inkuru imwe ivuga ivy'igihe Jabwe yagiye guhiga, imvura iratontoma, agahungira kwa mushiki wa nyina (belle-sœur).

**Nk'uko tubisoma mu gitabo «Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée» canditswe na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, ku rupapuro rwa 34**, hivugwa hati:

> « Ntare élimine Jabwe. »

— mu kinonkano mu Kirundi, bikaba tugenekereje: *"Ntare arica Jabwe."*

🟡 **Ico dutandukanya:** Uburyo nyakuri Jabwe yishwe (aho, ryari, gute) buratandukana mu masoko, kandi Chrétien ubwiwe avuga ko iki gikorwa (kuraha kwa Jabwe n'imvura imujana kwa mushiki wa nyina) cavuzwe **rimwe gusa**, mu buryo bugufi, na Ruvugazinaniwe.

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Amasoko yose ahuriza ku kintu kimwe: Ntare yaje gutsinda/gucisha bugufi ubutware bwa Jabwe, aca aba we umwami wenyene.

---

### IGICE 6 — UWUFISE INZIRA IYINJIRA (🟡)

**[VISUAL: Ibigabiro/amashamba meranda, amazina "Mbibe", "Nsoro", "Jabwe" agaragara ku bibanza bitandukanye]**

**NARATION:**

Muri iki gihe, amashamba meranda menshi (ibigabiro) agifitaniye isano n'amazina ya Mbibe (uwitwa "umuvukanyi" wa Ntare mu bindi bisigi), Nsoro na Jabwe — biboneka cane mu bumanuko bw'igihugu, kuva ku ntango y'uruzi Ruvyironza gushika i Mugamba.

🟡 **Ico dutandukanya:** Ibi bibanza vy'imigenzo ni ibimenyetso vy'imigenzo, si ivyacukuwe mu vy'ubucukuzi.

🔴 **Ico tutemeza:** Ubushakashatsi bw'ubucukuzi (archéologie) muri utwo turere ntiburakorwa ku buryo bwuzuye — Chrétien ubwiwe avuga ko utwo turere twokwiga bikwiye ubushakashatsi bw'ubucukuzi mu gihe kizoza.

---

### IGICE 7 — IMPAMVU IKI GIKORWA GIKOMEYE (Bridge kuri Episode 1E)

**[VISUAL: Ingoma Karyenda iragaragara ku mugaragaro, hejuru y'ikirimba]**

**NARATION:**

Aha ni ho ubwami bw'Uburundi butanguriye mu vy'ukuri, mu migenzo: atari ku ivuka rya Cambarantama, atari ku ntambara ye ku Buha, ariko **ku musozi wa Nkoma**, aho umugina n'inzoka vyavyaye ingoma yashinze igihugu.

Ariko ubwami budashobora kuguma ku bimenyetso vy'ivuka gusa. Muri Episode 1E, tuzoraba **Umuganuro**, umuhango ukomeye wahujije ubwami n'ubuzima bw'igihugu cose — kandi tuzobona uko Ntare, amaze gutsinda Jabwe, yagiye ashiraho imigenzo izokomeza kugeza mu bihe vya vuba.

## ICYITONDERWA KU BUSHAKASHATSI (Editorial note — ntibigomba kuvugwa mu video)

✅ **Amasoko yose y'iki gice avuye mu gitabo ca Chrétien ubwiwe** (*Burundi : l'histoire retrouvée*, 1993), impapuro 34, 39, 41, na 44 — ivyakusanyijwe biva ku nkuru ya Ruvugazinaniwe (1954/1979) hamwe n'isesengura rya Chrétien.

🔖 **Amahame yakoreshejwe:** Ibendera ry'amakuru menshi (Chrétien p. 34, 39, 41, 44) arahurira ku kintu kimwe: umugina/inzoka/Karyenda ni igikorwa cy'ibanze cy'imigenzo y'ubwami, gikomeye kurusha uburyo Jabwe yaciwe ku bubasha.
