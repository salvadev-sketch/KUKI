# MON PAYS NATAL — ABANTU 100 BAGIZE AMATEKA Y'UBURUNDI
## EPISODE 1C — KU BUHA

**Igitigiri:** Episode 1C (ikurikira Jabwe na Nsoro, imbere y'ivuka rya Karyenda)
**Umutwe nyamukuru:** Cambarantama ahungira ku Buha — kandi ni ng'aho ahakura intambara imuhindura umwami
**Ururimi:** Kirundi
**Ibipimo:** 🟢 Ikimenyamenya gikomeye · 🟡 Imigenzo mvugo/disputed · 🔴 Ntibihamye

---

### IGICE 1 — INTANGAMARARA (Bridge kuva kuri Episode 1B)

**[VISUAL: Umuhungu w'umwana ahagaze ku mupaka, hanyuma agenda arenga akarere ka Buha]**

**NARATION:**

Muri Episode 1B, twabonye Cambarantama — uwo mwana w'igishika havutse ubuhemu — arahunga akarere kavutsemwo. Uyu musi, turamukurikira aja ku Buha, aho azohura n'ikibazo kizomuhindura umwami.

---

### IGICE 2 — TANTE WIWE NAMABUYE (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Diagram y'umuryango — Ntwero na Namirembe bavyaye Jabwe, Nsoro, na Namabuye; ikarata y'akarere ka Buha]**

**NARATION:**

Cambarantama ntiyajanywe kwa nyirakuru wiwe — yajanywe kwa **tante wiwe**: **Namabuye**, mushiki wa se Jabwe (na Nsoro), umwana wa Ntwero na Namirembe. Namabuye yari yararongowe na **Ruhaga**, umwami w'i Buha yo mu Bumanuko.

Iyi si imico gusa — Chrétien avuga ko urwo rugendo rwakozwe **ku bufasha bwa Jabwe ubwiwe, hamwe n'abapfumu** bamufashije kuronka ubuhungiro.

🟢 **Ico twemeza (citation ihamye):** Mu gitabo *Burundi: L'histoire retrouvée* ca Jean-Pierre Chrétien, hivugwa hati: *"...chez sa tante paternelle Namabuye, épouse d'un roi du Buha du Sud."* — bisobanura: "...kwa tante wiwe, Namabuye, umugore w'umwami umwe wo mu Buha yo mu Bumanuko." Chrétien avuga kandi ku bundi rupapuro ko uwo mwami ari **Ruhaga**, uwarongoye Namabuye, umwana wa Namirembe.

---

### IGICE 3 — KIBUGUZO N'INTAMBARA Y'IMPFIZI (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Storyboard y'umukino wa trictrac (amabuye ku kabaho), hamwe n'ishusho z'impfizi zibiri zambaye amabara atandukanye]**

**NARATION:**

Ku Buha, Cambarantama arahiganwa n'umwami mu buryo bubiri: **kibuguzo**, umukino nka trictrac (backgammon), n'**intambara y'impfizi** — impfizi za buri ruhande zambaye amabara y'abazikoresha.

Muri byompi, Cambarantama aratsinda.

🟢 **Ico twemeza (citation ihamye):** Chrétien asobanura neza ivyo bihiganwa nk'ibintu bibiri bitandukanye: *"les défis du jeune Cambarantama à ce roi, au jeu de trictrac (kibuguzo) et lors d'un combat de taureaux."*

---

### IGICE 4 — URUNOGO RW'INTARE (🟢)

**[VISUAL: Urunogo rwiherereye rwuzuye intare, umuhungu ahunga, uruzi runini inyuma yiwe]**

**NARATION:**

Gutsindwa mu bihiganwa vyose bituma umwami w'i Buha agirira Cambarantama ishari. Aragerageza kumwica — **amuteranya mu runogo rw'intare**.

Ariko Cambarantama arikiza, akwiza urupfu. Arahunga, aca uruzi rwa **Malagarazi**, agasubira mu gihugu ciwe.

🟢 **Ico twemeza (citation ihamye):** Chrétien avuga ko: *"le jeune homme échappe à une tentative de meurtre du roi du Buha (la fosse aux lions) et fuit vers son pays d'origine en retraversant le fleuve (la Malagarazi)."*

🟡 **Ico dutandukanya:** Uku ni ukwo imigenzo ibivuga (Ruvugazinaniwe, 1954 na 1979) — ni imigenzo mvugo, atari raporo y'ivyabaye ku itariki n'ahantu bihamye.

---

### IGICE 5 — IMPAMVU IYI NTAMBARA IRAKOMEYE

**[VISUAL: Timeline ishirwa hagati ya Buha na Nkoma]**

**NARATION:**

Iki gikorwa — guhunga urupfu, gutsinda uwahiganwa na we — ni co gitangura kumugira umwami adasanzwe muri mvugo z'inkuru. Ntiyaronse ubwami kubera ko yavutse ku ntebe; yaronse ubwami kubera ko yikijije urupfu.

🟢 **Ico twemeza:** Uku ni ishusho isanzwe mu migenzo y'ubwami bw'akarere: umwami mushasha akenshi arangwa n'urugendo rw'intambara n'ikaga imbere yo gutorerwa ku ngoma — atari kuronka ubwami mu buryo bworoshe.

---

### IGICE 6 — ASUBIRA MU GIHUGU CIWE (Bridge kuri Episode 1D)

**[VISUAL: Umuhungu ariko atembera, umusozi wa Nkoma uragaragara kure]**

**NARATION:**

Cambarantama yikijije urupfu ku Buha, arasubira mu gihugu ciwe. Ariko urugendo rwiwe ntiruraheze — hariho umusozi umwe akiri kumurindira: **Nkoma**.

Ni ng'aho, muri Episode 1D, azohabwa Karyenda — kandi azoba Ntare Rushatsi ubwiwe.

## ICYITONDERWA KU BUSHAKASHATSI (Editorial note — ntibigomba kuvugwa mu video)

✅ **Vyakosowe kuri iyi verusiyo (isubiramwo):** Ibice vyose vy'iki gice vyahujwe na citation ihamye ivuye mu gitabo ca Chrétien ubwaco (*Burundi: L'histoire retrouvée*, 1993, hafi ku rupapuro rwa 38 mu PDF dufise) na BBG.docx:
1. **Namabuye ni tante wa Cambarantama** (mushiki wa Jabwe na Nsoro, umwana wa Ntwero na Namirembe) — **atari nyirakuru**, nk'uko naravuze mu isubiramwo ryabanje. Iri kosa ryahinduwe.
2. **Ruhaga ni umugabo wa Namabuye**, umwami w'i Buha — ikibazo twari dusigaje mu isubiramwo ryabanje kirasubijwe.
3. **Kibuguzo** ni umukino wa trictrac (backgammon), watandukanywe n'**intambara y'impfizi** — bibiri bitandukanye, atari kimwe.
4. **"Urunogo rw'intambwe"** ryahinduwe kuba **"urunogo rw'intare"** (fosse aux lions), kandi haseruwe n'uruzi rwa Malagarazi mu ruhunga.
