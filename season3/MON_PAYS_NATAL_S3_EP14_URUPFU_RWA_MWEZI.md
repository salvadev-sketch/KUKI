# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP14 — URUPFU RWA MWEZI

Umwami wamaze imyaka mirongo itanu ategeka, arwanya Abadagi, agatakaza ubushobozi, akabusubirira — na we, amaherezo, arapfa.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga dushize umutima hamwe ni iki: **Mwezi IV Gisabo yapfuye ku wa 21 Myandagaro 1908, i Buhonga.**

Inyandiko y'Ikigo c'Igihugu c'Ibarurishamibare ca Kayanza, hamwe n'iy'Université du Burundi, zombi zihurira kuri iyo tariki n'ahantu.

---

Ariko hari ikibazo kimwe kigumye gifunguye: **yoba yarishwe?**

Hano hari inkuru zitandukanye, kandi ntidukwiye kuvanga imigenzo n'amateka yemejwe.

Hari inkuru ivuga ko yari yagiye i Usumbura guhura n'umutegetsi w'Umudagi, hanyuma apfa ari mu nzira agaruka i Muramvya. Umuhinga Émile Mworoha na we avuga ko yapfiriye i Buhonga, kwa Mutware Mareza — ivyo bikaba bihura n'ivyanditswe na Kayanza.

Hari n'inkuru y'imigenzo ivuga ko abami b'Uburundi batari bakwiye kuraba ikiyaga Tanganyika — kandi iyo nkuru ihuza urupfu rwa Mwezi n'uko yagiye i Usumbura akegera iryo kiyaga. Université du Burundi ubwayo ivuga ko iyo ari imyemerere yo mu bantu, atari gihamya y'uko ari yo yatumye apfa.

Hari n'abashakashatsi bavuga ko bishoboka ko yapfuye azize **malaria**.

---

Ntitwovuga ngo tuzi neza ni ihe muri izo nkuru y'ukuri.

Ico dushobora kwemeza: **yapfuye ku wa 21 Myandagaro 1908 i Buhonga.**

Ico tutokwemeza nk'ukuri kudasubirwako: ko yishwe, ko yarozewe, ko yapfuye kubera kuraba ikiyaga, canke ko malaria ari yo yamwishe.

---

Umwami yari amaze imyaka mirongo itanu arwanya abamurwanya — abavukanyi biwe, abagabo bo mu buraruko, hanyuma abanyamahanga bavuye kure y'ikiyaga Tanganyika — na we, nk'uko bimwe vyose bigenda vyicara, arapfa mu buryo bugumye bufise agatima k'ibanga.

---

Ariko urupfu si iherezo ry'inkuru.

Kuko umubiri wiwe wagiye ahandi — kandi ahantu iyo yajanywe, na ho, hafise inkuru yaho.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP15 — AHO YAHAMBWE**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
