# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP07 — KIRIMA

Hari umuntu, mu buraruko bushira uburengero bw'Uburundi, yavuga ko afise uburenganzira ku ngoma.

Ntiyari umwe mu bahungu ba Ntare Rugamba bazwi.

Ariko yaravuga ko ari uwiwe.

---

Yitwa **Kirima**.

Mu migenzo yakusanyijwe, Kirima yavuga ko akomoka kuri Ntare Rugamba, biciye ku mugore yavuye mu Bushi, yitwa **Nyamvura**.

Iyi ni **imigenzo**, atari umuryango dushobora kwemeza nk'ukuri kutagira amakenga. Isoko ubwaryo rikoresha imvugo iserevya amakenga: Kirima "yivugako" ari umuragwa — atari ko yari azwi n'abandi ko ari we.

---

Ariko yaba koko afise ico gitsina canke atacifise, ikintu kimwe kigaragara: Kirima yaronse **abayoboke benshi**, mu turere two mu buraruko bushira uburengero.

Ntabwo yari umuntu ata co amaze.

---

Kandi ni ho havuka ikibazo gikomeye kuri Mwezi Gisabo:

Umuntu adafise ubuhamya buhagije bwo kuba umukomoka ku ngoma, ariko afise abayoboke benshi, ubutaka, n'ubushobozi — ni akaga gakomeye kuruta uwuvuga rumwe na wewe biciye ku muryango.

Kuko Kirima adafise ico atakaza mu guhiganwa. Ntiyakiri mu muryango w'ubwami mu buryo bwemewe. Yari afise ico yiyubakira, atari ico arinda.

---

Ariko Kirima ntiyari kubaho wenyene, mu buryo bw'akaga gato ku ngoma.

Kuko mu bihe bimwe, hari abandi bagenda binjira mu Burundi — batavuga ururimi rw'igihugu, bavuye kure, bafise ibirwanisho bitigeze biboneka.

Kandi abo banyamahanga, bazoza kubona muri Kirima ikintu badashaka kubura: umuntu bashobora gukoresha kugira barwanye umwami bo mu buryo bworoshe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP08 — MACONCO**, ni ho tuzokwiga uwundi muntu, uyu akaba ari mu muryango w'ubwami — umukwe wa Mwezi ubwiwe.
