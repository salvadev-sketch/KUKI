# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP12 — AMASEZERANO YA KIGANDA

Ku wa **6 Ruheshi 1903**, Mwezi Gisabo yashize umukono ku bintu bizoranga Uburundi mu myaka izokurikira.

Ariko imbere yo kuvuga ico yasinye, hari ikintu tugomba gucisha ku murongo utukura, kugira ntihagire urujijo:

> **"Mwezi yatsinzwe, igihugu kirigarurirwa."**

Ntitubivuge gutyo.

Ni ukuri ko Abadagi bari bamutsinze mu rwego rwa gisirikare, muri iyo ntambara.

Ariko ayo masezerano yarasize **Mwezi akiri Umwami w'Uburundi**.

---

## Ico yemeye

Mwezi yemeye:

**Ubusegaba bw'Ubudagi** ku Burundi.

**Ubwigenge bwa Kirima i Bukeye.**

**Ubwigenge bwa Maconco i Muramvya.**

Gutanga **inka 424** nk'indishi — atari 420, nk'uko amasoko amwe yoshobora kubivuga make; urubuga rwa Leta y'Uburundi rutanga umubare uwo, ugaragara ko ari wo w'ukuri.

Kutongera kubuza ibikorwa vy'ubutumwa bwa Gatolika i Mugera.

---

## Ico ayo masezerano atari yo

Ayo masezerano ntabwo yari isozero ry'ingoma ya Mwezi.

Yari intambwe ikomeye, ariko si iherezo.

Kirima na Maconco, ku kanya gato, baratsindiye: ubwigenge mu turere twabo, bafashijwe n'Abadagi, byari bimaze kugabura ubushobozi bw'ubwami.

Ariko amasezerano yasize ikibazo kimwe gifunguye — ikibazo kizohindura vyose:

Ni gute Abadagi bazogenzura igihugu, bariko bafise abatware babiri bo mu gihugu, batavuga rumwe n'umwami, bagira ubwigenge muri utwo turere?

---

Iki kibazo, Abadagi ubwabo bazogenda babona ko atari cyoroshe nk'uko bari babyibwiye.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP13 — IGIHUGU KIRONGERA KWIYUNGA**, ni ho tuzoraba ukuntu, mu myaka ibiri gusa inyuma y'ayo masezerano, ibintu vyahindutse rwose.
