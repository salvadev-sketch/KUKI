# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP01 — INTANGO

Hari izina abahinga b'amateka batarashoboye gusobanura.

Ntibaravumbura aho ryakomotse. Ntibaravumbura igihe ryatanguye gukoreshwa. Ntibaravumbura n'insiguro yaryo nyakuri.

Ni izina ry'umwami.

**Bikata-Bijoga.**

Bamwe bavuga ko rifitaniye isano n'ibicu, abandi ntibavuga na kimwe. Kandi twebwe, aha, ntituzohimba icotuvako kubaza abandi ico bisobanura, tugategereza gushika ku bimenyetso.

---

Uwo mwami yitwa **Mwezi IV Gisabo**.

Muri urwo rutonde rw'abami dukurikiye kuva Ntare Rushatsi, ni we uwa cumi na indwi.

Yategetse hafi imyaka mirongo itanu — igihe kirekire kurusha uko twari twaramaze kubona kuri uwo n'uwo mwami, kuva twatangura urwo rugendo.

Ariko imbere yo kuraba icamugejeje kure, dutegerezwa gutangura aho igihe cose dutangura: ku muzi w'umuryango wiwe.

---

Se yari **Ntare IV Rutaganzwa Rugamba** — uwo twize mu Season 2, umwami yaguye ubwami akubya kabiri, akashiraho ibikanga, akongera akagabira abahungu biwe uturere.

Nyina, kuri we, ntiturabizi. Amasoko dufise ubu ntatanga izina.

Naho abamubanjirije bose, muri urwo rutonde, hari usanga nyina bavugwa (Msabiyije wa Ntare Rugamba, Inanzerwe ya Mwezi Nyaburunga...), Gisabo yagumye ari umwe muri bo dutazi nyina wiwe. Ntitwoshaka kwiyumvira izina, nk'uko twagenje kuri Rufuku muri Season 1.

---

Icobonye n'amatariki ubwayo, ntabwo aramutse.

Amasoko amwe avuga ko yatanguye ingoma mu **1850**, andi akavuga **1852**. Nta gihamya gihagije gitandukanya izo nyishu zombi. Rero kuri iyi documentaire: **yatanguye gutegeka hagati ya 1850 na 1852.**

---

Kandi hari ikindi tugomba kwitondera, imbere yo kubandanya, kugira ntihagire uwuvanga inkuru zitandukanye.

Izina **Mwezi** ryagiye risubiramwo kenshi mu rutonde rw'abami: Mwezi I Baridamunka, Mwezi II Nyaburunga, Mwezi III Ndagushimiye/Kavuyimbo. Uyu, ni **Mwezi IV**.

Ni co gituma azwi cane hamwe n'amazina yiwe akurikira, kugira ntihagire uwumuvanga n'abandi: **Mwezi IV Gisabo Bikata-Bijoga.**

Hari n'amasoko amwe amwita **Mwezi IV Mbikije** — ariko izina "Mbikije" ryonyene ryabaye iry'umwana wiwe azoza gusubira ku ngoma, **Mutaga IV Mbikije**. Ntitwovuga ngo ayo mazina ni umwe, kandi ntitwovanga ba bantu babiri.

---

Ariko ikintu kimwe gikomeye kigomba kuvugwa imbere yo kubandanya:

Mwezi Gisabo yaje ku ngoma **akiri muto cane**.

Twaramaze kubona, mu isozero rya Season 2, ukuntu se, Ntare Rugamba, yashize abahungu biwe mu turere — Ndivyariye, Rwasha, Birori, Busumano, Twarereye, na Gisabo ubwiwe — kugira babe abategetsi b'uturere twose yari yaronse.

Igihe se yapfa, Gisabo ntiyari afise ubushobozi bwo gutegeka ku giti ciwe.

Yari akeneye umuntu wo kumurera, kumufasha, kumurinda — mu gihe c'ubwana bwiwe.

---

Uwo muntu, imigenzo n'ubushakashatsi bimwe bivuga, ni umwe mu bavukanyi biwe ubwiwe.

**Ndivyariye.**

Ariko iyo nkuru — y'umurezi yaje kuba umwanzi, y'intambara yabereye i Nkondo, n'iy'uko amashami ya Batare na Bezi yavutse muri iryo tandukaniro — ni nkuru irekire cane, ihabwa ubugari bwayo.
