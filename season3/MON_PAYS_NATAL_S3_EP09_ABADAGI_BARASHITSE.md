# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP09 — ABADAGI BARASHITSE

Baravuye kure.

Ntibavuga ururimi rw'Uburundi. Bafise ibirwanisho Uburundi butigeze bubona. Kandi ntibaje gusura.

---

Kuva mu myaka ya **1890**, Abadagi bari bamaze gushika ku nkengera y'ikiyaga Tanganyika.

Ariko kwinjira ku nkengera ntibisobanura kwinjira mu gihugu.

Hagati y'ikiyaga n'ikirimba c'umwami, hari intambwe ndende — kandi Mwezi Gisabo ntiyari afise umutima wo kubareka bakinjira ata co avuze.

---

Amasoko yose ahurira ku kintu kimwe: **Mwezi Gisabo yabanje kurwanya ukwinjira kw'Abadagi**, mu myaka yinshi.

Ntitwoshaka gukoresha ijambo "guhagarika" — kuko ntiyabahagaritse igihe cose. Ariko ntiyabemereye kwinjira bidatevye, nk'uko biboneka ko babigira ahandi.

Yari umwami wamaze imyaka yigarurira ubwami bunini bwa se. Ntiyari kwemera ata kurwana, ko umuntu uwo ari we wese, uvuye hehe uwo ari we wese, aza kumugabanyiriza ubwami bwiwe.

---

Ariko Abadagi bari bafise ikintu Mwezi atari afise: ibirwanisho bishasha, hamwe n'ubushobozi bwo gushaka abamufasha mu gihugu ubwaco.

Kandi twaramaze kubona, mu ma-episode abiri ashize, ko Mwezi yari afise abo bamurwanya, imbere mu gihugu ubwaco: Kirima, mu buraruko bushira uburengero; Maconco, mu muryango w'ubwami ubwawo.

Abadagi ntibaraheza kubona ico gitangaza.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP10 — ABADAGI, KIRIMA NA MACONCO**, ni ho tuzoraba ukuntu Umudagi umwe, akaba ari umukuru w'ingabo, azoshaka ubufasha bw'abo bagabo babiri — bakaba baramaze imyaka bamurwanya — kugira ngo bose hamwe, bashire Mwezi Gisabo ku gitutu.
