# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP16 — UMURAGE

Twamukurikiranye kuva ku bwana bwiwe, munsi y'umurezi Ndivyariye.

Twaramubonye atsinda Twarereye i Nkondo, hanyuma agabanya ubushobozi bw'uwamurezeko.

Twaramubonye ahangana n'abandi banzi, bo mu muryango wiwe n'abatari bo, imbere y'uko abandi banyamahanga, bavuye kure y'ikiyaga Tanganyika, binjira mu nkuru yiwe.

Twaramubonye atsindwa, agatakaza ubushobozi mu Masezerano ya Kiganda, hanyuma agasubizwa — atari kubera imbaraga ziwe, ariko kubera inyungu z'abamutsinze.

Twaramubonye apfa, ku wa 21 Myandagaro 1908, i Buhonga, mu buryo bugifise agatima k'ibanga.

---

None se, ico Mwezi Gisabo yasize Uburundi, ni iki?

Yamaze ku ngoma hafi igice c'ikinjana.

Yarwanyije abamurwanya b'imbere mu gihugu, uko bavuye impande zitandukanye.

Yarahanganye n'ukwinjira kw'Abadagi, mu myaka irenga icumi.

Yarasinyanye Amasezerano ya Kiganda mu 1903, yemera ubutegetsi bw'Abadagi, ariko ubwami bugumaho.

Yarasubiye kumenyekana nk'Umwami w'Uburundi bwose mu 1905.

Yapfuye mu 1908, asiga ubwami bugikomeye nk'ikigo c'ubutegetsi — naho ubutegetsi bw'Abadagi bwari bumaze kwiyongera, buri bwose.

Umuhinga Émile Mworoha na Jean-Pierre Chrétien, mu gitabu barangiye kwandika kumwerekeye, bakoresha imvugo *"yarwaniye Uburundi."*

---

Inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe, uwamusubiriye ni umwana wiwe.

Amasoko amuha imyaka 13 kugeza 15 — akiri muto cane.

Ni co gituma, nk'uko twabibonye kuri Mwezi ubwiwe kera, igihugu carongowe n'abamufasha, umwami akiri muto.

Hano hari umugore wa Mwezi Gisabo, yitwa **Ririkumutima**, uwagize uruhara rukomeye muri ubwo butegetsi bw'abamufasha — naho hari amasoko avuga ko atari nyina w'amaraso w'umwami mushasha. Nyina we ariko yitwa **Ntibanyiha Bizama**, mu masoko amwe. Ntitwovanga abo babiri.

---

Uwo mwana yaje kwitwa **Mutaga IV Mbikije**.

Ariko ingoma yiwe ntizoba nk'iy'abamubanjirije.

Kuko igihe yaje ku ngoma, mu 1908, Uburundi ntibwari bukiri igihugu cishinze umutwe wenyene, nk'uko cyari kimeze igihe Ntare Rushatsi yatanguraga urwo rugendo, imyaka amajana menshi mbere.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 4 — MUTAGA IV MBIKIJE**, ni ho tuzokomeza.
