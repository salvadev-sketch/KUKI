# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP10 — ABADAGI, KIRIMA NA MACONCO

Umuntu arwanya umwami wiwe, ata bufasha, ashobora kuguma ari akaga gato.

Ariko umuntu arwanya umwami wiwe, afashijwe n'abanyamahanga bafise ibirwanisho bishasha — ni akaga kandi.

---

Umukuru w'ingabo z'Abadagi, yitwa **Von Berling**, yabonye ico Kirima na Maconco bari bamaze imyaka bafise: ubushotozi ku ngoma ya Mwezi Gisabo, abayoboke, n'ivyifuzo.

Aca abasanga.

---

Minisiteri y'Ubukerarugendo y'Uburundi ivuga yeruye: Von Berling yaronse ubufasha bwa Maconco na Kirima, maze abo bagabo babiri, bari basanzwe barwanya umwami, bafasha ingabo z'Abadagi kumushira ku gitutu.

Ntitwovuga ngo Abadagi bari bakunda Kirima na Maconco.

Ivyari bibashimishije ni ikindi kintu: **kugabanya ubushobozi bwa Mwezi, no kwinjira mu gihugu.**

Kirima na Maconco, na bo, bari bafise ivyifuzo vyabo bwite: kwikuza ku bushobozi bw'umwami babuze mu muryango w'ubwami, canke bagasa n'abatabuze ico bari kubona.

---

Ni ukuvuga: ivyifuzo vy'abagabo babiri bo mu Burundi, hamwe n'ivyifuzo vy'igihugu kivuye kure y'ubwoko bwose bw'Uburundi, byahavuye bihura.

Ntibari bafitaniye urukundo. Bari bafitaniye inyungu zihuriye — mu gihe kimwe gusa.

---

Kandi ico gihuza gitoya, hamwe n'ibirwanisho by'Abadagi bishasha, ni co cazoba imbaraga zitigeze ziboneka mu Burundi imbere yaho: umwami ntabwo yari azohangana n'abandi baganwa gusa, canke n'abantu bo hanze batazi ururimi rw'igihugu.

Yari azohangana n'ivyo vyombi, bikorana.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP11 — INTAMBARA 1896–1903**, ni ho tuzobona ico gihuza kigira ico gitanga — n'ukuntu Mwezi Gisabo azoharirwa ava ku murwa wiwe bwite, i Muramvya.
