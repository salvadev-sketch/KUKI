# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP13 — IGIHUGU KIRONGERA KWIYUNGA

Abadagi bari babonye ico bashaka: Uburundi bugabuwe hagati y'abategetsi batatu, ata umwe muri bo afise ubushobozi buhagije bwo kubarwanya.

Ariko mu myaka ibiri gusa, bagiye kubona ko iyo migambi itagenda nk'uko bari babyiteze.

---

Umutegetsi w'Umudagi, **Von Grawert**, yatanguye kubona ikintu gikomeye: mu rugendo yakoze mu Burundi mu 1904, yasanze abatware bo mu turere twinshi, tudakuye kure y'ikirimba, bagifata **Mwezi** nk'umwami wabo — atari Kirima canke Maconco.

Ico kibazo cabaye ingenzi cane ku Badagi: kugabura ubutegetsi mu bagabo batatu, bitari bibafasha kugenzura igihugu — vyabatera akaga gakomeye ko gutegeka igihugu badafise uwo bashingikiriza kubera icubahiro.

---

Aca ahindura politike.

Ku wa **15 Rusama 1905**, Von Grawert yafashe **Maconco**, aramwica i Usumbura.

Iyi ntsinzi imwe yahinduye vyose:

Maconco → arakurwaho → ubutegetsi bwa Mwezi burakomera.

---

Ku wa **8 Gitugutu 1905**, mu **Bukeye**, habaye itangazo ry'ingenzi: Mwezi Gisabo asubira kumenyekana nk'Umwami w'Uburundi bwose.

Iyo si intsinzi y'ubugarariji ku ruhande rw'Abadagi bakunda ubwigenge bw'Uburundi. Ni intsinzi y'imigambi yabo bwite: gucungera igihugu biciye ku mwami umwe, ari we bantu bakiri kwemera, aho kuba abategetsi batatu bagabuye igihugu.

---

Kirima, na we, ntiyagumye ari akaga gakomeye igihe kirekire.

Yafashwe i **Mibirizi**, mu Rwanda, mu **Ndamukiza 1906**, hanyuma ajanwa mu bunyagano i **Neu-Langenburg**, hafi y'ikiyaga Nyasa, mu bihugu bikwiragiye none nka Malawi.

---

Rero, mu myaka ibiri:

**1903** — Mwezi aratsindwa, ubushobozi burasama.

**1905** — Maconco arishwe; Mwezi arasubizwa kumenyekana.

**1906** — Kirima ajanwa mu bunyagano.

Iyi ni imwe mu mpinduka zikomeye cane z'ingoma ya Mwezi Gisabo: umwami yari amaze gutsindwa, akongera aragaruka — atari kubera imbaraga zamurwanira, ariko kubera inyungu z'abamutsinze.

---

Ariko urugendo rwa Mwezi Gisabo ntirurangira aha.

Kuko umwami wagumye ategeka imyaka mirongo itanu, atarwana intambara imwe canke ebyiri, ariko amagana — arashaje.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP14 — URUPFU RWA MWEZI**, ni ho tuzoraba iherezo ry'ubuzima bwiwe.
