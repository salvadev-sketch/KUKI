# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP03 — INTAMBARA YA NKONDO

Hari imvugo, mu Burundi bwa none, ikoreshwa igihe ikintu casheshe vy'ubusa, gitigera gishika ku ntumbero yaco.

**"Biracitse nk'ivy'i Nkondo."**

Abayikoresha benshi ntibazi aho yavuye.

Ni ho, mu ntango y'ingoma ya Mwezi Gisabo.

---

Twarereye, umwe mu bandi bahungu ba Ntare Rugamba, ntiyemera Mwezi nk'umwami.

Imigenzo ntitanga insiguro imwe rudende y'igituma — ariko dushobora kwibuka ico twabonye mu EP02: uturere twari twarahawe abahungu ba Ntare Rugamba twari twabatumye bose bafise ubushobozi, abantu, n'amatungo. Twarereye ni umwe muri abo.

Afise iryo vyifuzo, arafata ibirwanisho.

---

Mwezi Gisabo, ico gihe, yari akiri muto.

Isoko ry'Université du Burundi rivuga ko **Ndivyariye na Rwasha**, bombi, ari bo bayoboye ingabo zatsinze iza Twarereye, ahitwa **Nkondo**, hafi ya Muramvya.

Isoko rindi, Yaga Burundi, ryo ryerekana ikindi gice cy'inkuru: Mwezi yahunze aja mu bumanuko, hanyuma **Rwasha na Birori** baza kumushigikira mu kurwanya Twarereye.

Ntitwovuga ngo izo nkuru zibiri zivuguruzanya rwose — zishobora kuba zivuga ibihe bibiri bitandukanye vy'intambara imwe, canke amasoko yagiye ashimika ku bantu batandukanye. Ariko ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe ni iki: **Twarereye yaratsinzwe, kandi Ndivyariye na Rwasha (ku bumwe mu buryo) bagize uruhara rukomeye muri iyo ntsinzi.**

---

Ivyerekeye umubare w'abasirikare, canke uko intambara nyene yabaye ku buryo burambuye — igihe yamaze, uburyo bwakoreshejwe, ahandi hantu twaharwaniwe — ni make cane. Iyi ni imigenzo n'inkuru z'amateka, atari ivyanditswe n'umuntu yari ahari ico gihe.

---

Ariko ico gikorwa kimwe — intsinzi i Nkondo — carafashije Mwezi Gisabo ku buryo bubiri:

Ubwa mbere, umwanzi wiwe wa mbere, Twarereye, ntiyakomeje kuba akaga gakomeye ku ngoma yiwe.

Ubwa kabiri, kandi ni iki gikomeye kurusha ibindi: **Ndivyariye yaronse icubahiro gikomeye**, akaba yaragize uruhara mu kurwanira umwana yari yarezye.

Ariko iryo cubahiro nyene, ryo, ni ryo rizoba intandaro y'ikindi kibazo.

Kuko umwana ntagumana akiri umwana.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP04 — NDIVYARIYE ARABURA**, ni ho tuzoraba ico gihe Mwezi Gisabo amaze gukura — n'ico catumye Ndivyariye, uwamurezeko, uwamufashije gutsinda i Nkondo, aza kuba umwe mu ba mbere bamurwanya.
