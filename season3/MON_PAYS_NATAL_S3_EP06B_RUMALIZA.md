# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP06B — RUMALIZA

Mu banzi Mwezi Gisabo yagize, ntibari bose bo mu gihugu ciwe.

Hari uwavuye kure, akaba yaravuye ku bwo kudandaza — atari ubudandaji busanzwe.

---

Yitwa **Rumaliza**, izina ryiwe ry'ukuri akaba ari **Mohammed bin Khalfan**.

Yari umukuru w'abadandaza b'abacakara, bakorera hafi y'ikiyaga Tanganyika, mu myaka ya 1880.

---

Imigenzo n'ubushakashatsi bike dufise bivuga ko Mwezi Gisabo yarwanye na Rumaliza n'abo bari kumwe, hafi ya **1884**.

Ariko ico dufise kuri iyo ntambara ni umurongo umwe gusa: **"Yarwanye n'abadandaza b'abacakara ba Rumaliza."** Nta karere kavugwa. Nta buryo bw'intambara burambuye. Nta gihe kirambuye.

---

Aha dutegerezwa kwibuka indero yacu, uko twagiye tuyikurikiza kuva ku ntango: **kutamenya ni na ryo igice c'amateka.**

Ariko naho iri ijambo rimwe ari rito, rifise insobanuro ikomeye: rierekana ko Mwezi Gisabo atarwanya gusa abavukanyi biwe canke Abadagi. Yarwanije n'akaga gakomeye kandi katari ke ko ubudandaji bw'abacakara, bwari bwarakwiragiye mu karere kose k'ibiyaga bigari muri ico gihe.

---

Rumaliza ntiyaguma mu Burundi. Amaherezo, mu myaka yakurikiye, yatsindwa n'ingabo z'Abongereza n'iz'Ababiligi, ahandi mu karere.

Ariko iyi ntambwe ntoya mu ngoma ya Mwezi Gisabo yari ikimenyetso c'ikintu kikomeye kizoza: **Uburundi ntibwari bugikikuzwe n'ivyifuzo vy'imbere gusa. Isi yose y'akarere yari itangura kwinjira mu nkuru yabwo.**

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP07 — KIRIMA**, ni ho tuzobandanya, kuko abo Mwezi azohangana na bo, mu myaka izokurikira, na bo bazovuye kure, ariko bagakoresha uburyo butandukanye.
