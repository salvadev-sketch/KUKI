# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP04 — NDIVYARIYE ARABURA

Umwana akura.

Kandi umwami akura na we.

Mwezi Gisabo, uwari yaronse ubwami akiri muto, uwari yaronse intsinzi ya mbere i Nkondo afashijwe n'uwamurezeko, ubu ntiyari akiri uwo mwana.

Kandi ico yashaka, ntikiri ico Ndivyariye yashaka.

---

Amasoko avuga ko Mwezi, amaze gukura, ntiyashaka ko Ndivyariye aguma afise ubushobozi bwinshi nk'uko yari yabufise mu gihe c'ubwana bwiwe.

Yaronse abamufasha: **Rwasha na Birori**, abandi bavukanyi biwe, abo na bo bari barashigikiye intsinzi ya Nkondo.

Hamwe na bo, Mwezi yagabanije ubushobozi bwa Ndivyariye.

---

Ntitwabona amakuru arambuye ku buryo ico gikorwa cabaye — nimba cari ihiganwa ryo ku mugaragaro, canke ryabaye buhoro buhoro, mu myaka.

Ariko ico dushobora kuvuga ni ico gisigaye: **Ndivyariye arabura.**

Ntitwovuga ngo turazi ico catumye canke uwamwishe.

Imigenzo ivuga ko yishwe n'abantu bo ku kirimba — ariko uburyo nyabwo bw'urupfu rwiwe, canke n'ubushobozi bwo kuvuga ngo koko yarishwe, ntibwemejwe.

Ico dushobora kuvuga ni ukuvuga ko urupfu rwiwe — canke ukubura kwiwe — rwabaye rufunguye, kandi ko ata soko riduha inyishu yizewe.

---

Ariko ico kibazo, hamwe n'uko cakemuwe, nticarangiye na Ndivyariye ubwiwe.

Abana biwe n'abamukomotseko ntibibagiye ko se yari amaze gutakaza ubushobozi.

Umuhinga Jean-Pierre Chrétien avuga ko ubugarariji bw'abahungu ba Ndivyariye bwamaze **hafi imyaka mirongo itandatu** — kuva hafi 1870 gushika mu myaka ya 1930.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Chrétien (1993) yagize ati: « un véritable sanctuaire... vénéré par les grands chefs Batare » — arivyo bisobanura mu Kirundi ngo: "ahera cane... aharamywa n'abatware bakuru b'Ababare," ku bijanye n'ikigabiro ca Kamaramagambo. [FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Mwezi IV of Burundi") yemeza ko agace k'ubuseruko n'ubumanuko kayobowe n'abatware b'Ababare, bakomoka kuri Ntare Rugamba, kandi ko ivyo bigometse vyinshi vyavuye ku bahungu n'abuzukuru ba Ndivyariye. Iryo soko rishigikira ivy'uburemere bw'ako gace mu myaka ya Mwezi Gisabo. 🔴 *(Ariko imyaka nyakuri "1870-1930" y'uko ikigabiro ca Kamaramagambo nyene cakoreshejwe ntikiraboneka ku buryo bweruye.)*

Ni ukuvuga ko ikibazo kitari ic'umwami umwe n'umurezi wiwe.

Cari kimaze guhinduka ikindi kintu, kirekire kurusha ubuzima bw'umuntu umwe.

---

Aha ni ho tubona amashami abiri azoranga ubwami bw'Uburundi mu binjana bikurikira:

**Bezi** — abakomoka kuri Mwezi Gisabo.

**Batare** — abakomoka kuri Ntare Rugamba, biciye kuri Ndivyariye n'abandi bavukanyi biwe.

Ntitwovuga ngo Batare bose barwanya Bezi igihe cose. Amatati yarahinduka bivanye n'abantu n'ibihe, uko imyaka yagiye igenda.

Ariko intandaro yayo — umwana n'umurezi wiwe, ubushobozi bwasangiwe hanyuma bugatakara — igumye ari iyo dushobora kubona neza, muri iyi documentaire.

---

Ndivyariye ntabwo yari umwanzi wa mbere Mwezi Gisabo yagize.

Kandi ntabwo yari uwa nyuma.

Kuko mu turere twagutse Ntare Rugamba yari yarasize, hari abandi bavukanyi, abandi bagabo, abandi batware — bose bafise ivyifuzo vyabo bwite ku ngoma nshasha.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP05 — RWASHA NA BIRORI**, ni ho tuzoraba abo bavukanyi babiri bashigikiye Mwezi mu ntango y'ingoma yiwe — n'uturere twabo bwite, two mu buseruko n'ubumanuko bw'Uburundi.
