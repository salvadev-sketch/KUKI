# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP02 — NDIVYARIYE UMUREZI

Umwana ntashobora gutegeka.

Ku ngoma, ico ni ikibazo gikomeye — kuko igihugu ntigitegereza ko umwami akura.

Mwezi Gisabo yari uwo mwana.

Kandi uwamurezeko, uwamurindiye, uwamufashije gutegeka mu gihe atarabishoboye, ni **Ndivyariye**.

---

Ndivyariye yari umuvukanyi wa Mwezi — umwe mu bahungu ba Ntare Rugamba, uwahawe akarere ka Bweru na se, nk'uko twabibonye mu Season 2.

Ariko inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntare Rugamba, Ndivyariye ntiyagumye ari umutware wa Bweru gusa.

Amasoko amwe, harimwo Yaga Burundi, avuga ko yabaye **umurezi** wa Mwezi Gisabo, mu gihe uyu yari akiri umwana.

Ni ukuvuga: Ndivyariye yari afise ubushobozi bubiri icarimwe — yari mu muryango w'ubwami, kandi yari afise uruhara mu kuyobora igihugu mu izina ry'umwami adashobora ku giti ciwe.

---

Ubwo bushobozi bwabiri, mu ntango, bwari ingirakamaro.

Ariko ubushobozi bumaze imyaka, bufatirwa n'umuntu umwe, buragora kwitandukanya na we.

Ni na ko byari kuba hagati ya Mwezi na Ndivyariye — nk'uko amasoko atandukanye abivuga.

---

Ariko ni ikihe catumye amakimbirane atangura?

Hano dutegerezwa kwitonda: ubushakashatsi bw'Université du Burundi bwerekana ko **imigenzo y'akanwa itanga insiguro zibiri**, atari imwe.

**Insiguro ya mbere** ivuga ko ikibazo cari ic'ugusubira ku ngoma ubwako. Iyi nkuru ivuga ko, inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntare Rugamba, Ndivyariye yashigikiye uwundi muvukanyi — **Twarereye** — nk'uwokwiye kwima, atari Mwezi. Ariko igihe Ndivyariye yagarutse ku kirimba, Mwezi yari amaze kwimikwa, afashijwe na **Rwasha na Birori**.

Ivyo vyatumye havuka imigwi ibiri: Ndivyariye na Twarereye ku ruhande rumwe; Mwezi, Rwasha, Birori, na Busumano ku rundi.

**Insiguro ya kabiri** ivuga ikindi kintu: ikibazo ntabwo cari ic'ugusubira ku ngoma. Cari ic'**amatongo n'ubutunzi**. Ndivyariye yagirizwa kuba yariyegukiye amatongo meza cane, ayegereye ikirimba c'umwami — atari kwikubira ubutegetsi, ni ukwikubira ivyiza.

Ntitwovuga ngo tuzi neza insiguro nyayo hagati ya zo zombi. Ni imigenzo y'akanwa, kandi imigenzo, mu bihe nk'ivyo, ikunda kwiganjiramwo ikibazo kimwe, kigatuma ibindi bikwirakwira.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga neza ni iki: mu ntango y'ingoma ya Mwezi Gisabo, umwana w'umwami yari akiri muto, afise umurezi ukomeye, uwo umurezi na we afise ivyifuzo vyiwe bwite.

Kandi imbere y'uko ico kibazo gikemuke — imbere y'uko Mwezi akura, imbere y'uko yigarurire ubutegetsi bwiwe — hari intambara imwe igomba kuja imbere.

Twarereye ntiyari kubyemera atarwana.
