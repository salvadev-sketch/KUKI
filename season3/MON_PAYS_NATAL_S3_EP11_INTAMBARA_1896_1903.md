# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP11 — INTAMBARA 1896–1903

Umwami wagumye ategeka imyaka mirongo itanu ntiyari kwemera vy'ubusa ko ibwami ryiwe rigwa.

Ariko intambara ntizibarwa ku vyifuzo vy'umwami.

Zibarwa ku bushobozi.

---

Kuva mu **1896**, Abadagi bagerageje kwinjira imbere mu gihugu.

Mwezi Gisabo yaramaze igihe kirekire atemera kuganduka.

Iyi si intambara imwe, iyabereye umusi umwe. Ni imyaka irenga icumi, y'ibitero bike bike, ubwirinzi, n'ihiganwa — hagati y'ingabo z'Abadagi, zifashijwe na Kirima na Maconco, n'ingabo za Mwezi.

---

Chronology imwe ishira **1903** nk'umwaka w'ingenzi — aho Mwezi yatsinzwe mu buryo bwa gisirikare, biciye ku bufasha Abadagi baronse bo mu gihugu ubwaco.

Mu gihe c'urugamba, ibwami i **Muramvya** — icicaro ca Mwezi kuva kera — haratewe.

---

Urubuga rwa Leta y'Uburundi ruvuga ko Abadagi n'abari babashigikiye bahatiye Mwezi kuva i Muramvya, inyuma y'ibitero n'ugutwika ibice vy'ibwami.

Mwezi arava i Muramvya, aja **i Kiganda**.

Hari n'isoko rimwe, iry'umuhinga Émile Mworoha, rivuga ko Mwezi yagiye i **Burunga** — ahantu h'ingenzi mu migenzo y'ubwami — imbere y'uko ibintu bigera ku musozo w'intambara.

Amasoko ntahurira ku rukurikirane runaka rw'ibintu byabaye. Ntitwoshaka guhimba urukurikirane nk'aho hari isoko imwe gusa.

---

Ariko ico dushobora kuvuga neza, aho amasoko ahurira, ni iki: **umwami wa Burundi yari ariko arasubira inyuma imbere y'ingabo z'Abadagi.**

Ariko ntaraherwa ingoma.

Kuko ico gikurikira — ico gitangaje kurusha uko wokwiteze — ni co kizoranga inkuru ya Mwezi Gisabo kurusha ikindi cose.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP12 — AMASEZERANO YA KIGANDA**, ni ho tuzobibona.
