# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP13B — MWEZI N'IDINI

Twaramaze kubona Mwezi Gisabo nk'umwami w'intambara — arwanya abavukanyi, arwanya Kirima na Maconco, arwanya Abadagi.

Ariko uruhara rwiwe mu buzima bw'Uburundi ntirwari intambara gusa.

---

Mu bushakashatsi bumwe, Mwezi Gisabo yabonwa nk'**"Umuvyeyi w'Igihugu"** — umuntu w'idini kurusha uwa politike, yubahwa nk'umuntu w'amayobera.

Ntitwovuga ngo iyi ni yo nsobanuro imwe rudende y'ubuntu bwiwe. Ariko iyi mvyerekano igaragara mu bushakashatsi, kandi ifise ikimenyetso kiyishigikiye.

---

## Umuganuro n'imigenzo

Nk'uko abami bamubanjirije bagenje, Mwezi Gisabo yagumije **Umuganuro**, umunsi mukuru w'isarura, hamwe n'imigenzo yagendanaga na Karyenda — ivyo twize kera muri Season 1.

Ubwami ntibwari ubutegetsi bwa gisirikare gusa. Bwari na none uruhara mu idini n'imigenzo y'igihugu.

---

## Igikorwa ku bamisiyonari

Hari inkuru imwe ishimishije cane, iduha ishusho y'uwo Mwezi yari we, mu mutima wiwe.

Mu **1899**, hari **abamisiyonari ba Gatolika** bashinze ikigo i **Mugera**.

Imigenzo ivuga ko Mwezi Gisabo yagerageje **kubirukana**, akoresheje ibirwanisho vy'imigenzo — uburozi n'ubuhanuzi, aho gukoresha ingabo.

Ariko hari **uwundi mupfumu** yamubwiye ko akwiye guhagarika ico gikorwa, kuko **Imana** ubwayo izokingira abo bamisiyonari.

Mwezi Gisabo arahagarika.

---

Iyi nkuru ni ngufi, ariko ifise insobanuro ikomeye: Mwezi ntiyakoresheje ingabo ku bamisiyonari, nk'uko yabigenje kuri Kirima na Maconco. Yakoresheje uburyo bw'idini n'imigenzo — kandi yareke, igihe uwundi mupfumu yamubwiye ko ari uko bikwiye.

Ivyo bituma tubona uruhara Mwezi yari afise, atari nk'umwami w'intambara gusa, ariko nk'umuntu w'idini, ashobora kugumya canke guhagarika ibikorwa bishingiye ku myemerere, atari ku nguvu z'ingabo gusa.

---

## Ubutegetsi bugabuwe mu bice bine

Mu myaka mirongo itanu y'ingoma yiwe, hashizweho uburyo bw'ubutegetsi bugabuwe mu bice bine: agace k'imbere, munsi y'ubutegetsi bw'umwami ku giti ciwe; agace k'abavukanyi biwe canke abahungu biwe, bakomeye kumwegera; agace kagutse, ko mu buseruko no mu bumanuko; n'agace ka Batare.

Iyi mvyerekano ifasha kwerekana ko ubwami budasanzwe bushingiye ku ntambara zonyene, ariko no ku miterere y'ubutegetsi bwuzuye, bufise ivyiciro bitandukanye.

---

Aya makuru make aduha ishusho itandukanye ya Mwezi Gisabo, itari iy'umwami w'intambara gusa — ariko iy'umwami afise uruhara mu buzima bw'idini n'imigenzo y'igihugu ciwe, kandi bikaba ari ryo, mu buryo bumwe, ryamugumije akaba icibutso c'akaranga k'Uburundi, ata guhera ku ntambara yagize.
