# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP05 — RWASHA NA BIRORI

Ntidukwiye kwibwira ko amashami yose ya Ntare Rugamba yari ku ruhande rumwe.

Iyi ni insiguro yoroshe cane, ariko ikomeye kurusha uko yoboneka.

---

Twaramaze kubona Ndivyariye — umurezi yaje kuba umwanzi.

Ariko Ndivyariye ntiyari we wenyene mu bahungu ba Ntare Rugamba bari bafise ubushobozi.

Hari na **Rwasha na Birori**.

---

Rwasha, twaramaze kumubona: ni we, hamwe na se, bafashe Ubuyogoma, mu Season 2. Ni na we, hamwe na Ndivyariye, batsinda Twarereye i Nkondo.

Birori na we, ku ruhande rwiwe, yari umwe mu bahungu ba Ntare Rugamba bafise ubushobozi bukomeye — kandi imigenzo ivuga ko na we yagize uruhara mu gukingira ubutegetsi bwa Mwezi, mu gihe Twarereye yarwanya umwami.

---

Ni kuki abo babiri bahavuye baba ingenzi?

Kubera ico twabonye mu Season 2: Ntare Rugamba yari yarahaye abahungu biwe uturere twagutse, kugira ngo babe abategetsi. Ivyo vyatumye Rwasha na Birori bagira abantu, ubutaka, n'ububasha — nk'uko Ndivyariye na Twarereye na bo bari babifise.

Mwezi Gisabo, akiri muto, yari akeneye ubwo bushobozi kugira ngo arinde ingoma yiwe. Rwasha na Birori bamuhaye ubwo bufasha.

---

Ariko hano hari ikintu gikomeye tudakwiye kwibagirwa, imbere yo kubandanya:

**Batare, mu buryo bwose, ntibari abagarariji.**

Inyandiko y'Université du Burundi ivuga ko, inyuma y'ukubura kwa Ndivyariye, ari **bamwe** mu bamukomokako bakomeje kurwanya Mwezi — atari bose. Naho **abakomoka kuri Rwasha, bo mu buseruko, bagumye ari intahemuka ku mwami**.

Ni ukuvuga: **Batare ≠ abagarariji bose.**

Hari abaganwa bakomoka kuri Ntare Rugamba bashobora kuba bari ku ruhande rwa Mwezi. Abandi bakamurwanya.

Ikarata y'ubushobozi ntiyari yoroshe nk'uko yosa — atari "Batare kuruhande, Bezi kurundi." Yari igoye kurusha ico.

---

Iyi ni imwe mu ngingo za ngenzi za iyi documentaire: ukwirinda kworohereza amateka mu buryo bworoshe cane, kugira turusheho kwerekana ukuntu vyagenze koko.

---

Ariko naho Mwezi yari amaze kwironkera abamufasha bo mu muryango wiwe bwite, hari abandi banzi biwe, batari abavukanyi — bagenda bakwirakwira uko igihugu kigutse.
