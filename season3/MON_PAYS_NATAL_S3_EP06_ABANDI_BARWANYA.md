# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP06 — ABANDI BARWANYA

Ndivyariye. Twarereye. Ndivyariye n'abamukomotseko, mu Bweru.

Ariko izo si zo mvo zonyene z'akaga Mwezi Gisabo yagize kugira ngo arinde ingoma yiwe.

Igihugu cari kinini. Kandi abavukanyi ntibari bo bonyene bafise ivyifuzo vyabo.

---

Yaga Burundi ivuga abatware batandukanye bigeze guhagurukira Mwezi, mu bihe bitandukanye vy'ingoma yiwe:

**Rwoga**, mu myaka ya 1870.

**Kibango**, mu myaka ya 1890.

**Rwerekanabirenge**, mu buraruko bushira ubuseruko.

**Bihinda**, i Banga.

**Biroro**, mu bumanuko, hafi y'ikiyaya ca Rusizi.

---

Ku bo, amasoko dufise ubu ntaduha inkuru irambuye — ni amazina, uturere, n'ibihe gusa. 🔴 *(Aya mazina n'ibihe bikeneye kwongera kwemezwa mu kindi gitabo — ntarasangwa muri Chrétien canke Nduwamahoro.)*

Ntitwoshaka guhimba icobayeko, canke inkuru zabo bwite, ata co amasoko dufise adutangira. Twovuga gusa ico dushobora kuvuga: ko bahagurukiye ubutegetsi bwa Mwezi, mu bihe n'uturere bitandukanye, kandi ko batsinzwe canke bakoreshejwe mu buryo butarasigaye kirambuye mu migenzo dufise.

---

Ico dukwiye kwibuka, ku bwabo, ni ikintu kimwe gikomeye:

Ingoma ya Mwezi Gisabo ntiyari ifise **ikibazo kimwe** yagira kwitonderako.

Yari ifise abahiganwa benshi, mu bihe bitandukanye, mu turere dutandukanye — bamwe bo mu muryango, abandi batari bo.

Iyo ni myumvire tudakwiye kwibagirwa mu nkuru iri kwiza: Mwezi Gisabo, umwami tuzoza kubona ageze ku bwami bunini kurusha uko bwigeze kuba, yatanguye ategeka mu gihugu cari cikwiragiye ivyifuzo, atari agasaganya ka Kirima na Maconco bonyene.

---

Kuko, mu bahiganwa bose Mwezi yahuye na bo, hari babiri bazoba bakomeye kurusha abandi bose — kandi bazoza kuboneka, atari mu rugo, ariko biciye ku bandi bantu bashasha, bazoza kure y'ikiyaga Tanganyika.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP07 — KIRIMA**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
