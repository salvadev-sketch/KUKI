# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP15 — AHO YAHAMBWE

Inyandiko y'Intara ya Kayanza iraduha ahantu.

Inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe, umubiri wa Mwezi Gisabo wajanywe i **Nkiko-Mugamba**.

Aho **inganzo yiwe** — imva y'umwami — iri ku **musozi wa Remera**, mu karere ka Muruta, komine Muruta.

Kandi iri munsi y'uburinzi bw'**Abiru** — abo bacunga imigenzo n'ivy'idini vy'ubwami.

---

Iyi ni imwe mu nkuru zitworohera kubona ahantu nyakuri, atari imigenzo gusa. Nk'uko twabibonye kuri Gishora, mu Season 2, hari ahantu amateka y'Uburundi ashobora kwerekana, atari kuvuga gusa.

---

Ariko hari ikintu kimwe tuguma nako, kuva mu ntango y'iyi Season: izina **"Bikata-Bijoga."**

Twaravuze, mu EP01, ko nta muhinga washoboye gusobanura neza aho ryakomotse.

Ubu, tugeze ku iherezo ry'ubuzima bwa Mwezi Gisabo, twoshobora kwibaza: none, ni ryari iryo zina ryamuhawe? Ku bwana bwiwe? Mu ntsinzi ye ya mbere i Nkondo? Inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe, nk'ikimenyetso c'ico yari?

Ntitwovuga ngo tuzi.

Kandi ni vyo bikwiye — kuko izina ry'umwami, mu bihe nk'ivyo, ryagiye rikura hamwe n'inkuru yiwe, atari ikintu twoshobora gushira ku itariki imwe.

---

Icatumye tumwibuka, atari izina ryonyene, ni urugendo yagize: kuva ku bwana, munsi y'umurezi yaje kuba umwanzi; kubura ubushobozi mu ntambara y'imbere mu gihugu; guhura n'abanyamahanga bavuye kure y'ikiyaga Tanganyika; gutakaza ubushobozi bwiwe mu Masezerano ya Kiganda; kongera kumenyekana, atari kubera imbaraga ziwe, ariko kubera inyungu z'abamutsinze; hanyuma, amaherezo, urupfu rukigumana agatima k'ibanga.

---

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP16 — UMURAGE**, ni ho tuzoraba ico Mwezi Gisabo yasigiye Uburundi — n'ico gikurikira, igihe umwana wiwe azoza gufata ingoma, mu gihe c'impinduka nini kurusha izo Uburundi bwari bumaze kubona.
