# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP08 — MACONCO

Rimwe na rimwe, amateka manini atangurira ku kintu gitoya cane.

Ikibazo tugiye kuraba muri iyi episode catanguye ku mbwa.

---

**Maconco** ntiyari umuntu wo hanze y'umuryango w'ubwami, nka Kirima.

Amasoko amwita **umukwe wa Mwezi Gisabo** — ni ukuvuga yari yarongoye umukobwa w'umwami.

Yari umuntu akomeye, afise ubushobozi n'abayoboke.

---

Ariko umubano wiwe na Mwezi urononekara — kandi impamvu, nk'uko amakuru menshi abivuga, ni imbwa.

Imbwa yitwa **Rurebeya**, canke **Mushuzo** — amazina atandukanye avuye ku masoko atandukanye.

Mwezi yari yafashe iyo mbwa.

Maconco ntiyabyemeye.

---

Ntitwoshaka gutwarwa n'iyo nkuru nk'aho ari yo yonyene yatumye amatati yakura hagati yabo. Imbwa imwe ntishobora, ku yindi mvo, gutera intambara nini. Ariko iyi nkuru y'imbwa igaragara mu masoko menshi nk'intandaro yavuzwe — kandi biboneka ko yaba ikimenyetso c'ikindi kintu gikomeye kurusha: ugusuzugura hagati y'umwami n'umukwe wiwe, mu buryo bw'icubahiro.

---

Umubano wa Mwezi na Maconco urononekara.

Maconco arigomeka.

Kandi nk'uko twabibonye kuri Kirima, umuntu afise abayoboke n'ubushobozi, adashaka kwemera ubutegetsi bw'umwami, ni akaga gakomeye ku ngoma.

---

Ubu, dufise Mwezi Gisabo afise imbere yiwe abanzi babiri bakomeye: Kirima, mu buraruko bushira uburengero, na Maconco, mu muryango w'ubwami ubwawo.

Ariko hari ikindi kigiye guhindura vyose.

Kuko izo ntambara zo imbere mu gihugu, mu myaka izokurikira, ntizizoguma ari ivya Uburundi bwonyene.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 3 — EP09 — ABADAGI BARASHITSE**, ni ho tuzobona ukuntu abantu bavuye kure, bafise ibirwanisho bitigeze biboneka mu Burundi, batanguye kwinjira mu gihugu — kandi ukuntu bazoza kubona muri Kirima na Maconco ikintu bashaka.
