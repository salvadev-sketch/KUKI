# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP20 — BIKOMAGU: 1993–96, IPEREREZA

Ndadaye amaze gutorwa, ntiyaciye akuraho Bikomagu.

Bikomagu yagumye ari **Chef d'État-Major**, mu ntango y'ubutegetsi bwa Ndadaye — naho Ndadaye yari afise umugambi wo kuvugurura inzego z'umutekano: gutandukanya igisirikare na gendarmerie, kwongereza uburinganire bw'abaturuka mu turere n'imigwi itandukanye.

Ntitwovuga ko Bikomagu yari yanse ivyo vugururwa — nta gihamya dufise.

---

Inyuma y'ico gikorwa, Bikomagu yatangaje ko kirangiye, ategeka abasirikare gusubira mu bigo vyabo.

Ariko ivyo ntibihanagura amakenga yose ku ruhara rwiwe imbere y'ico gikorwa — nk'uko twabonye muri EP08 na EP11.

---

Commission internationale d'enquête ya ONU yasanze ingabo z'Uburundi zaragize uruhara — rutaziguye canke rutaziguye — mu bikorwa vyakurikiye ico gikorwa, harimwo iyicwa rya Ndadaye.

Ariko ivyo ntibisobanura "Bikomagu ni we yategetse ibintu vyose." Ni co gituma tuvuga "basirikare bamwe" canke "igice c'igisirikare," atari "igisirikare cose."

---

Mu **1999**, urubanza rw'ivyabaye ku wa 21 Gitugutu 1993 rwageze i Cour Suprême.

Abantu 79 baregwa. Bataniya baciriwe urwo gupfa.

**Abandi 38 barekuwe** — muri bo harimwo **Colonel Jean Bikomagu.**

---

Ntitwovuga "urukiko rwaramwehereye." Tuvuga: **urukiko rwamurekuye kubera kubura ibimenyetso bihagije vyo kumuhamya.**

Ntitwovuga na ngo yari "umutima mweru rwose" — ni ikintu urukiko ntacyavuze.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP21 — NTIBANTUNGANYA: INTAMBARA 1994–96**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
