# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP06 — PONTIEN KARIBWAMI: INTANGO NA FRODEBU

Amakuru arambuye ku bwana bwa Pontien Karibwami — aho yavukiye, umuryango wiwe — ni make mu masoko dufise ubu. Ni ikibazo tugumana uko kiri.

---

Ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe ni uko yari amaze kugira uruhara muri FRODEBU imbere y'uko iyo mugambwe iva mu kinyegero.

Centre des Grands Lacs, muri Université d'Anvers, ibitse inyandiko za FRODEBU zo mu 1991–1992, zisinywe na Karibwami ubwiwe — ikimenyetso ko yari asanzwe ari umunyamuryango w'ingenzi, atari umuntu yagaragaye gusa mu 1993.

---

Ku wa **29 Ruheshi 1993**, ariko amatora y'Abashingamateka:

**FRODEBU — imyanya 65 kuri 81.**

**UPRONA — imyanya 16 kuri 81.**

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("1993 Burundian parliamentary election") na IPU (Inter-Parliamentary Union, archive.ipu.org) bemeza itariki (29 Ruheshi 1993) n'imyanya (FRODEBU 65, UPRONA 16, ku myanya 81 yose).

---

Inama Nshingamateka nshasha yatoye **Pontien Karibwami** kuba **Umukuru wayo**, **Gilles Bimazubute** aba **Icegera ciwe.**

---

Uwo mwanya wari ukomeye cane — kuko wari uwa gatatu mu ruhererekane rw'ubutegetsi, inyuma ya Perezida na Leta.

Ntitwovuga ko Karibwami yari "Perezida wa kabiri" canke "Vice-Président." Yari Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka gusa — ariko uwo mwanya wari uzoba ingenzi cane, mu bihe biri imbere.
