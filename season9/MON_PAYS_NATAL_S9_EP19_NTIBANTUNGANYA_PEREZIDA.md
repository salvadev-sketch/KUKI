# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP19 — NTIBANTUNGANYA: ABA PEREZIDA

Ku wa **23 Kigarama 1993**, Sylvestre Ntibantunganya yatorewe kuba **Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka** — kubera ko uwahoze ari uwo mwanya, Pontien Karibwami, yari yishwe.

---

Aha yaje kuba umuntu w'ingenzi cane mu gushaka uburyo inzego z'igihugu zishobora gusubira gukora.

Ntitwovuge ko yaciye aba Perezida ubwo nyene — yabanje kuba Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka, mu myaka igoye cane y'igihugu.

---

Hari ibintu bibiri vyamufashije kuboneka nk'umuntu ashobora kwemerwa n'impande zitandukanye za politike: yari umunywanyi wa FRODEBU, ariko yari kandi yarigeze gukora muri UPRONA — nk'uko twize muri EP10.

---

Ku wa **6 Ndamukiza 1994**, inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ntaryamira (twize mu ma-episode akurikira), Sylvestre Ntibantunganya yaciye aba **Umukuru w'Igihugu**, mu buryo bw'agateganyo.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Sylvestre Ntibantunganya," "Timeline of Burundian history") yemeza itariki (6 canke 8 Ndamukiza 1994, bivanye n'isoko) — twahinduye "8 Nzero 1994" kuko iyo tariki itaboneka ku masoko yose, kandi ntibishoboka ko yaba Umukuru w'Igihugu imbere y'uko Ntaryamira yatorwa (13 Nzero) na mbere y'uko arahira (5 Ruhuhuma).

Yasubiriye Cyprien Ntaryamira, inyuma y'urupfu rwiwe.

Ariko na we, nka Ntaryamira, **ntiyatorewe n'amatora y'abanyagihugu.** Yatorewe n'Inama Nshingamateka, mu gihe igihugu cari mu kibazo ca politike.

---

Uruhererekane rw'abakuru b'igihugu rwari rwaratomotse:

Ndadaye (yatowe n'abanyagihugu) → Karibwami (yishwe, atarigeze arongora) → Ntaryamira (yatowe n'Inama Nshingamateka) → Ntibantunganya (yatowe n'Inama Nshingamateka).

Imyaka itarenga umwaka, Uburundi bwari bumaze kubona abakuru b'igihugu bane.
