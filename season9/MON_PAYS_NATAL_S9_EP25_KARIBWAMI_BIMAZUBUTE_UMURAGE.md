# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP25 — KARIBWAMI NA BIMAZUBUTE: UMURAGE

Pontien Karibwami na Gilles Bimazubute barishwe uwo munsi nyene na Ndadaye, ku wa 21 Gitugutu 1993.

---

Urupfu rwabo rwagize ingaruka zirenze ubuzima bwabo bwite: kubera ko Karibwami ari we yari ategekanijwe gusubirira Perezida, hakurikijwe Itegeko Nshinga, urupfu rwiwe ni rwo rwatumye habaho ikibazo gikomeye c'uwosubirira Ndadaye — ikibazo cashikanye ku guhindura Itegeko Nshinga no gutora Cyprien Ntaryamira, twize muri EP16.

---

Bimazubute, uwari amaze imyaka irenga mirongo itatu mu vya politike — kuva UCJAB na JNR mu 1959, agera ku kuba Icegera c'Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka mu 1993 — na we yaguye uwo musi nyene.

Urugendo rwiwe rwerekana ikintu gikomeye: Bimazubute ntiyari umunyapolitike mushasha wo mu 1993. Yari amaze kubaho impinduka nyinshi z'Uburundi — kuva ku bwami, kuja kuri Repubulika, agera no kuri demokarasi y'amashirahamwe menshi.

---

Bombi, hamwe na Ndadaye, barahambwe ku Vugizo, i Bujumbura — ahantu twize kera, muri Season 5, ko hari hafise insobanuro ikomeye mu mateka ya politike y'Uburundi.

---

Karibwami na Bimazubute ntibari abakuru b'igihugu.

Ariko ikibanza cabo mu ruhererekane rw'ubutegetsi — Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka n'Icegera ciwe — ni co catumye urupfu rwabo ruba n'ingaruka za constitutionnelle, atari uruhombo rw'abantu babiri gusa.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP26 — SYLVIE KINIGI: UMURAGE**, ni ho tuzoheza inkuru yiwe.
