# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP10 — SYLVESTRE NTIBANTUNGANYA: URUGENDO RURERURE

Sylvestre Ntibantunganya yavutse ku wa **8 Rusama 1956**, i Nyamutobo.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Wikipedia ("Sylvestre Ntibantunganya") yemeza itariki (8 Rusama 1956) ariko ivuga ko ikibanza ari komine Gishubi, intara ya Gitega — atari Nyabiraba nk'uko episode ivuga. Twasize "Nyamutobo" (umusozi) kuko ata soko ryawuvuguruza, ariko twasize ikimenyetso 🟡 ku bijanye n'izina ry'akomine.

Igihe yari umusore, yigeze kwiyumvira kuba umupadiri — ni co catumye aja muri **Petit Séminaire de Mugera**.

---

Ariko ntiyabandanije inzira y'ubusaseridoti.

Yaje kwiga amateka n'ubumenyi bw'isi, arangiza afise licence — mu nzira igoye, ikwiragiye no mu Rwanda (1979), amaherezo asubira mu Burundi kurangiza amashure yiwe.

---

Hagati ya 1984 na 1987, yakoreye **Radio-Télévision Nationale du Burundi**, nk'umunyamakuru.

---

Ariko urugendo rwiwe rwa politike ntirwatanguriye muri FRODEBU, nk'uko benshi boshobora kwibaza.

Hagati ya **1979 na 1983**, yari mu ishirahamwe rya politike ryitwa **UBU (Union des Travailleurs du Burundi)**, ryafitaniye isano n'ivyiyumviro vya Marxism.

Hanyuma, hagati ya **1986 na 1992**, yari mu ibikorwa vya **FRODEBU**, iri mu kinyegero.

Ariko **hagati ya 1987 na 1991**, na none, yakoreye muri **UPRONA**, umugambwe wari ku butegetsi — aba umunyamabanga mukuru ajejwe amakuru, hanyuma ajejwe Institut Rwagasore.

---

Ni ukuvuga: umuntu umwe, mu myaka mike, yagiye ava muri UBU, aja muri FRODEBU y'ibanga, akongera akaja muri UPRONA, hanyuma agasubira muri FRODEBU igihe iyo shirahamwe rija ku mugaragaro.

Ntitwovuga ngo ivyo ari ukwiyunga ata nsobanuro — ivyo bishobora kuba biratwereka uko politike y'Uburundi y'ico gihe yari igoye, ata mirongo yoroshe hagati y'imigambwe.

---

Ku wa **29 Ruheshi 1993**, atorwa aba umushingamateka wa FRODEBU i Gitega.

Ku wa **10 Mukakaro 1993**, agenwa kuba **Umushikiranganji w'Imigenderanire n'Amahanga**, muri Leta ya Ndadaye.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Sylvestre Ntibantunganya") yemeza ko yatorewe mu Nama Nshingamateka inyuma y'intsinzi ya FRODEBU muri Mukakaro 1993, kandi ko yagenywe Minisitiri w'Imigenderanire n'Amahanga ku wa 10 Mukakaro, akorera munsi ya Kinigi.
