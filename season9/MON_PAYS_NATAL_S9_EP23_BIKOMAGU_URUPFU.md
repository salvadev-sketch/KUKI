# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP23 — BIKOMAGU: INYUMA — SENATEUR, URUPFU

Mu **1997**, raporo ya ONU ivuga ko abasirikare 53 bari bashikirijwe ubutungane kubera uruhara bakekwagako muri coup yananiwe yo mu 1993.

Muri bo harimwo: **Colonel Charles Ntakije** (uwahoze ari Umushikiranganji w'Ingabo), **Colonel Jean Bikomagu**, na **François Ngeze** (umushingamateka wa UPRONA).

Ntitwovuga "Bikomagu yari umunyacaha" kubera iyo mvo yonyene — kuba yarashikirijwe urubanza ni ikintu kimwe, ico urubanza rwemeje (uwo twize muri EP20: yarekuwe mu 1999) ni ikindi.

---

Mu **1996**, Commission internationale d'enquête ya ONU yashikirije raporo yayo ya nyuma. Yerekanye ko coup yari ifise uruhara rw'abasirikare bari ku rwego rwo hejuru, ariko ntiyashoboye kwemeza uruhara rwa buri muntu.

---

Inyuma yo kuva ku buyobozi bw'igisirikare mu 1996, Bikomagu ntiyahise ava mu buzima bwa Leta.

Yagize uruhara mu nzego z'inzibacyuho, hanyuma akora muri **SOCABU** (ishirahamwe ry'ubwishingizi).

---

Ku wa **15 Myandagaro 2015**, **Jean Bikomagu yarishwe mu gitero c'abantu bitwaje ibirwanisho, i Bujumbura.**

Urupfu rwiwe rwabaye mu yindi krize ikomeye ya politike yari yatanguye muri uwo mwaka — ijanye n'igihe Perezida Pierre Nkurunziza yashakaga manda ya gatatu.

---

Umuntu yari mu bantu bakuru cane b'igisirikare igihe umwami wa nyuma yishwe (Ntare V, 1972 — Season 6), akaba na we, imyaka 43 inyuma, yaje kwicwa muri ubundi buryo bw'ubugizi bwa nabi bw'Uburundi.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP24 — NTIBANTUNGANYA: ARUSHA N'UMUSENATEUR**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
