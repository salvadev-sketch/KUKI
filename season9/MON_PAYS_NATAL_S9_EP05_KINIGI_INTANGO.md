# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP05 — SYLVIE KINIGI: INTANGO NA PREMIER MINISTRE

Yavutse **Sylvie Ntigashira**, hafi 1952/1953, i Mugoyi.

Se yari umudandaza, nyina akora uburimyi.

Ntiyakuriye mu muryango wa politike.

---

Yize kuri Université du Burundi, mu bijanye n'ubutunzi n'amabanki, arangiza mu 1979.

Yakoze muri **Banque de la République du Burundi**, kuva 1990 ayobora igisata c'ubushakashatsi n'imibare.

Mu 1991, agizwa umuntu ajejwe programme d'ajustement structurel y'igihugu.

Yari kandi afitaniye isano na **UPRONA**, agira uruhara mu bikorwa vy'Union des Femmes Burundaises.

---

Ndadaye, umunyapolitike wa FRODEBU, amaze gutsinda amatora, ntiyashatse Leta ya FRODEBU gusa.

Yahisemwo **Sylvie Kinigi**, Umututsikazi wo muri UPRONA, kugira abe Umushikiranganji wa mbere — Umugore wa mbere kuri uwo mwanya mu Burundi.

---

Cabinet yari igizwe n'abashikiranganji 15 b'Abahutu, 7 b'Abatutsi — Kinigi ayirongoye.

Yashize imbere: kusangira ubutegetsi, iterambere ry'ubutunzi, no kugabanya amacakubiri.
