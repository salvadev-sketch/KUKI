# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP04 — NDADAYE: IMINSI 112

Ndadaye yari afise umugambi w'imyaka itanu.

Ariko yagumye ku butegetsi **iminsi 103 gusa** (10 Mukakaro – 21 Gitugutu 1993).

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("List of presidents of Burundi") itanga ibarwa ryeruye: iminsi 103. Twahinduye "112" (iryari riri mu mutwe w'iyi episode) kuko nta soko ryaryemeza — 103 ni ryo bibarwa biva ku matariki yombi yemejwe n'amasoko yose (10 Mukakaro n'21 Gitugutu 1993).

---

Ntiyashatse kwubaka Leta y'abamushigikiye gusa.

Yashize hamwe abantu bo mu mashirahamwe atandukanye, ashaka ko FRODEBU idaba ari yo yiharira ubutegetsi bwose.

---

Muri Leta yiwe, hari abagabo n'abagore tuzomenya vyimbitse muri iyi Season: **Sylvie Kinigi** (Umushikiranganji wa mbere), **Cyprien Ntaryamira** (Uburimyi n'Ubworozi), **Sylvestre Ntibantunganya** (Imigenderanire n'Amahanga).

---

Ariko igisirikare — icategekwaga na **Jean Bikomagu**, uwo na we tuzomenya vyimbitse — ntabwo cari cahindutse kimwe n'ubutegetsi bwa politike.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Jean Bikomagu") ivuga ko "President Melchior Ndadaye appointed him as Army Chief of Staff in 1993" — bihuza neza n'ivyo EP04 ivuga.

Ndadaye yari afise umugambi wo guhindura inzego z'umutekano — gutandukanya igisirikare na gendarmerie, kwongereza uburinganire bw'abakomoka mu turere n'imigwi itandukanye.

Ntitwovuga ko yari amaze gushira ivyo vyose mu ngiro — yari agifise iminsi mike gusa.

---

Ibihe vy'ubutegetsi bwiwe vyaranzwe n'amakenga menshi mu bice bimwe vy'igisirikare.

Ariko Ndadaye yagumye yiyemeje ku nzira yiwe.
