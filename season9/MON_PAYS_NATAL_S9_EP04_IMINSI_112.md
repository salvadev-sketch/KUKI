# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP04 — NDADAYE: IMINSI 112

Ndadaye yari afise umugambi w'imyaka itanu.

Ariko yagumye ku butegetsi **iminsi 112 gusa.**

🟡 *(Ibarwa ry'imisi ritandukanya ku masoko: isoko rimwe (military-history fandom) ritanga hafi iminsi 103, igitabu c'umutwe w'iyi episode kikoresha 112. Twagumije "112" nk'uko umutwe w'episode uvuga, ariko turabimenyesha ko ryoshobora kugirira hafi iminsi 100-112, bivanye n'uburyo bubarwa (kuva ku matora canke kuva atangura gutegeka nyene).)*

Ntiyashatse kwubaka Leta y'abamushigikiye gusa.

Yashize hamwe abantu bo mu mashirahamwe atandukanye, ashaka ko FRODEBU idaba ari yo yiharira ubutegetsi bwose.

Muri Leta yiwe, hari abagabo n'abagore tuzomenya vyimbitse muri iyi Season: **Sylvie Kinigi** (Umushikiranganji wa mbere), **Cyprien Ntaryamira** (Uburimyi n'Ubworozi), **Sylvestre Ntibantunganya** (Imigenderanire n'Amahanga).

Ariko igisirikare — icategekwaga na **Jean Bikomagu**, uwo na we tuzomenya vyimbitse — ntabwo cari cahindutse kimwe n'ubutegetsi bwa politike.

Ntitwovuga ko yari amaze gushira ivyo vyose mu ngiro — yari agifise iminsi mike gusa.
