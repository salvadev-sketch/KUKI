# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP07 — GILLES BIMAZUBUTE: INTANGO

Muri aba bantu bane b'ingenzi twize (Ndadaye, Kinigi, Karibwami, na Bimazubute), Bimazubute ni we ufise inkuru ndende cane, ikwira imyaka irenga mirongo itatu.

---

Ku myaka y'ivuka ryiwe, hari amakuru abiri atavuga rumwe: amasoko amwe avuga **1934**, ku Ijenda; naho umwana wiwe, Adélard Bimazubute, avuga ko se yavutse **1937**, ku musozi wa Mugano, Matana, intara ya Bururi. Ntitwohitamwo kimwe muri byo.

---

Yize muri **Université Officielle du Congo et du Ruanda-Urundi** i Élisabethville (Lubumbashi ubu), kuva 1958, ariko arirukanwa muri 1961.

---

Mu **1959**, Bimazubute na Prime Niyongabo barashinze **UCJAB — Union Culturelle de la Jeunesse Africaine du Burundi.**

Mu **1961**, iryo shirahamwe ryahinduye izina, riba **JNR — Jeunesse Nationaliste Rwagasore** — izina ryafitaniye isano na Louis Rwagasore, uwo twize muri Season 5.

Ni ukuvuga: Bimazubute yari amaze kugira uruhara mu ntango za politike y'Uburundi bwikukira, imyaka irenga mirongo itatu imbere y'uko ashika ku wa 21 Gitugutu 1993.

---

Mu **1962**, aba directeur wa Radio Burundi.

Mu **1963**, aba Chef de cabinet mu Minisiteri y'Imigenderanire n'Amahanga, hanyuma Minisitiri w'agateganyo, mu kiringo c'amezi hafi atatu.

---

Mu **1964–66**, yize amategeko i Paris — ata kurangiza — akongera akandika mu kinyamakuru co mu Bubiligi, **Remarques Africaines**, ku bijanye n'Uburundi.

Muri ico gihe, yashigikiye iciyumviro c'uko Uburundi bukwiye kuba **Repubulika** — atari ubwami.

---

Mu **1991**, aba umwe mu bashinze **Ligue Iteka**, ishirahamwe ry'uburenganzira bwa muntu.

Aca yinjira muri **FRODEBU** — aba umwe mu Batutsi bake bari mu bayobozi bakuru b'uyo mugambwe.

---

Mu 1993, atorwa aba Umushingamateka, hanyuma **Icegera c'Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka**, inyuma ya Pontien Karibwami.
