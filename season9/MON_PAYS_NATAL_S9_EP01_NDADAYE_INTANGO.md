# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP01 — MELCHIOR NDADAYE: INTANGO

Yavutse mu **1953**, mu ntara ya Ngozi.

Amakuru arambuye ku bwana bwiwe n'umuryango wiwe ni make — iki ni ikibazo BBG ubwayo ivuga ko kigikeneye ubushakashatsi.

---

Yagiye kwiga muri Rwanda, aho yize amashure yisumbuye, hanyuma muri Côte d'Ivoire, aronka impamyabushobozi mu vy'ubutegetsi n'ubutunzi.

Ntitwovuga ngo turazi neza igituma yagiye kwiga hanze — iki ni ikindi kintu tuzogumana nk'ikibazo, atari ukuri kwuzuye.

---

Yagiye gukorera **banki**, ku murimo w'ubutunzi.

Ariko mu mpisho, kuva mu myaka ya 1980, yari afise uruhara mu bikorwa vya politike.

Mu **1986**, yari mu bantu bashinze **FRODEBU — Front pour la Démocratie au Burundi** — umugambwe wakoze mu kinyegero imyaka myinshi, mbere y'uko wemerwa ku mugaragaro mu **1992**.

---

Yari kumwe n'abandi bagabo benshi tuzomenya muri iyi Season: Pontien Karibwami, Cyprien Ntaryamira, Sylvestre Ntibantunganya, Gilles Bimazubute, Léonard Nyangoma, Jean-Marie Ngendahayo.

Bose, hamwe, bari mu bushake bumwe: guhindura Uburundi bukava ku ntwaro y'umugambwe umwe, bukaja ku mashirahamwe menshi ya politike.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP02 — URUGAMBA RWA POLITIKE**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
