# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP03 — AMATORA 1993 — NDADAYE ARATSINDA

Ku wa **1 Ruheshi 1993**, Melchior Ndadaye yaratsinze amatora y'Umukuru w'Igihugu.

Human Rights Watch itanga imibare: **Ndadaye hafi 65%, Buyoya hafi 33%.**

---

Uyu ni Umukuru w'Igihugu wa mbere w'Uburundi yatowe n'abanyagihugu biciye mu matora ya demokarasi — kandi Umuhutu wa mbere kuri uwo mwanya.

---

Ku wa **29 Ruheshi 1993**, habaye amatora y'Abashingamateka.

**FRODEBU yaronse imyanya 65 kuri 81.**

Pontien Karibwami, uwo tuzomenya vyimbitse, yaciye aba Umukuru w'Inama Nshingamateka, Gilles Bimazubute aba icegera ciwe.

---

Ariko imbere y'uko Ndadaye arahira, hari hamaze kuboneka ikimenyetso c'akaga.

Ku wa **2 Mukakaro 1993**, habaye ikigeragezo c'abasirikare co gufata ubutegetsi.

**Buyoya ubwiwe, akiri Perezida, ni we yagihagaritse.**

Ico kigeragezo cerekanye ko, naho amatora yari yaratsinzwe n'abenshi, ikibazo c'igisirikare kitari kirangiye.

---

Ku wa **10 Mukakaro 1993**, Melchior Ndadaye yararahijwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP04 — NDADAYE: IMINSI 112**, ni ho tuzoraba ingoma yiwe ngufi.
