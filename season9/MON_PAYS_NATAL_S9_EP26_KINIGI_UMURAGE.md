# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP26 — SYLVIE KINIGI: UMURAGE

Inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ndadaye, ikibazo gikomeye cari ukuronka umukuru w'igihugu mushasha — UPRONA yararwanya uburyo Itegeko Nshinga ryahinduwe kugira Ntaryamira atorwe.

Kinigi yakoranye n'uwuserukira ONU mu Burundi, Ahmedou Ould-Abdallah, mu kurondera umwumvikano: **FRODEBU ibona Perezida (Ntaryamira), UPRONA ibona Umushikiranganji wa mbere mushasha.**

---

Ku wa **5 Ruhuhuma 1994**, Ntaryamira ararahira. Kinigi ntiyagerageje kuguma ku butegetsi — yararetse ububasha, **Anatole Kanyenkiko** amusubirira.

---

Inyuma yo kuva muri politike, Kinigi yasubiye mu gisata yari asanzwe azi neza: **ubutunzi n'imari**, akorera muri Banque Centrale du Burundi, hanyuma muri Banque Commerciale.

Yaje gukora ku rwego mpuzamakungu, harimwo ku biro vy'intumwa idasanzwe ya ONU mu karere k'Ibiyaga Binini, i Nairobi.

Mu **2016**, Carter Center yamuhisemwo kuyobora mission y'abagenzura amatora muri Zambiya.

---

Ntitwovuga "Kinigi yarakijije Uburundi." Ivyo vyoba birengeje ivyo amasoko yemeza.

Ico dushobora kuvuga: **mu gihe igihugu cari mu kaga gakomeye, Kinigi yagize uruhara mu kuguma akomeza inzego za Leta, no mu kurondera umwumvikano wa politike watumye Cyprien Ntaryamira ashobora gufata ubutegetsi.**

---

Bimwe muri abo bagabo n'abagore bane — Ndadaye, Karibwami, Bimazubute, Kinigi — bararokotse, abandi barishwe uwo munsi nyene.

Ariko bose, hamwe na Bikomagu na Ntaryamira na Ntibantunganya, bagize uruhara mu gihe kimwe gikomeye cy'Uburundi — igihe demokarasi yatanguye, hanyuma igahura n'igikomere gikomeye cane.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP27 — ISOZERO — IHEREZO RYA PHASE 1**, ni ho tuzoheza urugendo rwose.
