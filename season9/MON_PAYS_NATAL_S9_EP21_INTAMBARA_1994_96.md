# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP21 — NTIBANTUNGANYA: INTAMBARA 1994–96

Ntibantunganya yatanguye ubutegetsi bwiwe igihugu kimaze gusenyuka mu vya politike.

---

Inyuma y'iyicwa rya Ndadaye, bamwe mu bantu bari muri FRODEBU n'abandi bari hafi yayo batanguje **CNDD — Conseil National pour la Défense de la Démocratie.**

Igisata caco c'igisirikare cari kizwi nka **FDD.**

---

Ntibantunganya ntiyari umusirikare. Yari umunyapolitike.

Ivyo vyatumye uruhara rwiwe mu gihe c'intambara ruba urwa politike, aho kuba urwa gisirikare — kandi ni co gituma tutamushira ku murongo umwe n'abakuru b'ingabo nka Bikomagu.

---

Human Rights Watch yerekana ko intambara yo mu Burundi yakomeje mu myaka ya 1994–1996, kandi ko **impande zitandukanye zose** — Leta, igisirikare, na CNDD/FDD — zagiye zigirizwa ibikorwa vy'ubwicanyi n'ibindi bikorwa bihonyanga uburenganzira bwa muntu.

Ntitwovuga ko uruhande rumwe gusa ari rwo rwakoze amakosa.

---

**Julius Nyerere**, uwahoze ari Perezida wa Tanzaniya, yaragize uruhara mu bikorwa vyo gufasha Uburundi kurondera inzira y'amahoro — ikibazo c'Uburundi cari kimaze kuba ikibazo c'akarere, atari ica Bujumbura gusa.

---

Ariko ibiganiro ntivyahagaritse intambara.

Ntibantunganya yayoboye igihugu mu gihe c'intambara ikomeye, ariko ntiyashoboye kurangiza iyo ntambara mu kiringo ciwe — atari kubera ubunyantege nke bwiwe, ariko kubera ko intambara yari ifise imvo nyinshi, abagize uruhara benshi, ata muntu umwe yari gushobora kuyihagarika wenyene.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP22 — 1996: COUP YA BUYOYA, NTIBANTUNGANYA AKURWA**, ni ho tuzobibona.
