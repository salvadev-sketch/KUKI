# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP09 — CYPRIEN NTARYAMIRA: INTANGO

Cyprien Ntaryamira ntiyatanguriye ku mwanya wa Perezida.

Yari **umuhinga mu burimyi (agronomie).**

---

Amakuru arambuye ku bwana bwiwe n'amashure ni make. Ico dushobora kuvuga ku bwizewe ni uko yari amaze imyaka yakorera iterambere ry'ivyaro n'uburimyi, imbere y'uko yinjira muri politike y'ingenzi.

---

Yari umwe mu **batangije Sahwanya-FRODEBU**, kandi yari afitaniye ubucuti bwa hafi na **Melchior Ndadaye.**

Inyuma y'amatora ya Ruheshi 1993, yahawe **Ubushikiranganji bw'Uburimyi n'Ubworozi.**

---

Ntiyashatse ko umurimo wiwe ugarukire kuri politike gusa.

Yashimikira ku burimyi, amatongo, n'imicungararo yayo — kubera ko igice kinini c'abanyagihugu b'Uburundi cari gishingiye ku buhinzi.

---

Muri Leta ya Ndadaye, yari umwe mu bagabo bakomeye.

Ariko urupfu rwa Ndadaye, ku wa 21 Gitugutu 1993, rwahindutse rushira igihugu mu kibazo gikomeye — kandi izina rya Ntaryamira ryatanguye kuba iry'ingenzi cane, mu buryo atari yiteze.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP10 — SYLVESTRE NTIBANTUNGANYA: URUGENDO RURERURE**, ni ho tuzomumenya.
