# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP14 — INYUMA Y'IYICWA — ICUBA C'UBUTEGETSI

Ndadaye yarishwe.

Pontien Karibwami, uwari ategekanijwe gusubirira Perezida hakurikijwe Itegeko Nshinga, na we yari yishwe.

---

Uburundi bwasigaye bufise ikibazo gikomeye: **ninde ategekanijwe gusubirira Perezida, mu gihe abantu bombi bari bategekanijwe kuri iyo mirimo bose bishwe?**

---

Raporo y'Umunyamabanga Mukuru wa ONU, yo mu mpera za Gitugutu 1993, ivuga ikintu gikomeye:

**"Neither the Government nor the army were exercising power and authority in the country."**

Ni ukuvuga: mu ntango, Leta n'igisirikare vyombi vyari vyarananiwe gukoresha ububasha bwabyo mu gihugu cose.

[FR-CITATION] 🟡 Nta soko ryavuze iyi nkuru nyene y'umutwe wa ONU, ariko iragenda neza n'ivyavuzwe na UN Security Council Resolution 1012 (1995) hamwe n'uruhushya rwa Commission internationale d'enquête (reba EP08).

---

Ku wa **28 Gitugutu 1993**, Colonel Jean Bikomagu yatangaje ko igikorwa cari kirangiye, abasirikare basubira mu bigo vyabo.

Ariko gutsindwa k'ico gikorwa ntivyazanye amahoro.

Ubwicanyi bwakomeje, abantu ibihumbi mirongo baricwa, abandi barahunga.

---

**Sylvie Kinigi**, Umushikiranganji wa mbere yari yararokotse, ni we yasigaye ayoboye ibikorwa vya Leta, mu buryo bw'agateganyo, mu gihe hariko hararonderwa inyishu yemewe n'amategeko.

Ntitwovuga ko yabaye "Perezida yatowe" — ntibyari vyo.

Yabaye umuntu mukuru mu buyobozi bw'igihugu, mu gihe ikibazo c'uwosubirira Perezida cariko kiratorerwa inyishu.
