# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP17 — NTARYAMIRA: LETA Y'UBUMWE

Ntaryamira yamaze ku butegetsi hafi amezi abiri gusa.

Ariko muri ico gihe kigufi, yagerageje gushira imbere impinduka ebyiri: **kwiyunga hagati y'imigwi ya politike, no gutera imbere uburimyi n'iterambere ry'ivyaro.**

---

Nk'umuntu yari yarize agronomie, ntiyashaka ko ijambo ry'ubutegetsi bwiwe ribe rijanye na politike gusa.

Inyandiko yashikirijwe ONU imwerekana nk'umuntu yari afise ubumenyi n'uburambe vyari bimushoboza gukorera ku bibazo vy'amatongo n'imicungararo yayo.

---

Ariko igihugu yari yaronse kuyobora ntabwo cari mu bihe bisanzwe: cari kimaze gutakaza Ndadaye, cari kimaze kubona ubwicanyi bukomeye, kandi ubwoba n'ukutizerana vyaguma vyihishe hagati y'imigwi ya politike.

---

Ku wa **6 Ndamukiza 1994**, imisi 60 gusa inyuma yo kurahira, Ntaryamira yagiye i **Dar es Salaam**, mu nama y'abakuru b'ibihugu.

Yari asubira i Bujumbura, ari mu ndege ya Perezida w'u Rwanda, **Juvénal Habyarimana.**

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP18 — 6 NDAMUKIZA 1994 — URUPFU RWA NTARYAMIRA**, ni ho tuzobibona.
