# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP18 — 6 NDAMUKIZA 1994 — URUPFU RWA NTARYAMIRA

Ku mugoroba wo ku wa 6 Ndamukiza 1994, indege yari itwaye Perezida Cyprien Ntaryamira na Perezida w'u Rwanda, Juvénal Habyarimana, yarashwe hafi y'ikibuga c'indege ca Kigali, iraguruka.

---

Bombi barapfuye.

Abantu bose bari muri iyo ndege barapfuye — harimwo n'abandi Barundi babiri: **Bernard Ciza**, Umushikiranganji w'Uburongozi bw'Iterambere n'Ubwubatsi, na **Cyriaque Simbizi**, Umushikiranganji w'Amakuru.

---

ONU ivuga ko indege yarashwe n'ibisasu vyaturutse hasi, ariko ko abatwaye ico gikorwa batigeze bamenyekana neza muri raporo, kandi ko iperereza ryuzuye ku gituma indege yahanutse ritashobotse.

**Ntitwovuga: "RPF ni yo yarashe indege." Ntitwovuge: "Abasirikare b'u Rwanda ni bo barashe indege." Ntitwovuge: "Ntaryamira yishwe n'uruhande kanaka."**

Igituma indege yarashwe n'abarashe iyo ndege ni ikibazo kiguma gifise impaka n'amaperereza atandukanye, kugeza none.

---

Iri joro ryahinduye amateka y'akarere kose.

Urupfu rwa Habyarimana n'urupfu rwa Ntaryamira, ku wa 6 Ndamukiza 1994, ni ryo joro ry'intango y'ibikorwa vy'ubwicanyi n'intambara mu Rwanda — bikaza kuzwi nka **jenoside y'Abatutsi mu Rwanda.**

Ku Burundi ubwabwo, urupfu rwa Ntaryamira rwaciye rusiga igihugu kidafise Perezida — inshuro ya kabiri mu myaka itarenga umwaka.

---

Uburundi ntibwari bugishoboye kubandanya nk'uko vyari bisanzwe.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP19 — NTIBANTUNGANYA: ABA PEREZIDA**, ni ho tuzoraba icakurikiye.
