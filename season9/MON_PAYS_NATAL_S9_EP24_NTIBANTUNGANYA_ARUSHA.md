# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP24 — NTIBANTUNGANYA: ARUSHA N'UMUSENATEUR

Inyuma y'igikorwa co gutembagaza ubutegetsi co 1996, Ntibantunganya ntiyazimiye mu mateka y'Uburundi.

---

Mu **1997**, ONU yavuze ko Ntibantunganya, hamwe n'uwahoze ari Perezida Jean-Baptiste Bagaza (uwo twize muri Season 7), baremerewe kugenda mu bwisanzure no kugira uruhara mu biganiro.

---

Ikintu gihambaye cane ku murage wiwe: yari ahari mu gihe c'**Amasezerano y'Amahoro n'Uwiyunge ya Arusha yo mu 2000**, uwo twize muri Season 8 EP13-14.

[FR-CITATION] 🟢 Wikipedia ("Arusha Accords (Burundi)") ivuga ko "The Arusha Peace and Reconciliation Agreement... was a transitional peace treaty signed on 28 August 2000 in Arusha, Tanzania."

Inyandiko yemewe y'amasezerano imushira ku rutonde rw'abahoze ari ba Perezida b'Uburundi bari bitavye uwo muhango.

---

Inyuma y'Arusha, Ntibantunganya yabaye **Umusenateri w'ubuzima bwose**, nk'uwahoze ari Umukuru w'igihugu, gushika muri **Myandagaro 2018**.

---

Ubwiwe, Ntibantunganya yavuze ko icamubabaje cane ari uko atashoboye gutuma Abarundi bagira amahoro n'umutekano mu gihe yari Umukuru w'igihugu.

Ariko yavuze ko yishimira ibintu bibiri: ko igihugu catakaza abakuru b'igihugu babiri (Ndadaye na Ntaryamira) mu gihe gito, ariko ntigicire mu kindi kago gikomeye kurushiriza; no kuba yarashize imbere ibiganiro vyagutse vyo kurondera umuti w'intambara.

---

Ntitwovuga ko yari intwari ata makosa afise. Ntitwovuga na ngo yari umutegetsi yananiwe muri vyose.

Yari umuntu wabayeho mu bihe bitatu bikomeye: **kurondera demokarasi, gucungera igihugu mu ntambara, kurondera amahoro.**

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP25 — KARIBWAMI NA BIMAZUBUTE: UMURAGE**, ni ho tuzoraba.
