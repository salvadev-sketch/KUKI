# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 9 — EP02 — URUGAMBA RWA POLITIKE

FRODEBU yakoze imyaka myinshi mu kinyegero.

Uburundi, kuva mu 1966, bwari bumaze imyaka irenga mirongo ibiri bufise umugambwe umwe — UPRONA — hamwe n'ubutegetsi bushingiye cane ku gisirikare, nk'uko twize muri Season 6, 7, na 8.

---

Mu ntango za 1990, ivyifuzo vy'amahinduka vyariyongera — ku rwego rw'igihugu, ariko no ku rwego mpuzamakungu.

Mu **1992**, inyuma y'ivyabaye Ntega na Marangara mu 1988 (twize muri Season 8), Constitution nshasha yaremye ubushobozi bw'amashirahamwe menshi ya politike.

FRODEBU yaciye ivayo mu kinyegero, ija ku mugaragaro.

---

Ndadaye yari umwe mu bayobozi bayo bakomeye.

Umugambwe wari uhagarariye cane Abahutu, naho utari uw'ubwoko bumwe gusa — hari n'Abatutsi bamwe muri FRODEBU.

---

Ku wa **1 Ruheshi 1993**, habaye amatora ya mbere y'Umukuru w'igihugu, ashingiye kuri multipartisme, kuva Uburundi bwaronka ubwigenge.
