# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP15 — INZIBACYUHO NA DOMITIEN NDAYIZEYE

Arusha yari yarashizeho iciyumviro c'inzibacyuho y'imyaka itatu, igizwe n'ibice bibiri vy'amezi 18.

Ikibazo cari: **ninde azoyobora igice ca mbere?**

---

Ku wa 23 Mukakaro 2001, Nelson Mandela — uwari amaze kuba umuhuza w'ibiganiro inyuma y'urupfu rwa Julius Nyerere — yashikirije umugambi:

**Pierre Buyoya** aba Perezida mu gice ca mbere.

**Domitien Ndayizeye**, umunyamabanga mukuru wa FRODEBU, akaba icegera ciwe.

Inyuma y'amezi 18, Ndayizeye akamusubirira.

---

Ku wa **1 Munyonyo 2001**, inzibacyuho yaratanguye ku mugaragaro.

Iki cari ikintu kidasanzwe: Buyoya yari yarafashe ubutegetsi mu 1996 biciye muri coup — ariko ubu yari asubiye kuba Perezida mu rwego rw'inzibacyuho rwumvikanyweko muri Arusha, atari mu buryo bw'igitugu.

---

Guverinoma ntiyari iya Buyoya wenyene: mu bashikiranganji 26, 15 bari abaserukira imigambwe yiganjemwo Abahutu, 11 bari Abatutsi.

Ariko intambara ntiyari yarahagaze. CNDD-FDD na FNL bakomeje kurwana, naho inzibacyuho yari itanguye.

---

Mu **2002**, ibiganiro hagati ya gouvernement na CNDD-FDD vyaratera imbere. Ku wa 2 Kigarama 2002, Buyoya yasinyanye amasezerano y'agahengwe na CNDD-FDD — uwasinye ku ruhande rwabo akaba **Pierre Nkurunziza**.

Ariko amasezerano ntiyaciye ahagarika intambara ubwo nyene.

---

Amaze kurangiza igihe ciwe c'amezi 18, **Buyoya yarubahirije icemezo.**

Ku mpera za Ndamukiza 2003, Domitien Ndayizeye yararahiriye kuba Perezida w'inzibacyuho, asubirira Buyoya.

Umunyamabanga Mukuru wa ONU yaramushimiye ku bwo kwubahiriza ivyo yari yiyemeje.

**Ntitwovuga ngo "Buyoya yarangije intambara."** Intambara yari ikibandanya, no mu 2003.

---

Aha ni ho ubutegetsi bwa kabiri bwa Pierre Buyoya burangirira — imyaka umunani inyuma y'uko yagarutse ku butegetsi biciye muri coup.
