# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP10 — 1993–1996: INTAMBARA IRATANGURA, BUYOYA ARI HANZE

Ku wa **21 Gitugutu 1993**, amezi atatu gusa inyuma yo kurahira, Perezida Melchior Ndadaye yarishwe, mu gikorwa co guhirika ubutegetsi cakozwe n'igice c'abasirikare.

Iyi nkuru y'ubwiwe — urupfu rwiwe, iperereza, n'abandi bategetsi barishwe (Pontien Karibwami, Gilles Bimazubute, n'abandi) — tuzoyiraba vyimbitse muri Season ikurikira, yihariwe.

---

Ku ruhande rwa Buyoya: ni ngombwa gushimikira ku kintu kimwe gikomeye.

**Buyoya yari amaze kuva ku butegetsi ku wa 10 Mukakaro 1993** — ni ukuvuga imisi mike inyuma y'itorwa rya Ndadaye. Igihe coup yabaye, Buyoya ntiyari Perezida.

---

Hari abamwagiriza kuba yari afise ubumenyi canke uruhara mu mugambi wo guhirika Ndadaye — kubera ko yari uwahoze ari Perezida, umusirikare mukuru, kandi afise ijambo mu basirikare n'Abatutsi bamwe.

Ariko **nta gihamya ikomeye dufise yo kuvuga ko Buyoya ari we yategetse urupfu rwa Ndadaye canke ko ari we yari inyuma y'iyo coup.**

---

Inyuma y'urupfu rwa Ndadaye, **Cyprien Ntaryamira** yaragiye ku butegetsi, ararahira ku wa 5 Ruhuhuma 1994.

Ariko ku wa **6 Ndamukiza 1994**, Ntaryamira yapfiriye hamwe na Perezida w'u Rwanda, Juvénal Habyarimana, igihe indege yabo yaraswa hafi ya Kigali — ikintu cazoba intango ya jenoside y'u Rwanda.

**Sylvestre Ntibantunganya** yabaye Perezida akurikira.

---

Muri ico gihe cose, Uburundi bwinjiye mu ntambara: armée, imirwi yitwaje ibirwanisho, abaturage, ubwicanyi, impunzi, gusubira kwihora.

Buyoya yari hanze y'ubutegetsi, ariko yagumye ari umuntu akomeye mu basirikare no muri politike.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP11 — COUP YA 1996 — BUYOYA ASUBIRA KU BUTEGETSI**, ni ho tuzobibona.
