# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP01 — PIERRE BUYOYA: INTANGO N'AMASHURE

Yavukiye ku wa **24 Munyonyo 1949**, mu ntara ya Bururi.

Amasoko atandukanya ku bibanza nyabwo: hari avuga **Rutovu**, andi **Mutangaro**. Ntitwohitamwo kimwe nk'ukuri kudasubirwako.

---

Ikintu kimwe gikomeye: nka Bagaza (twize muri Season 7) na Micombero (uwatwaye Uburundi imbere yiwe), Buyoya na we avugwa cane cane nk'uwavukiye i **Rutovu** — nk'uko amasoko menshi abivuga. Ni ukuvuga: **abaperezida batatu bakurikiranye b'Uburundi (Micombero, Bagaza, Buyoya) bose bavugwa nk'abakomotse mu gisagara kimwe nyene**, mu ntara ya Bururi — ikintu abahinga b'amateka ya politike bakunda kuvuga nk'igitangaza kidasanzwe.

---

Yakuriye i Rutovu, yize amashure ya mbere no ayisumbuye ku kigo c'Abakatolika, aho nyene.

Hari amasoko amwerekana nk'umuntu wo mu muryango usanzwe — ariko amakuru arambuye ku babyeyi biwe ni make.

---

Mu **1967**, yagiye mu Bubiligi, aja mu **École Royale des Cadets**, hanyuma **École Royale Militaire de Bruxelles**.

Mu 1975, asubira mu Burundi, afise diplôme mu Sciences sociales et militaires.

---

Ariko amashure yiwe ntiyahagarariye i Buraya.

Mu **1976–77**, mu Bufaransa (École d'État-Major i Saumur).

Mu **1980–82**, mu Budagi bw'Iburengero (Bundeswehr Command and Staff College).

---

Muri ico gihe, azamuka mu gisirikare c'Uburundi: umuyobozi w'ibice (1975), commandant d'escadron i Gitega (1977), hanyuma commandant de bataillon, kugeza aba, mu **1984**, umuyobozi w'ibiro bijejwe instruction et opérations ku Général Staff y'ingabo.

---

Ntiyari umusirikare gusa. Yari yariyandikishije muri **UPRONA**, aba umunywanyi wa Comité Central.

Hari amasoko avuga ko yari umuntu wari hafi y'ubutegetsi bwa Jean-Baptiste Bagaza — uwo twize muri Season 7 — mbere y'uko atandukana na bwo. Ariko igituma nyaco yatanguye kutumvikana na Bagaza ntibirasobanutse neza.
