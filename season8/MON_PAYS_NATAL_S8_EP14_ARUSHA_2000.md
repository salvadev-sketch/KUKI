# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP14 — AMASEZERANO YA ARUSHA — 2000

Mu **Myandagaro 2000**, habaye intambwe ikomeye cane.

Abaserukira imigambwe mikuru y'Abahutu (G-7) n'Abatutsi (G-10), hamwe na gouvernement na Parlement, barasinye **Arusha Peace and Reconciliation Agreement**.

---

**[CITATION — 🟢]**

Congressional Research Service (RS20910) yemeza itariki nyayo (28 Myandagaro 2000) amasezerano yasinywe, hamwe n'uko FDD na FNL bitasinye icaro cose.

---

---

Amasezerano yarimwo ivyerekeye: gusangira ubutegetsi, ivugururwa ry'igisirikare, ubutungane, inzego za Leta, amatora, n'ukugarukana amahoro.

---

Ariko iki ni ikintu tudakwiye kwibagira: naho Arusha yasinywe mu 2000, **FDD na FNL ntizari zasinye amasezerano yose.**

Ni co gituma imirwano yabandanije inyuma ya 2000.

Ntituvuge ngo "Arusha yarangije intambara mu 2000." Tuvuge: **Arusha yashizeho umushinge ukomeye w'amahoro n'ugusangira ubutegetsi, ariko intambara ntiyahagaze ubwo nyene.**

---

Sanctions zari zaratanguye koroherezwa kuva mu 1997–99, kubera izo mpinduka za politike.

Ubutegetsi bwa kabiri bwa Buyoya, bwari bwaratanguye nk'ubutegetsi bwa gisirikare bwamaganwe n'akarere, yari yaragiye buhorobuhoro mu biganiro vya politike.

---

Ariko amasezerano ya Arusha yari yarateganije ikintu kimwe gikomeye: **inzibacyuho.**

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP15 — INZIBACYUHO NA DOMITIEN NDAYIZEYE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
