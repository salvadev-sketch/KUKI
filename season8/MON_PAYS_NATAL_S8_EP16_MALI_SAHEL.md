# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP16 — INYUMA Y'UBUTEGETSI — MALI NA SAHEL

Inyuma yo kuva ku butegetsi mu 2003, Buyoya ntiyavuye muri politike yo ku rwego rwa Afrika.

Yatanguye kugira uruhara mu bikorwa vyo gukemura amatati no guteza imbere amahoro muri Afrika yose.

---

Kuva mu **2009**, yari umwe mu bagize **AU High-Level Implementation Panel** ku bibazo vya Sudani na Sudani y'Epfo — imipaka, umutekano, ubutunzi, n'ugushira mu ngiro amasezerano y'amahoro.

Mu **2008**, yagizwe umuserukira wa African Union mu bikorwa vyo gushigikira amahoro muri Tchad.

---

Ku wa **25 Gitugutu 2012**, African Union yamugize **Haut Représentant wa AU muri Mali n'akarere ka Sahel** — icogihe Mali yari iri mu kibazo gikomeye ca politike n'umutekano, harimwo imirwi yitwaje ibirwanisho mu buraruko bw'igihugu.

---

Ku wa **30 Nzero 2013**, aba **Special Representative** wa AU akaba na **Head of AFISMA** — African-led International Support Mission in Mali.

Inshingano ziwe zari zijanye no gusubiza ubusugire bwa Leta ya Mali, kugarukana ubumwe bw'igihugu, no gutegura inzira y'amatora.

---

Yagumye kuri uwo mwanya imyaka hafi umunani, gushika mu **Munyonyo 2020**.

Le Monde yamudondoye nk'umuntu wagiye hagati ya **"coups d'État et compromis"** — hagati y'ugufata ubutegetsi n'ugushaka ubwumvikane bwa politike.
