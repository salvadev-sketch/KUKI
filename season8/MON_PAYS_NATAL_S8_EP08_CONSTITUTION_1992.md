# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP08 — RÉCONCILIATION, CONSTITUTION NSHASHA NA MULTIPARTISME

Inyuma ya Ntega na Marangara mu 1988, ubutegetsi bwa Buyoya bwatanguye inzira ndende yo guhindura uburyo igihugu catwarwamwo.

---

Hashinzwe inzego n'imigambi yo kwiga ikibazo c'amacakubiri hagati y'Abahutu n'Abatutsi, no kurondera uburyo bwo gusubiza hamwe igihugu.

Iyo nzira yaje gushikana ku **Constitution nshasha yo mu 1992**.

---

Iyo Constitution yarahinduye cane uburyo igihugu cari gisanzwe gitwarwamwo: Uburundi buva ku buryo bw'ubutegetsi bw'igisirikare, habaho uburyo bushasha bw'inzego za Leta, hashimikwa cane kuri **multipartisme**.

Ni intambwe ikomeye — kuko UPRONA yari imaze imyaka myinshi iri ku mutima wa système ya politike.

---

Mu ntango z'imyaka ya 1990, imigambwe mishasha yaratanguye gukora, harimwo **FRODEBU — Front pour la démocratie au Burundi**, ifise ubuyobozi buhagarariye cane Abahutu.

Ntitwovuga ko imigambwe yose yari ishingiye ku bwoko gusa. Politike y'ico gihe yari igizwe n'ibintu vy'amoko, uturere, amateka y'imigambwe, n'inyungu za politike, vyose bifatanye.

---

Ikintu gikomeye cane: ubutegetsi bwa Buyoya bwemeye gutegura amatora y'amashirahamwe menshi ya politike — ibintu bishasha cane, kuko kuva mu 1966 igihugu cari kimaze igihe kinini munsi y'ubutegetsi bw'igisirikare n'umugambwe umwe.
