# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP05 — NTEGA NA MARANGARA — INYISHU Y'IGISIRIKARE

Raporo ya Leta zunze Ubumwe za Amerika ivuga ko, mu gihe c'ukugarukana umutekano, abasirikare bakoze **reprisal killings** — ni ukuvuga kwica abantu basanzwe batari bafise ibirwanisho, mu kwihora.

Leta y'Uburundi ubwayo yemeye ko hari abantu ata co bari bafise bakoze bishwe mu bikorwa vy'igisirikare, naho yahakana ko habaye ukwihora ku mugaragaro.

Andi masoko y'ico gihe, harimwo The Washington Post, avuga ubwicanyi bukomeye bwakorewe Abahutu n'igisirikare.

---

Umubare w'abapfuye ni ikibazo tudashobora gukemura ata guhitamwo:

**Leta y'Uburundi** yavuze ko abantu bagera ku **5.000** ari bo bishwe.

**André Guichaoua**, umuhinga, avuga ko umubare ushobora gushika ku **20.000**, ukurikije izindi soko.

Ntitwovuga 20.000 nk'igitigiri cemejwe. Tuvuga: 5.000 ni igiharuro cemejwe n'abategetsi b'ico gihe, hafi 20.000 ni estimation y'izindi soko.

---

Ku wa **25 Myandagaro 1988**, Buyoya yavuze:

**"Crimes were committed and committed in broad daylight."**

Yavuze ko amategeko atavangura hagati y'imiryango, uturere, canke ubwoko, kandi yasezeranye ko abari inyuma y'ubwicanyi bazohanwa.

---

Ubwo bwicanyi ntibwaguma ku bihugu vyombi bireba. Bwahindutse ikibazo mpuzamakungu.
