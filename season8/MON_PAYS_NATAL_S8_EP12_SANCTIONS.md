# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP12 — SANCTIONS N'IKIBAZO C'AMAKAMBI YA REGROUPEMENT

Inyuma ya coup, ibihugu vyo muri akarere vyafashe ingingo yo guhagarika canke kugabanya imigenderanire y'ubutunzi n'Uburundi.

Intumbero yari ugutuma Buyoya asubiza igihugu ku nzira y'amategeko no gutangura ibiganiro n'abamurwanya.

Izo sanctions zaragize ingaruka ku bukungu bw'Uburundi, harimwo ibura ry'ibintu bimwe na bimwe n'ingorane ku bantu basanzwe.

---

Muri **1996–97**, gouvernement ya Buyoya yarashize abantu benshi mu makambi ya **regroupement**.

Gouvernement yavuze ko ayo makambi yari agamije kurinda abasivile, kubatandukanya n'abarwanyi, no kugenzura umutekano mu turere twari turimwo intambara.

Ariko **Human Rights Watch** yarayamaganye, ivuga ko abantu amajana y'ibihumbi bashizwe muri ayo makambi ku gahato, kandi ko ivyo vyagize ingaruka zikomeye ku basivile.

---

Ntidukwiye kuvuga ko umuntu wese yashizwe muri regroupement yabaye victime y'ubwicanyi — ivyo bisaba evidence yihariye. Ariko ntidukwiye kandi guhisha uburemere bw'ibirego vy'ihonyangwa ry'uburenganzira bwa muntu.

---

**[CITATION — 🟢]**

Human Rights Watch (*World Report* 1998 na 1999), Amnesty International (1997, 2000), na UNHCR (archive) byemeza ko amakambi ya regroupement yatanguye **mbere ya coup** — muri Ruhuhuma 1996, na guverineri w'intara ya Karuzi — Buyoya amaze gufata ubutegetsi (Nyakanga 1996) yarayaguje mu zindi ntara (Kayanza, Muramvya, Bubanza, Cibitoke, Bururi, Bujumbura-Rural). Ku bujuru bw'igitigiri: Leta ubwayo yavuze abarenga 300.000 mu makambi muri Nyakanga 1997; ONU (UN OCHA) yavuze 570.000 (nka 10% by'abanyagihugu bose), muri bo 220.000 mu makambi ya regroupement ku giti caco.
