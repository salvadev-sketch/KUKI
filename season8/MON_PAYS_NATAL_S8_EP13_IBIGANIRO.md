# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP13 — IBIGANIRO N'INTANGO YA ARUSHA

Mu 1996–97, habayeho kugerageza ibiganiro hagati ya gouvernement n'imirwi yitwaje ibirwanisho, harimwo CNDD.

Ariko ibiganiro vya mbere ntivyagenze neza — mu Myandagaro 1997, ubutegetsi bwa Buyoya bwasubiye inyuma ku biganiro vyari bitegekanijwe i Arusha.

---

Ariko mu mpera za 1997, ibintu vyatanguye guhinduka.

Mu Kigarama 1997, umubano hagati ya gouvernement na Assemblée Nationale waratanguye gutera imbere. Buyoya yemeye ko FRODEBU ikorera kongere yayo i Bujumbura.

---

Mu **1998**, Buyoya na opposition yiganjemwo FRODEBU barashitse ku masezerano ya politike: **Transitional Constitutional Act**, uruhara rushasha rwa Parlement, na FRODEBU yinjira muri gouvernement.

Iyi ni intambwe ikomeye cane: umugambwe wari waratsinze amatora yo mu 1993 ubu wari uri muri gouvernement ya Buyoya.

---

Mu **Ruheshi 1998**, ibiganiro vy'amahoro vya Burundi vyatanguriye i **Arusha, Tanzaniya**.

Umuhuza mukuru yari **Julius Nyerere**, uwahoze ari Perezida wa Tanzaniya.

Ariko Arusha ntiyaciye ikemura intambara ubwo nyene: uwuzoyobora inzibacyuho, uko igisirikare kizohindurwa, uko Abahutu n'Abatutsi bosangira ubutegetsi — vyose vyari ibibazo bikomeye. FDD na FNL ntibinjiye mu masezerano mu ntango, kandi intambara yarabandanije.

---

Mu **Gitugutu 1999**, ku wa 14, Julius Nyerere yarapfuye. **Nelson Mandela** yaramusubiriye nk'umuhuza w'ibiganiro, ku wa 1 Kigarama 1999.
