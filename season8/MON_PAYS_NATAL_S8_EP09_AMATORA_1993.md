# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP09 — AMATORA YA 1993 — BUYOYA YEMERA GUTSINDWA

Ku wa **1 Ruheshi 1993**, habaye amatora ya mbere y'umukuru w'igihugu ashingiye kuri multipartisme, kuva Uburundi bwaronka ubwigenge.

Buyoya, wari Perezida, yarahatanye nk'umukandida wa **UPRONA**. **Melchior Ndadaye** wa **FRODEBU** yaramutsinze. Ingene amatora yagenze n'imibare yayo, tuzobironka muri Season 9, EP03.

---

Iki ni ikintu gihambaye cane mu mateka yiwe: umusirikare yafashe ubutegetsi biciye ku gikorwa co gutembagaza ubutegetsi, mu 1987, ni we yaje gutanga ubutegetsi ku wari amaze gutsinda amatora menshi, mu 1993.

Inyuma y'amatora, Buyoya yarakiriye ivyavuye muri yo.

---

Ku wa **10 Mukakaro 1993**, Ndadaye yararahiye nka Perezida — Perezida wa mbere w'Umuhutu yatowe n'abanyagihugu biciye mu matora.

Buyoya aca ava ku butegetsi mu buryo bwemewe n'amategeko.

---

Muri ico gusuzuma Buyoya, dukwiye kwirinda ibintu bibiri bikabije: ntituvuge ngo ni we wenyene yazanye démocratie mu Burundi; ntituvuge kandi ngo multipartisme yari iy'inyuma gusa, ata mpinduka nyazo.

Ico dushobora kuvuga: **Buyoya yayoboye inzira y'ivugururwa yashikanye ku Constitution nshasha, multipartisme, n'amatora ya 1993 — inzira yari yaratewe n'ibintu vyinshi, harimwo igitutu c'abanyagihugu n'ica communauté internationale.**

---

Ariko intsinzi ya Ndadaye ntiyari iherezo ry'ibibazo vya politike vy'Uburundi.

Amatati y'amoko, ubwoba mu gisirikare, n'ukutizerana hagati y'imirwi ya politike vyaguma vyihishe.
