# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP07 — GUVERINOMA NSHASHA — ADRIEN SIBOMANA

Ntega na Marangara vyahinduye politike ya Buyoya.

Mu ntango, gouvernement yari yanse ko haba enquête internationale, ivuga ko ari ikibazo c'ubwigenge bw'Uburundi. Ariko mu Gitugutu 1988, Buyoya yemeye ko mission ya ONU iza mu Burundi gusuzuma ikibazo c'amatati y'amoko n'impunzi.

---

Ku wa **4 Gitugutu 1988**, hashinzwe **Commission nationale consultative chargée d'examiner le problème de l'unité nationale** — Commission y'igihugu yategerezwa gushakashaka uburyo bwo gutorera umuti ikibazo c'amacakubiri no kubaka ubumwe bw'igihugu.

---

Ku wa **19 Gitugutu 1988**, Buyoya yarahinduye gouvernement.

**Adrien Sibomana**, Umuhutu, yagizwe **Premier Ministre.**

Mu cabinet nshasha, Abahutu baronse uruhara runini kuruta muri gouvernement ya mbere — raporo ivuga ko Buyoya yahaye Abahutu 12 kuri 24 by'imyanya, harimwo uwa Premier Ministre.

---

Aha ni ho dutangura kubona Buyoya nka Perezida agenda mu nzira yo guhindura système ya politike, atari umusirikare wafashe ubutegetsi gusa.

Ntega na Marangara vyamweretse ko igisirikare conyene kidashobora gutorera Uburundi umuti w'amacakubiri.

---

Ariko ntitwokwihutira kuvuga ko ibintu vyose vyahindutse ubwo nyene.

Amatati yari akiriho, kandi inzira iri imbere yari ndende cane: réconciliation, dialogue, Itegeko Nshinga rishasha, multipartisme, amatora.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP08 — RÉCONCILIATION NATIONALE (1988–89)**, ni ho tuzobandanya.
