# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP06 — IMPUNZI N'INGARUKA

Inyuma y'ubwicanyi bwo muri Ntega na Marangara, abantu benshi — cane cane Abahutu bo mu turere twari twashikiwe — barahungiye mu **Rwanda**.

Amakuru yo muri ico gihe avuga ko Abarundi benshi bahungiye mu Rwanda.

---

Ntidukwiye gufata ico gitigiri nk'icerekana abahunze Ntega na Marangara gusa mu buryo budashidikanywako — soko z'ico gihe rimwe na rimwe zakoresheje ibiharuro vy'abahunze igihugu cose.

---

Icakurikiye ntabwo cari ukubashikana mu Rwanda gusa.

Inyuma y'aho, benshi mu bahunze baratanguye gusubira mu Burundi bavuye mu Rwanda.

Abasubiye ntibagarukiye ku mupaka gusa — habaye ubufasha bwo gusubira mu mihana, kwubaka amazu, no gusubira mu buhinzi.

---

Ikibazo c'impunzi ntabwo cari ic'ubwicanyi bwonyene.

Cari kandi ic'uburundu: umuntu yarahunze, asiga urugo, asiga umurima, agenda mu gihugu c'akavamahanga, hanyuma agasubira agifise igikomere kitoroshye guhagarikwa.

---

Ariko ikibazo gikomeye cane kirasigaye: **Uburundi bushobora gute kwirinda ko ibi bisubira kubaho?**
