# MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP04 — NTEGA NA MARANGARA — UBWICANYI

Mu ijoro ryo ku wa **14 rija ku wa 15 Myandagaro 1988**, hatanguye ubwicanyi bukomeye muri **Ntega**, mu ntara ya Kirundo, na **Marangara**, mu ntara ya Ngozi.

---

Ubwicanyi bwatanguye hagati y'Abahutu n'Abatutsi, kandi abantu benshi barishwe.

Ntitwovuga ngo inkuru yatanguye mu buryo bumwe bwonyene — amasoko y'ico gihe ntahurira ku vyatumye ubwicanyi butangura.

Raporo ya Leta zunze Ubumwe za Amerika, yo mu 1988, ivuga ko mu ntango hari Abahutu bateye bakica Abatutsi benshi — ariko ko ata bimenyetso bikomeye vyerekanye ko ubwo bwicanyi bwari bwateguwe na politique.

---

Inyuma y'ubwicanyi bwo mu ntango, igisirikare c'Uburundi carinjiye muri ako karere, kivuga ko kigiye kugarukana umutekano.

Ariko igisirikare nticagarukiye gusa ku kurwanya abari bakoze ibitero.

**MON PAYS NATAL — SEASON 8 — EP05 — NTEGA NA MARANGARA — INYISHU Y'IGISIRIKARE**, ni ho tuzobibona.
